## Biography
Suraax Smith is a Dragonborn paladin whose life has been shaped by equal parts determination, misunderstanding, and an unwavering belief in destiny. She was born in the frontier town of [[Phandalin\|Phandalin]] to Karaax Smith and a father she never truly knew. Though her parents separated early in her life, Suraax grew up in a loving household with Karaax and Karaax’s second spouse, Chicxulub Smith. Having been raised by the pair since childhood, Suraax has always assumed Chicxulub to be her biological parent — an assumption no one has yet found reason, or perhaps courage, to correct.

Her path toward heroism began with a childhood misadventure. While wandering the forests beyond Phandalin, a young Suraax was attacked by a wild boar. She fled deeper into the woods until the creature lost her trail. There, half-buried in a weathered stump, she discovered an abandoned axe. After a determined struggle to free it from the wood, she turned to face the pursuing beast and slew it. To Suraax, this moment felt less like chance and more like fate. In her mind the axe had chosen her, marking her as its destined wielder and the beginning of her heroic journey. Though she did not realise it at the time, that moment of courage and conviction forged the first spark of the oath that would later guide her life.

From that day onward Suraax became convinced that the axe was magical — a relic of heroic power that had selected her as its champion. She does not consider herself the source of her abilities, believing instead that any remarkable feats she performs are the result of the axe’s hidden magic responding to her bravery. As she grew older, she eagerly joined travelling adventurers who passed through Phandalin, helping them hunt dangerous creatures in the surrounding wilderness. With every victory her confidence in the axe’s power grew, and with it the divine magic she unknowingly channels through sheer conviction.

One of her most formative battles came years later, when she joined a band of adventurers tracking a mysterious predator responsible for slaughtering livestock near the town. Their pursuit led them back into the same forest where Suraax had first claimed her axe. There the creature revealed itself — a werewolf, cunning enough to use the sound of Suraax’s half plate armour to locate and ambush the group. The fight quickly turned desperate, their mundane weapons unable to pierce the creature’s hide. Suraax stood her ground regardless, raising her shield and trusting the axe to guide her. When radiant power burst forth in her strike — what others would recognise as a divine smite — she took it as further proof that the weapon’s magic had awakened. Though the party was forced to retreat, Suraax managed to wound the monster badly enough to drive it off, buying time for the others to escape.

She returned to Phandalin battered but triumphant, more convinced than ever that the axe had chosen her for greatness. Seeking to better understand the legends of heroes and enchanted weapons like her own, Suraax later enrolled at Neverwood Academy and joined Lorehold. There she studies ancient histories and heroic sagas, certain that somewhere in the past lies the story of a weapon just like hers — and the destiny that accompanies it.

Suraax approaches life with bold enthusiasm and unshakeable confidence. She sees every challenge as an opportunity for glory and every story as a lesson waiting to be lived. Though sometimes naive in her certainty, she is fiercely loyal to her companions and always willing to place herself in harm’s way to protect them. Her greatest fear is not death or defeat, but the possibility that the axe might one day abandon her or prove to be ordinary after all.

Until that day — which she firmly believes will never come — Suraax Smith will continue charging headlong into danger, convinced that she and her mysterious axe are destined to carve their names into the legends she studies.
