## Biography
Ferbinious Refubical is a halfling sorcerer whose life has been shaped by curiosity, exile, and a peculiar inheritance he refers to simply as the curse. Standing barely two feet tall, with spiky amaretto-brown hair and wide grey eyes that seem permanently filled with wonder, Ferbinious cuts a somewhat unusual figure even among halflings. His dark skin is almost always smudged with soot, oil, or dirt from whatever device he has most recently dismantled in the name of “understanding how it works.” Despite the hardships that have followed him since birth, Ferbinious maintains an almost infectious cheerfulness, approaching the world with enthusiasm rather than resentment.

Ferbinious was born into a quiet village where generations of his family had carried a strange affliction — a lingering psychic influence left behind by an encounter with a Mind Flayer long ago. The condition manifests as aberrant magical power, unpredictable and unsettling to those unfamiliar with it. As Ferbinious grew older, the strange abilities began to reveal themselves in increasingly obvious ways: whispers at the edge of thought, unnatural intuition, and bursts of power he could neither explain nor fully control. Fearful of what this inheritance might bring, the villagers eventually cast him out, unwilling to risk whatever dangers they believed lurked within him.

Rather than allow bitterness to take root, Ferbinious chose a different path. He set out into the wider world determined to learn, build, and experiment. Fascinated by mechanisms and forgotten inventions, he developed a habit of collecting scrap and dismantling objects to study their inner workings. Many of his earliest “materials” were acquired through less-than-honourable means, and there are likely a number of merchants and travellers who would recognise him as the small halfling responsible for missing parts and mysteriously disassembled equipment. To Ferbinious, however, these acts are rarely malicious — merely steps in his ongoing quest to understand and improve the tools of the world.

His strange magical affliction has also shaped his beliefs. Ferbinious venerates Annam the All-Father, the giant deity associated with magic, knowledge, fertility, and philosophy. Influenced in part by the distortions of his curse, he holds an almost reverent view of giants, believing them to be divine beings whose wisdom and power represent the height of civilisation. This unusual faith often leads others to view him as eccentric, particularly when paired with his unexpectedly high and mismatched voice — a trait that frequently draws laughter from those who hear it for the first time.

Eventually, Ferbinious found his way to Neverwood Academy, where his interest in ancient knowledge and lost technologies led him to join Lorehold. There he studies both history and magical theory, hoping that somewhere in the past lies an answer to his condition. More importantly, he hopes to discover ways to harness his aberrant sorcery safely, transforming what others see as a curse into a tool for progress.

Despite being a social outcast, Ferbinious remains optimistic about the future. His dream is to one day return to the village that rejected him — not with anger or vengeance, but with inventions, knowledge, and proof that the strange power within him can be used for good. In his mind, the greatest victory would not be mastering the curse, but showing the people who feared him that it never made him a monster in the first place.
