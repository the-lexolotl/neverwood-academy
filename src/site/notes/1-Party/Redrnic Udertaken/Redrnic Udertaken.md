## Biography
Redrnic Udertaken is a Minotaur raised among the ploughed fields and low stone walls of a small farming township nestled in a valley near a sheltered cove. His people were poor in coin but rich in kinship, bound together by shared labour and long evenings of communal meals. That peace was shattered when a travelling cult swept through the region, turning its dark attention on the valley and its helpless inhabitants. Redrnic’s earliest memory of true terror is of fire and chanting — and of salvation arriving in the form of a holy knightly order, whose charge broke the cult’s ranks and drove them from the town.

From that day onward, Redrnic’s path seemed clear. He resolved to one day stand among those knights, to become a shield for others as they had been for him. Years of training followed, marked by physical discipline and earnest devotion. Yet when the time came for him to be tested and formally considered for initiation, another truth revealed itself: Redrnic possessed a rare and troubling form of magic, one that drew upon blood and vitality, and whose origins were bound to forces the holy order deemed suspect. Though not cast out as a monster, he was denied entry, told that such power could not be trusted without proof of its purpose.

Refusing to abandon his ideals, Redrnic sought another road forward. He enrolled at Neverwood Academy and joined Witherbloom, where life, decay, and the uneasy balance between them are studied without illusion. There, he began to examine his own abilities through the lens of blood magic, determined to understand their nature rather than fear them. Alongside his martial training as both barbarian and blood hunter, he has developed a keen interest in history and the natural world, believing that past civilisations and ancient ecosystems may hold examples of power once thought taboo being turned toward noble ends.

In character, Redrnic is earnest and quietly intense. He sees himself as a protector first and foremost, even when his temper and physicality push him toward violence. Among companions he is loyal and steadfast, though he struggles with the knowledge that his magic sets him apart from those he once hoped to join. His greatest fear is not rejection, but corruption - that the source of his power might one day shape his will rather than serve it.

Redrnic’s ultimate ambition is to return to the holy order not as a penitent, but as living proof that dark-born magic need not lead to dark deeds. He hopes to forge a legacy where blood magic is understood as a tool rather than a curse, and where strength drawn from suffering can be turned instead toward preservation. Until that day, he studies, fights, and endures, walking a narrow path between faith and forbidden power in the hope that both can be reconciled.
