## Biography
Vigilius Palamas grew up with two worlds pulling at his heart. Though his earliest memories trace faintly back to Dragonlance, he was not raised by his birth parents, whom he barely remembers, but by Corven and Elara Palamas - a pair of human traders who took him in as their own. Alongside his siblings, Kabo and Eliania, Vigilius spent his childhood on the road, moving from market to market. While he often felt distant from the societies around him, his bond with his human family has always been steadfast, and he treasures every opportunity to remain close to them.
At Neverwood Academy, Vigilius approaches his studies with quiet determination, though his outlook has recently changed. Where once he was driven by a need to uncover the truth of his birth parents, recent events - dangerous encounters and the rescue of a missing student - have forced him to reconsider what truly matters. Vigilius has chosen to stop chasing fragmented memories of a past he cannot reclaim, and instead focus on the life and family he has in the present. In doing so, he has grown closer than ever to those who raised him, embracing them fully as his true family.
In class, he still finds joy in subjects like A is for Ankheg, fascinated by the creatures that inhabit the world, while more academic disciplines continue to challenge his patience. Yet his growth has extended beyond the classroom. Recently, Vigilius has begun studying healing magic in small sessions with his fellow student Lyra, seeking to better protect those he cares about. Though not a traditional spellcaster, he approaches this new path with the same discipline that defines his monastic training.
Unlike many of his peers, Vigilius walks the path of a monk rather than a wizard or sorcerer, blending physical discipline with spiritual focus. This distinction continues to set him apart, though he no longer views it solely as a source of isolation. Instead, he channels his differences into strength, grounding himself in loyalty and purpose. His greatest fear remains failing those he cares about - but now, that fear drives him toward growth rather than doubt.
Vigilius does not follow a deity, but he respects the beliefs of others and strives to foster understanding wherever he can. His ideals remain simple: to do good, to protect the vulnerable, and to build bridges where others see only division. He continues to admire the qualities of those around him - Harry’s tenacity, Glue’s curiosity, and Lyra’s reason - while holding fast to the lessons of honesty and compassion instilled by his father. Though cautious of certain peers, he is learning that trust, like strength, is something built over time.
Looking ahead, Vigilius no longer seeks answers buried in the past, but purpose in the present. He envisions himself as a protector - not defined by where he came from, but by the choices he makes now. His path is no longer about reclaiming what was lost, but about safeguarding what he has found: a family, a purpose, and the strength to stand for others when it matters most.
