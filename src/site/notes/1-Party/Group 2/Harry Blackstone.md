## Biography 
Harry Blackstone is a sharp-minded student whose roots lie in Gillian’s Hill, though he often deflects questions about his past with evasions or half-truths. Raised for a time by his maternal grandfather Reynard Blackstone and his sister Adalynn, Harry carries conflicted feelings about his family: deep affection for Adalynn, lingering grief for Reynard, and a reluctance to confront either memory too closely. He avoids speaking of them openly, but their influence is never far beneath the surface.

At Neverwood Academy, Harry sets modest goals on paper — to pass his classes and keep afloat academically — but his true ambitions cut deeper. He craves knowledge, particularly in abjuration and divination, hoping to grow powerful enough to shield others and uncover hidden truths. His investigative streak drives him to chase mysteries others would shy away from, though this focus often leads him to neglect his studies or overreach beyond his ability. Quandrix studies suit him best, where his knack for connecting the dots and his raw magical force find fertile ground. More routine courses, such as General Studies, often fall by the wayside.

Harry presents himself as aloof, even brusque, with a sharp tongue and little patience for authority or rules. Beneath that exterior, however, is a student who shoulders responsibility to a dangerous degree. He is willing to risk himself, even recklessly, if it means protecting others. His fears — of failing his friends, of being watched by hidden enemies, of repeating tragedies like the death of Matreous — remain carefully hidden. Even trivial anxieties, such as an unshakable aversion to frogs, rarely escape his mask of control.

Though he claims not to worship any deity, Harry admires figures like Drizzt Do’Urden, who shaped their own destiny regardless of divine will. His distrust of the Neverwood faculty grows as he digs deeper into Academy mysteries, and he has little patience for those he sees as incompetent or secretive. Yet among his peers, despite his gruff manner, he has formed bonds of loyalty that run deep. For Harry, betrayal is only justified when life or death is at stake — though his judgment of what qualifies is sometimes questionable.

When he imagines the future, Harry sees himself as a scholar and protector, honing his magic in the quiet of a tower and offering his strength to those in need. He dreams of making knowledge free to all, stripped from the ivory towers of academia. Yet a shadow lingers in his vision of the years to come: a fear of becoming powerless, forced into a role he despises, or worse, failing so completely that others suffer for it. For all his cynicism and sharp edges, Harry Blackstone remains, at heart, a student trying to live up to the weight of responsibility he has claimed for himself.

# Traits

### Arcane Recovery
*PHB-2024, pg. 166*

When you finish a Short Rest, you can recover. The spell slots can have a combined level equal to no more than 1, and none of the slots can be level 6 or higher.

You can use this feature once per Long Rest.

Arcane Recovery: (No Action) 1/Long Rest

### Crafter
*PHB-2024, pg. 200*

-  Tool Proficiency. You gain proficiency with three different Artisan's Tools.
-  Discount. When buying an nonmagical item, you receive a 20 percent discount.

#### Fast Crafting.
When you finish a Long Rest, you can craft one piece of gear from the Fast Crafting table, if you have the associated tools and are proficient with those tools. The item lasts until you finish another Long Rest, at which point the item falls apart.
-  Woodcarver's Tools
-  Alchemist's Supplies
-  Jeweller's Tools
