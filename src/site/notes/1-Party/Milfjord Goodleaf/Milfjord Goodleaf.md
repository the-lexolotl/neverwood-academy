## Biography
Milfjord Goodleaf is a halfling druid of the Old Faith, born and raised in a quiet settlement nestled on the edge of an estuary where the river meets the sea. The village was a sanctuary of lush greenery and tranquil waters — until a nearby human harbour began to choke its beauty with smoke, refuse, and noise. Watching the slow decay of his homeland sparked something deep in Milfjord: a conviction that civilisation had wandered too far from the rhythm of the natural world. And so, with a satchel of herbs, a notebook full of questionable recipes, and a “pet” rock named Mossy, he set out to study the balance of life and decay.

At Neverwood, Milfjord has found his place among the Witherbloom scholars, delighting in all things that sprout, rot, or ferment. He is a passionate advocate for harmony between people and nature — though his methods are… unconventional. His experiments in potion-brewing often involve ingredients most would consider compost, and the results range from “surprisingly refreshing” to “legally classed as a biohazard.” Nevertheless, he insists that “there’s no growth without a little bit of mould.”

Despite his laid-back demeanour and fondness for hallucinogenic flora, Milfjord takes his mission seriously. He believes that the future of every race depends on rediscovering the sacred link between life, death, and regeneration — and he’s determined to teach others to touch grass both literally and spiritually. His peers might describe him as equal parts philosopher and stoner, but beneath the rambling monologues and moss-covered cloak lies a genuine heart and a sharp mind attuned to the pulse of the world around him.

Milfjord dreams of returning home one day as a community leader, guiding others to rebuild in harmony with nature rather than against it. Until then, he can usually be found tending to the Academy’s gardens, brewing potions of dubious legality, or staring meaningfully into the middle distance while discussing the interconnectedness of all things — particularly rocks.
