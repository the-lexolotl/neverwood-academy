*Academic Feat · Level 3+ · Completion of First Year at Neverwood Academy*
## Description
Your coursework has granted you practical magical techniques, drilled through study and repetition.
- You gain two 1st or 2nd level spells, each determined by courses you successfully completed during your first year.
- You can cast each of these spells once without expending a spell slot, and you regain all uses when you finish a long rest.
- You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have.
- Your spellcasting ability for these spells is your class’s spellcasting ability.
- If you do not have one, choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma.
