# Free Time Slots
Each character has **2 Free Time Slots** per tenday.
You may fill these with:
* **Extracurricular Clubs** (1 slot)
* **Jobs** (1 slot)
* **Guilds** (2 slots)

> ⚠️ Guilds require **both slots**, meaning you cannot take other activities alongside them.

---
# Activity Types

### Extracurricular Clubs
* Cost: **1 slot**
* Provide: **[[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Die]] (1d4)**
* Choose **1 of 2 skills** associated with the club
* Die refreshes every **tenday**

### Jobs (Basic Student Work)
* Cost: **1 slot**
* Provide: **Base Pay**
	* First Year: **5gp per tenday**
	* Second Year: **6gp per tenday**
	* Third Year: **7gp per tenday**
	* Forth Year: **8gp per tenday**
* Pay can be increased with **[[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Skills System\|Student Skill Points]]**

### Guilds
* Cost: **2 slots**
* Provide:
	* **[[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Die]] (1d4)**
	* Choose **1 of 3 skills**
	* Access to **guild jobs (extra pay)**
	* Opportunities to earn **[[Group Cup Points\|Group Cup Points]]**
* Base Pay:
	* **6gp per tenday**
	* **+ additional pay** for completing guild jobs
* Commitment:
	* Some guilds require **async tasks per term**

---
## [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Dice]]
* A **[[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Die]] = 1d4**
* Can be added to relevant **skill checks**

### Refresh & Usage
* Student Dice from **Clubs and Guilds** refresh every **tenday**
* Student Dice from **Exams** are **one-time use bonuses**

### How to Gain Student Dice
* Joining an **[[2-Player Handouts/Academic Information/Extracurricular Clubs/Extracurriculars List\|Extracurricular Club]]**
* Joining a **Guild**
* **Performing well in [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Neverwood Academic System\|Exams]]**

> 🎯 Characters may hold multiple Student Dice from different sources.

---
## Choosing Your Setup

### Option 1: Balanced
* 1 Job + 1 Club
  ✔ Income + utility

### Option 2: Double Job
* 2 Jobs
  ✔ Maximum consistent income
  ✖ No Student Dice from activities

### Option 3: Double Club
* 2 Clubs
  ✔ Multiple Student Dice
  ✖ No income

### Option 4: Guild Focus
* 1 Guild (uses both slots)
  ✔ Strong rewards + story impact
  ✔ Student Die + bonus pay
  ✖ No additional activities

# Group Cup Points
You can earn Group Cup points through:
* Guild jobs and activities
* Event participation
* Exceptional performance in roles
* Academic achievement
Guilds are the **primary source of high-value Group Cup rewards**.

| Activity Type | Slots | Pay | Student Die       | Extra Benefits    |
| ------------- | ----- | --- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Club          | 1     | ❌   | ✅ (1d4, 2 skills) | Utility + flavour |

| Guild         | 2     | ✅   | ✅ (1d4, 3 skills) | Jobs + Group Cup  |

# Final Notes
* Activities can typically be changed at the **start of a new academic year**
* Guild membership is **limited and application-based**
* Student Dice represent your character’s **growth beyond the classroom**
* Student Skill Points represent **long-term personal development**

*“At Neverwood, what you do outside your studies may matter just as much as what you learn within them.”*
