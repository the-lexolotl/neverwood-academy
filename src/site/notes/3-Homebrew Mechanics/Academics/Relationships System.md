# Overview
Relationships represent how your character connects with student NPCs over time.
You track this using Relationship Points (RP). These reflect friendship, rivalry, trust, and personal history.
Relationships grow through:
- Conversations
- Shared activities
- Guild work
- Story events
- Player choices

# Relationship Points (RP)
All NPC relationships begin at:
- 0 RP = Neutral Acquaintance
Each meaningful interaction changes RP by:
- +1 RP = positive interaction
- -1 RP = negative interaction
The DM may adjust this for major story moments (±2 or more for significant events).

## Relationship States
### Friend (5+ RP)
An NPC becomes a Friend when their RP reaches the friendship threshold.
**Effects:**
- You gain a Bond Boon from that NPC
- The Boon provides a small narrative or mechanical benefit when relevant
- The effect is active as long as they remain your Friend

### Rival (-5 RP or lower)
An NPC becomes a Rival when their RP reaches the rivalry threshold.
**Effects:**
- You gain a Bond Bane from that NPC
- The Bane creates complications or disadvantages when interacting with them
- The effect is active as long as they remain your Rival

### Neutral (between -4 and +4 RP)
- Between your friendship and rivalry thresholds
- No mechanical effects
- Most relationships start here

## Changing Relationship Thresholds
Your [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Skills System\|Student Skill Points]] (Popularity) can change how easily you form Friends or Rivals.
### Base Thresholds
**Friend:** +5 RP
**Rival:** -5 RP
### Popularity Adjustments
For every point of Popularity above 2:
- Friendship threshold lowers by 2 RP
- Rivalry threshold becomes more extreme by +2 RP
#### Example
If your Popularity is 4:
- Friendship threshold becomes +1 RP
- Rivalry threshold becomes -9 RP
This means:
- You become Friends more easily
- You become Rivals less easily

## How Relationship Points Change
You gain or lose RP through:
- Conversations and roleplay
- Working together in clubs, guilds, or jobs
- Helping or hindering NPCs
- Exam and study interactions
- Story decisions

# Beloveds
### Becoming Beloveds
If you have:
- 10 RP or more with a Friend
- You may choose to deepen the relationship during a Relationship Encounter and become Beloveds.
- This occurs in addition to any normal interaction during that encounter.
### Beloved Inspiration
If you have one or more Beloveds:
- You gain Beloved Inspiration at the end of each long rest
- Beloved Inspiration works like normal Inspiration (Player’s Handbook rules)
- You regain a number of uses of Beloved Inspiration each long rest equal to your number of Beloveds
- You cannot have more uses than your proficiency bonus
### Ending Beloved Relationships
You may end a Beloved Relationship at any time.
When you do:
- The NPC stops granting Beloved Inspiration
- They remain in your relationship system at their current RP
- Their status reverts to Friend (if RP is still 5+)

# Bonds
### Bond Boons (Friends)
When someone becomes your Friend, you gain a Bond Boon from them.
These can include:
- Small bonuses to specific skill checks involving them
- Help during relevant story moments
- Access to favours, advice, or assistance
- Narrative advantages tied to your relationship
### Bond Banes (Rivals)
When someone becomes your Rival, you gain a Bond Bane from them.
These can include:
- Disadvantage in interactions with them
- Increased difficulty when competing against them
- Social or narrative complications
- Obstruction in shared situations

# Relationship Progression
| State   | Requirement                       | Effect              |
| ------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Friend  | 5+ RP (Adjusted for Skill Points) | Bond Boon           |
| Rival   | -5 RP (Adjusted for Skill Points) | Bond Bane           |
| Beloved | 10+ RP (Friend)                   | Beloved Inspiration |
