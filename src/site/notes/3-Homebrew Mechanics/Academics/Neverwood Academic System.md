At Neverwood Academy, your academic success is measured across three areas:
- Homework
- Labs (Practical Classes)
- Exams
Each course combines these into a final grade at the end of term.

*⚠️ **Important:** Each class sets its own grade weighting (how much homework, exams, and labs matter). Make sure to check with your DM for each subject.*

# Homework
Students receive two pieces of homework per class each tenday.
- Homework is due at the end of each tenday
- Each assignment requires one ability check
### Grading Homework
- Any roll above 20 counts as 20
- The student’s homework grade is the average of all homework rolls

# Labs (Practical Classes)
Labs represent hands-on learning experiences, and may take the form of:
- Combat encounters
- Roleplay scenarios
- Ability check challenges

### Grading Labs
Grades are assigned at the DM’s discretion based on performance:
- Failed
	- The student died, didn’t participate, or performed poorly
- Passed
	- The student completed the task successfully
- Distinction
	- The student demonstrated initiative, creativity, or exceptional performance

### Lab Grade Conversion
Fail: 0–50%
Pass: 50–90%
Distinction: 90–100%

# Studying
Each character may choose one study method before exams:

### Standard Study
- Make an ability check
	- On success: gain 1 exam reroll
### All-Nighter
- Gain 2 exam rerolls
- Gain 1 level of exhaustion
### Study Together
- Gain advantage on study check
### Skip Studying
- No rerolls gained

# Exams
Each exam consists of 2 ability checks.
- Players may use rerolls gained from studying and [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Skills System\|Student Skill Points]]
- The student’s exam grade is the average of the two rolls
## Results
### Failed
- 0 successful checks
- No [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Dice]] gained
### Passed
- 1 successful check
- Gain 1 [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Die]]
### Aced
- 2 successful checks
- Gain 2 [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Dice]]

## Cheating (Optional Risk Route)
To attempt cheating, the player must succeed on:
- A Deception check
- A Sleight of Hand check
Failure consequences are at DM discretion (typically higher than normal failure outcomes).

# Final Grades
At the end of the year, each subject gives you a final percentage grade, based on:
- Homework
- Exams
- Labs
(Weighting varies by class.)

## Grade Bands
Your required passing grade depends on your year:
### Years 1–2
**Fail:** 0–59%
**Pass:** 60–89%
**Distinction:** 90–100%
### Years 3–4
**Fail:** 0–69%
**Pass:** 70–89%
**Distinction:** 90–100%

# Rewards
### During Term
- Exams award [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Dice]] based on your performance
- You will also gain [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Skills System\|Student Skill Points]] as you progress through the term
	- These represent your character’s growth, learning, and experience at the academy
	- They can be spent according to the [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Skills System\|Student Skill Points]] system
### End of Year Rewards
At the end of the academic year, rewards are based on your overall performance:
- You gain rewards from each class you pass
- Higher grades may result in better or additional rewards (at your DM’s discretion)
##### Additional Rewards
You may also earn rewards from:
The [[z_Website/Group Cup Challenge\|Group Cup Challenge]]
Special events, festivals, or competitions
Story-based achievements during the year
