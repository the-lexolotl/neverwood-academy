# Student Skill Points
Student Skill Points represent a character’s long-term development outside of standard class progression.
They are earned through:
* Academic performance
* Guild participation
* Clubs and extracurricular involvement
* Jobs and real-world experience

### Skill Categories
Student Skill Points are invested into one of five learning styles:
* **Studied Learning** – academic focus, lectures, homework
* **Applied Learning** – hands-on practice, exams, experimentation
* **Extracurriculars** – clubs, sports, and non-academic growth
* **Jobs** – part-time work and income experience
* **Popularity** – social influence and relationships

## Student Skill Perks
Once a character has **3 or more points in a category**, they unlock the associated perk:

### Studied Learning
You are devoted to academic study and written work.
* You may complete **one homework assignment between classes**, roll with advantage

### Applied Learning
You learn best through doing and repetition.
* You begin each [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Neverwood Academic System\|exam]] with **one reroll available**

### Extracurriculars
You thrive outside the classroom in [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Free Time System\|clubs and activities]].
* Your [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Student Dice System\|Student Die]] from [[2-Player Handouts/Academic Information/Extracurricular Clubs/Extracurriculars & Guilds\|Extracurriculars]] increases in size per Extracurriculars Skill Point (e.g. 1d4 → 1d6 → 1d8)
* You gain **one additional Student Die source per week** (from clubs/extracurricular involvement)

### Jobs
Your work ethic is recognised and rewarded.
* You are offered **higher-paying positions**
* You earn **+1 gold per Job Skill Point per tenday of work

### Popularity
Your social presence shapes how others treat you.
* [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Relationships System\|Friendship thresholds]] are **lowered by 2 per point above 2**
* [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Relationships System\|Rival thresholds]] are **increased by 2 per point above 2**

> Example: Popularity 4 shifts thresholds to **6 (friendship)** and **-14 (rivalry)**
