| 1st   | ! Bonus Action | 60 ft.     | S, M (A Glass of Water) | 1 Round  | Enchantment | CON Save    | Incapacitated |
You silently level a finger at a creature you can see within range, focusing your will into a sudden and deeply embarrassing enchantment. The target must make a Constitution saving throw.

- On a failed save, the creature immediately and involuntarily voids their bladder and becomes incapacitated until the start of their next turn. If they are wearing clothing, it is thoroughly soiled.
- On a successful save, the creature still urinates, but manages to quickly remove or adjust their clothing to avoid soiling it. They are not incapacitated.

This spell affects any creature that wears clothing and has a body capable of urination. Creatures without clothing are still affected, but have nothing to soil.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:** You can choose to cast this spell at a higher level, increasing the duration by 1 round per level. For example, at level 3, this lasts 2 rounds
