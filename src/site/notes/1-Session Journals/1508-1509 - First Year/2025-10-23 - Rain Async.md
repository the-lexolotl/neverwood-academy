## Summary
An evening of art and innovation spirals into chaos when the [[2-World/Groups/Colleges/Prismari College\|Prismari]] Gallery’s annual exhibition is struck by magical sabotage. Amidst the shattering of enchanted glass and the warping of living paintings, [[1-Party/Her/Her\|Her]] discovers a deeper power within her art — one that binds emotion, magic, and self-expression into something transcendent. What begins as a disaster becomes a revelation, as H’er’s soul ignites in colour and [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Elandra Stormwhisper\|Elandra]] witnesses the birth of a new kind of magic.

### The Exhibition Begins
* The Prismari Gallery opens its doors, filled with floating artwork, elemental music, and displays that blur the line between spellcraft and emotion.  
* H’er stands beside her own creation, *The Light Between* — a living prism of shifting colour that mirrors her feelings.  
* [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Elandra Stormwhisper\|Elandra]], radiant and thoughtful, admires the piece, calling it “beautiful” and saying it “feels like you.” The moment is warm, intimate — until chaos erupts across the hall.

### Chaos in the Gallery
* A nearby sculpture by [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Cadoras Damellawar\|Cadoras Damellawar]] shatters without warning, shards of enchanted glass and frost scattering through the air. Other exhibits begin to flicker, colours draining into black static.  
* Investigating, H’er learns from [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Cadoras Damellawar\|Cadoras]] that his magic had worked perfectly before the show. The implication is clear: someone tampered with the art.  
* Before H’er can react, the magical tension spreads. Paintings twist, music distorts, and a young loxodon artist, [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1507/Emeline Redstream\|Emeline Redstream]], watches in panic as her portrait of Tasha deforms into a chaotic mess of angles and colour.

### The Living Prism
* [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Elandra Stormwhisper\|Elandra]] takes H’er’s hand, drawing her attention back to *The Light Between* — now pulsing in time with her heartbeat.  
* The prism whispers in colour, emotions translating into movement and sound. H’er touches the surface again and feels the entire gallery resonate in reply.  
* The paintings and sculptures come alive, responding to her presence. In the mirrored surface of her prism, the true saboteur is revealed — their reflection flickering amid the waves of colour.

### Catching the Saboteur
* Channelling her newfound control, H’er shapes the prism’s colours into a ball of living light and hurls it toward the reflection. The orb bursts, splattering across the culprit and exposing them before the crowd.  
* As the onlookers gasp, H’er lunges forward and tackles the figure with help from nearby students. The staff arrive and restrain the saboteur — a bitter third-year who had been harshly criticised for their last work.  
* With their removal, the unstable magic subsides, leaving the gallery aglow in soft, living colour.

### The Mural of Souls
* When the light fades, a new mural remains — a shimmering masterpiece of refracted hues and emotional resonance. Within it, the shapes of two figures can be seen: H’er and Elandra, hands entwined in the chaos.  
* Elandra gazes at the mural in awe, whispering, “Whatever that was… You didn’t just paint with colour. You painted with your soul.”

### Ongoing Threads
* **The Prismari Gallery Incident** – A night of beauty and chaos that left the faculty speechless. The magical residue of the gallery still hums faintly with emotion.  
* **The Saboteur’s Motive** – The jealous third-year’s act of sabotage may have deeper roots in Prismari’s culture of competitive artistry.  
* **The Light Between** – H’er’s living artwork evolved beyond her intention, responding to emotion and thought. Its true nature — and potential — remain unknown.  
* **Elandra’s Connection** – Her bond with H’er deepened in the storm of colour and chaos; the mural that bears their likeness hints at a fate intertwined by magic and art.
