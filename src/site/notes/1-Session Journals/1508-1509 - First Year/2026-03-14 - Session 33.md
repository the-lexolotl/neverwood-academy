## Summary
The crawl descends into true chaos at **The Rambling Toad**, where riddles, rituals, and reckless experimentation culminate in a dangerous elemental encounter. Through trial, error, and brute force, the party uncovers the correct sequence to complete the challenge and distil their next drink. From there, they proceed to **The Beauty’s Mirror**, where artistry — not violence — is required. After missteps and broken mugs, the group discovers the true nature of the “vessel,” unlocking the final draught. With minds altered and purpose revealed, the party learns the location of a mysterious sixth pub beyond Neverwinter… and sets off in pursuit.

### The Rambling Toad
* The party arrives at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Rambling Toad\|The Rambling Toad]], a ramshackle structure of mismatched, decaying extensions.
* A warped veranda wraps around the front; croaking and buzzing fill the air as silent patrons watch.
* [[1-Party/Cyinth Agora/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]] drunkenly waves at the crowd.
* [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] attempts to order a “Screwball Swill.”
	* The barkeep claims no such drink exists.
* [[1-Party/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] corrects the name to “Skewball Swill.”
	* The barkeep confirms and explains:
	    * The drinks are free.
	    * The party must complete a task in the **garden out back**.
* Cyinth is mistaken for a child:
	* The barkeep produces a massive tome to verify Aarakocra drinking age.
	* Her explanation fails to convince him.
	* She escalates by threatening him (unsuccessfully) and demonstrating the shrinking potion via a homunculus.

### The Garden Trial
* In the garden:
	* A stone-lined pool sits at the centre.
	* A bullywug strums a guitar, singing cryptic lyrics: *“Pass it along, drink it down to make you strong.”*
	* Five pedestals surround the pool, each depicting a different creature:
	    * Carrier Crawler
	    * Dragon
	    * Kobold
	    * Otyugh
	    * Shrieker
* The bullywug offers only one clue: **“Kask.”**

### Trial by Error (and Combat)
* The party experiments with pouring liquids into the pedestals.
* Incorrect sequencing triggers consequences:
	* An **otyugh** appears behind Cyinth.
	* A malformed **ooze wyrmling** manifests, initiating combat.
* The fight quickly escalates:
	* Vigilius retreats, taking a hit.
	* Cyinth is knocked unconscious by the wyrmling’s breath.
	* Suraax is heavily wounded.
	* [[1-Party/Vith Pahlok/Vith Pahlok\|Vith]] uses ice breath, weakening the creature.
	* T.1.T.4.N charges in and deals significant damage.
	* Suraax revives Cyinth with Lay on Hands and grapples the wyrmling into the pool, dissolving it.
	* Vigilius finishes another creature by slamming it into the water.
* The bullywug provides a clearer hint: **kobolds eat mushroom bowls**.

### Solving the Sequence
* The correct order is discovered:
  1. Shrieker
  2. Kobold
  3. Carrier Crawler
  4. Otyugh
  5. Dragon
* As each step is completed, the liquid changes.
* At the final stage, it becomes **golden with the scent of ethanol** — the completed drink.
* Cyinth, still disoriented, mistakes her homunculus for a fairy and thanks it.

### The Beauty’s Mirror
* The party travels to [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Beauty’s Mirror\|The Beauty’s Mirror]].
* Suraax attempts to order in Draconic and is told to speak Common.
* She is initially denied entry due to her appearance and quickly changes into her formalwear.
* Inside, they meet [[Bellina\|Bellina]], the barkeep.
* She presents them with a poem:

> *A pub that crawls, to enter, a price must be paid,*
> *Along this crawl, its five pieces we laid,*
> *To craft a draught that will take you to the end,*
> *You must depict the vessel, these pieces, to mend.*

### Missteps in the Studio
* Cyinth ends up in the kitchen, requests mugs for painting, then immediately breaks them.
* She rejoins the group in the art studio with the remains.
* The room contains many depictions of **bottles**, and two of a **bearded man** (a charcoal drawing and a broken bust).
* The significance is not immediately understood.
* The party begins painting:
	* Suraax and Cyinth attempt to decorate mugs.
	* [[1-Party/Vith Pahlok/Vith Pahlok\|Vith]] has Folass run through paint across a canvas.
* Bellina intervenes, explaining:
	* The potion is only complete when **all five drinks are finished**.
	* Their current approach is incorrect.

### Becoming the Vessel
* Realisation dawns:
	* The “vessel” is not an object — it is **a person**.
* Suraax begins a **self-portrait**.
* Cyinth questions whether she qualifies, having not consumed all prior drinks.
* Upon completion, a **hidden mechanism clicks**:
	* A portrait swings open, revealing an alcove.
* Inside:
	* A bottle of dark red liquid.
	* A cup for each party member.
* The party drinks without hesitation.

### The Sixth Location Revealed
* A warmth spreads through them.
* As it reaches their heads, they gain sudden clarity:
	* The location of a **sixth pub**, far north beyond Neverwinter.
* Bellina provides:
	* A **Decanter of Endless Spirits**
	* Tin cups
	* A sealed envelope
* The note inside reads:
  > *“When you find it, drink until the pub stops spinning.”*

### The Final Chase
* Cyinth has T.1.T.4.N carry her, enhanced with *Longstrider*.
* The rest of the party uses Grifft transport to pursue.
* In the distance, they see it:
	* A **ramshackle pub on long legs**, spinning wildly.

## Ongoing Threads
* **The Sixth Pub** – A moving, spinning structure beyond Neverwinter. The final destination of the crawl.
* **The True Nature of the Crawl** – No longer a rumour or hazing ritual — this is something real and magical.
* **Bellina’s Role** – She knowingly guides participants toward the final stage.
* **The Decanter of Endless Spirits** – A powerful magical item tied directly to the crawl’s completion.
* **“Drink Until It Stops Spinning”** – A cryptic instruction that likely defines the final challenge.
* **Accumulated Chaos** – Theft, destruction, public disturbances, and Academy intervention are all catching up to the party.
