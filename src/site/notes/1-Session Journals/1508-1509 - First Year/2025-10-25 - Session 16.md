## Summary
After the chaos of recent weeks — suspensions, detentions, and the fallout from the Chasey Lane incident — life at Neverwood Academy slowed down. The students took advantage of the lull to catch up on studies, relax, and try (mostly) to stay out of trouble.

### Session Events
* The morning began quietly. Lyra stayed in her dorm room, catching up on notes and coursework, while the rest of the party headed to the **Biblioplex**.  
* Harry and Vigilius spent time researching **cyphers and coded spells**, hoping to help Harry decipher the mysterious spellbook gifted to him by **[[2-World/People/Other/Unspecified/Professor Delta\|Professor Delta]], [[2-World/Groups/The Silent Equation\|The Silent Equation]]**.  
* Glue stopped by to chat with **[[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/General/Isabrough\|Isabrough]]** about their date before grabbing breakfast at **Whalebucks**.  
* At Whalebucks, Glue met a **new transfer student named [[1-Party/Marionette Moonbeam/Marionette Moonbeam\|Marionette]]**. The two struck up an odd but friendly conversation that took a turn when Glue asked her to sign over her bones after death. Marionette, understandably cautious, said she’d need time to think about it.  
* Deciding to do something fun, Glue invited Marionette to join the group for a swim. She agreed, and Glue brought her over to meet the rest of the party.  
* The students made their way to [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/The Dramarium\|The Dramarium]] on [[2-World/Places/Silverquill Campus\|Silverquill Campus]], only to realise they’d forgotten to grab swimwear from their dorms.  
* With the pool building empty, Harry cast **Alarm** and secured the door before everyone (except Lyra) stripped down to varying degrees of undress and jumped into the water.  
* The group took turns **showing off, diving, and splashing around**, enjoying a rare moment of peace and laughter amidst the usual chaos of academy life.  
* Lyra, however, remained poolside — lounging on a chair and quietly **studying the strange sigil** that had recently appeared on her arm, its meaning still unknown.

### Ongoing Threads
* Lyra’s mysterious **sigil** — what is it, and why did it appear?  
* Harry’s **encoded spellbook** from Professor Delta remains unsolved.  
* The **date between Glue and Isabrough** went well, what's next?  
* The **new student, Marionette**, has joined the story — but what brought her to Neverwood Academy?
