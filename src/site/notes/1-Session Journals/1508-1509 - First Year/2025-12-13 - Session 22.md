## Summary
What begins as a desperate escape from disciplinary consequences quickly becomes a journey beyond Neverwood itself. As a runaway beanstalk pierces the clouds, the party is split by lightning, authority, and gravity — forcing staff intervention and an ultimatum from the Head Teacher herself. Those who climb higher find themselves no longer above the Academy, but *elsewhere*: a cloud-walking realm of giant roads, oversized cottages, and a storm giantess whose hospitality hides a far darker tale involving curses, lost love, and a shadowy figure from the Material Plane.

### The Beanstalk Incident
* [[1-Party/Group 3/Marionette Moonbeam/Marionette Moonbeam\|Marionette]], [[1-Party/Group 2/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]], [[1-Party/Group 1/Guiloip Guhloo/Guiloip Guhloo\|Glue]], and [[1-Party/Group 1/Atudark/Atudark\|Atudark]] cling to the still-growing beanstalk as it rises into the clouds.
* The weather turns violent:
	* Lightning shaped like **ancient runes** lashes the sky.
	* The students hold on as best they can.
* Several winged [[2-World/Groups/Neverwood Staff\|Neverwood Staff]] fly up, begging the students to come down.
* A powerful lightning strike hits the beanstalk:
	* Marionette and Harry lose their grip and begin to fall.
	* Glue calls on [[1-Party/Group 1/Guiloip Guhloo/Cynthia\|Cynthia]], who swoops down and rescues Marionette, returning her to the beanstalk.
	* Harry continues falling, swearing as he plummets.
* [[2-World/People/Staff/Silverquill/Mavinda Sharpbeak\|Mavinda Sharpbeak]] dives after Harry, catches him, and lowers him safely to the ground.
* Harry attempts to flee — only to run straight into Head Teacher [[2-World/People/Staff/Taiva Loreweaver\|Taiva Loreweaver]].

### Lyra Responds
* [[1-Party/Group 2/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]], relaxing in her dorm, hears a loud crash from somewhere on campus.
* She investigates and reaches the ground floor of the [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Biblioplex\|Biblioplex]] just in time to hear students loudly discussing:
	* A beanstalk
	* Missing students
* Rushing outside, Lyra looks up and sees a massive green beanstalk rising from a building on [[2-World/Places/Lorehold Campus\|Lorehold Campus]].
* Knowing Glue and Marionette are involved, she takes flight and follows the beanstalk upward.

### An Ultimatum from the Head Teacher
* Lyra arrives to see Harry being yelled at by several staff members.
* [[2-World/People/Staff/Taiva Loreweaver\|Taiva Loreweaver]] delivers a blunt command:
	* **“Fix whatever you caused, retrieve anything dangerous, and shut that sky portal before someone else climbs it.”**
* Harry and Lyra head back up the beanstalk together.

### Climbing Beyond Neverwood
* As they climb higher, the world grows stranger:
	* Lightning shaped like arcane runes.
	* Giant-sized feathers drifting down from above.
	* A distant harp note echoing on the wind.
* Eventually, they reach the top.
	* Despite the height, the air is not thin.
	* Looking back down, they see only clouds — Neverwood’s campus is completely gone.
* Glue tests the clouds by standing on them.
	* He sinks slightly but does not fall through.
	* Concluding it’s safe, he wanders off toward a distant **giant road sign**.
* Harry and Lyra realize they must have **plane shifted** and follow him.

### Skyberry House
* The road sign is enormous, clearly built for creatures many times their size.
* It points east and reads: **“Skyberry House.”**
* With no better option, the party follows the sign.
* After a long walk, a quaint cottage appears on the horizon.
	* Everything is far too large.
	* The front steps tower over the students.
* They struggle up the steps to the door.
	* Marionette listens at the door, hearing faint singing.
	* Lyra flies up and rings the doorbell.
* The door opens to reveal a **huge storm giant**.
	* Half-asleep and irritated, he declares they are not taking visitors.
	* He slams the door shut.

### Breaking In (Politely)
* Glue decides to squeeze through the keyhole.
	* With effort, [[1-Party/Group 1/Guiloip Guhloo/Guiloip Guhloo\|Glue]], [[1-Party/Group 1/Guiloip Guhloo/Buhuiop\|Buhuiop]], and [[1-Party/Group 1/Guiloip Guhloo/Cynthia\|Cynthia]] manage to wriggle through.
	* After briefly getting distracted, they open the door from the inside.
* The party begins exploring:
	* [[1-Party/Group 3/Marionette Moonbeam/Shroom\|Shroom]] breaks into a cupboard and steals several pieces of **blue grain**.
	* Glue heads toward the singing, with Marionette and Atudark following.
	* Lyra and Harry head the opposite direction, finding an empty parlour.

### Teanna of Skyberry House
* Glue makes his way through the dining room and into the kitchen.
* There he encounters a beautiful giantess.
	* With Cynthia’s help, Glue climbs onto the counter and clears his throat
	* The giantess screams briefly, then covers her mouth in shock.
* Glue apologises and asks for food.
* The giantess introduces herself as [[2-World/People/Other/Teanna Aethelos\|Teanna]].
	* She begins cooking Glue an omelette while they talk.
* Glue explains he arrived with friends.
	* Teanna goes to find the rest of the party.
	* She finds Marionette and Shroom near the kitchen and invites them to breakfast.
	* She then heads to the parlour.

### Gathering the Guests
* In the parlour, Lyra and Harry hide beneath the oversized furniture.
* Teanna notices Harry dart under a chaise lounge.
	* She kneels and makes gentle kissy noises, as if calling a cat.
* Harry cautiously emerges.
* Teanna explains she has already found some of his friends and invites him to breakfast.
* After confirming he will *not* be breakfast, Harry agrees.
* Teanna lifts him onto her palm and carries him to the kitchen.
	* She sets him on the counter beside Glue and Marionette.
* Teanna places a huge plate of omelette before them and encourages them to eat.
	* Marionette declines eggs and asks for coffee.
	* Teanna pours a cup and sets it on the counter.
	* Marionette climbs into the mug and bathes in the coffee.

### A Curse Revealed
* As they eat, Teanna speaks of her husband.
	* He was once kind.
	* He could sing storms into rainbows.
* She explains he is now cursed:
	* *“The quill found him. The quill that writes wounds into hearts.”*
* The curse was placed by a powerful magic user from the [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]]:
	* **The Shadow-Handed Scribe**.

### Elsewhere in the House
* Meanwhile, [[1-Party/Group 2/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] and [[1-Party/Group 1/Atudark/Atudark\|Atudark]] continue moving around the ground floor.
	* Lyra searches for anything important.
	* Atudark searches for Lyra.
* Lyra hides inside a coat cupboard.

### Ongoing Threads
* **The Beanstalk Portal** – A planar breach still connecting Neverwood to a cloud realm. How will it be closed?
* **Head Teacher’s Ultimatum** – Taiva expects results, not excuses. Failure will have consequences.
* **Skyberry House** – A giant-scale home in a foreign realm, occupied by beings tied to storm and song.
* **Teanna’s Husband** – Cursed by the Shadow-Handed Scribe, once gentle, now something else.
* **The Shadow-Handed Scribe** – A dangerous figure from the Material Plane whose magic leaves lasting scars.
* **Lyra’s Separation** – Alone and hiding, Lyra may uncover something — or be discovered herself.
