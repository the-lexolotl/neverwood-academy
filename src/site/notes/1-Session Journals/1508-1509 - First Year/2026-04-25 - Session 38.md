## Summary
The students begin their summer work placement with [[2-World/Groups/Wintershield Watchmen\|The Wintershield Watchmen]] in [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]], taking on the duties of city patrol. What starts as routine guard work quickly turns into a grim investigation when they discover the body of an undercover agent.
Following a strange magical clue - a severed, enchanted eyeball - the group is led beneath the city into the catacombs, where they encounter dangerous creatures lurking in the depths. Their first assignment rapidly escalates into a deadly confrontation, leaving several of them wounded and aware that something far more sinister is at work beneath Neverwinter.

### Patrol Duty
* The students begin their placement with [[2-World/Groups/Wintershield Watchmen\|The Wintershield Watchmen]]:
	* Tasked with patrolling the streets of [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]
	* Focused on maintaining public safety
* The first few days pass:
	* **Uneventfully**
	* Allowing the group to settle into their roles

### A Grisly Discovery
* While patrolling the [[2-World/Places/River District\|River District]], the group hears a **blood-curdling scream** from a nearby alley
* They discover:
	* A woman holding a dying man
	* The man passes away moments later
* Notable details:
	* A **desecrated eyeball** falls from his hand
	* A **large, unnatural chest wound** is observed

### Meeting Kevori Fearnehart
* The woman identifies herself as [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/Kevori Fearnehart\|Kevori Fearnehart]]
* The deceased is her brother, [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/Delvin Fearnehart\|Delvin Fearnehart]]
**Key Information:**
* Both were working for [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/Dagult Neverember\|Lord Neverember]]
* Tasked with investigating a **cult operating in the city**
* Delvin had:
	* Infiltrated the cult
	* Planned to meet Kevori at a [[2-World/Businesses/Whalebucks Coffee Co.\|Whalebucks]]
* He died before he could share what he learned
* Kevori asks the party to:
	* **Find those responsible**

### The Eyeball
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] examines the eyeball:
	* Detects **divination magic**
* The group searches Delvin’s body:
	* Finds coins
	* Recovers a hidden note reading:
		* *“Hail the Undying”*

### Eremis’ Misstep
* [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis Varr]] takes the eyeball:
	* Puts it in her mouth
	* Accidentally **swallows it**
* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] administers a potion:
	* Forces Eremis to vomit
	* The eyeball is recovered (along with various unpleasant contents)

### The Eyeball’s Function
* When the note is read aloud:
	* The eyeball begins to **glow green**
* Once placed on the ground:
	* It spins and points **north-east**
* Key discovery:
	* The eyeball always points in the same direction
	* Acts as a **magical compass**
* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] flies ahead:
	* Determines the eyeball eventually points **downward**
	* Suggesting a location **beneath the city**

### Into the Catacombs
* The party returns briefly to their inn:
	* Files paperwork
	* Eats
	* Plans next steps
* They travel to a **catacomb entrance**:
	* The eyeball directs them inside 
* Entering the tunnels:
	* Lyra provides **darkvision** where needed
	* The group descends into darkness

### The Flooded Chamber
* The tunnel opens into:
	* A **partially collapsed chamber**
	* Filled with **brackish water**
* The party splits:
	* Lyra wades through the water toward a western passage
	* [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis Varr]] squeezes into a narrow eastern gap
	* [[1-Party/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] and [[1-Party/Ferbinious Refubical/Ferbinious Refubical\|Ferbinious]] follow her

### The Water Creature
* The water begins to move unnaturally
* Lyra is grabbed and pulled under
**Response:**
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] rushes to help 
* [[1-Party/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] finds an alternate route through a tomb 
* Combat ensues with a **water-like creature**
* Vigilius is nearly killed:
	* Stabilised and later healed by the group 
* The party:
	* Successfully defeats the creature

### Deeper in the Catacombs
* Meanwhile, [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis Varr]] explores further:
	* Enters a chamber filled with **sewage**
	* Encounters **three sludge creatures** made of undead matter
* Eremis:
	* Attacks from range
	* Laughing as she destroys them

### Regrouping
* The rest of the party follows the sound of Eremis’ laughter
* They arrive as she defeats the final creature
* With threats temporarily cleared:
	* The group assesses their **situation**
	* Several members are **badly injured**

### End of Session
* The party chooses to:
	* **Rest and recover** in the tunnels
	* Prepare before venturing deeper into the catacombs

## Ongoing Threads
* **The Cult of “The Undying”** – A dangerous organisation operating beneath [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]
* **Delvin Fearnehart’s Death** – The investigation has only just begun
* **The Eyeball Artifact** – A powerful divination tool guiding the party forward
* **The Catacombs** – Clearly infested with unnatural and undead threats
* **Party Condition** – Resources are strained early into the mission
* **What Lies Below** – The true objective of the cult remains unknown
