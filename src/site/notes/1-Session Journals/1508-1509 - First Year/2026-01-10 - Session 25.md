## Summary
The students dive into exam prep at the [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Biblioplex\|Biblioplex]], but the quiet studious atmosphere is punctuated by new faces, magical showmanship, and an impromptu display of destructive talent. Lyra encounters a mysterious new first-year, tensions flare as Vith Pahlok makes a move, and Suraax demonstrates her combat prowess. Meanwhile, Vigilius and Lyra add flair to the study hall, and H’er experiments with magic a little too literally — earning a fine. After completing the **A is for Ankheg** Owlbear exam, the students leave the hall only to receive a mysterious note from a familiar owlin, hinting that their next challenge is already waiting.

### Studying at the Biblioplex
* The students gather in the **Biblioplex** to prepare for the upcoming Owlbear exam.
* Lyra notices a **new first-year student** and moves to introduce herself.
* [[1-Party/Group 3/Vith Pahlok/Vith Pahlok\|Vith]] approaches aggressively, attempting to flirt, but is swiftly **shut down by Suraax**.
* Lyra brings [[1-Party/Group 3/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] to meet [[1-Party/Group 2/H'er/H'er\|H'er]] and [[1-Party/Group 1/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]].

### Magical Showmanship
* Suraax **shows off her axe**, impressing the group.
* Vigilius dons his **Cloak of Billowing** and strikes a dramatic pose, clock magically fluttering behind him.
* Lyra channels radiant magic, making herself **emanate a warm glow**.
* H’er experiments with her magic, **aging a table until it disintegrates**, earning a **4gp fine from the librarian**.

### The Owlbear Exam
* All first-years complete the **A is for Ankheg** Owlbear exam.
* As students file out of the exam hall, an **owlin from Bow’s End Tavern** hands H’er a [[2-Player Handouts/Others/Crumpled Paper\|crumpled piece of paper]].
* The mysterious note hints that the next adventure — or complication — is already in motion.

### Ongoing Threads
* **New First-Year Student** – Lyra’s introduction could have ripple effects among the students and staff.
* **Vith Pahlok’s Advances** – Already rebuffed, but his presence may cause future tension or rivalry.
* **Magical Showmanship** – Vigilius, Lyra, and H’er are drawing attention; the fine hints at consequences for unchecked magical experiments.
* **The Crumpled Note** – Delivered by a familiar owlin, this note may kick off the next plot or quest.
* **Exam Results and Owlbear Knowledge** – The outcome of the A is for Ankheg exam may affect first-year standings, reputation, and confidence.
