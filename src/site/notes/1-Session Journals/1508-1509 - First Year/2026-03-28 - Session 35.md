## Summary
In the aftermath of **Ricard Ruanad’s** death, **Neverwood Academy** is thrown into controlled chaos. A partial lockdown is enforced, and an official investigation begins under the watch of [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Administration/Taiva Loreweaver\|Taiva Loreweaver]] and [[2-World/People/Other/Unspecified/Arcturus Vell\|Investigator Vell]]. Over the following tenday, the party is questioned, each offering their own perspective on the events within **The Pub That Crawls**. As truths, half-truths, and omissions surface, tensions linger — both within the group and across the Academy.
Even as exams proceed, the weight of recent events takes its toll. When strange drinks appear unbidden at a campus [[2-World/Businesses/Whalebucks Coffee Co.\|Whalebucks]], the party is once again drawn toward something unnatural — something that may still be watching them.

### Academy Lockdown
* Following [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] returning [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Administration/Ricard Ruanad\|Ricard Ruanad]]’s body to the Academy:
	* A **partial lockdown** is enacted.
	* Students are ordered back to their dormitories.
* The following morning:
	* An announcement confirms **classes and exams will continue as normal**.
	* An **official investigation** into Ruanad’s death is underway.
* [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Administration/Taiva Loreweaver\|Taiva Loreweaver]] and [[2-World/People/Other/Unspecified/Arcturus Vell\|Investigator Vell]] spend the next tenday:
	* Interviewing students and staff.
	* Reconstructing the events surrounding the crawl.

### Testimonies
#### **[[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]]**
* Recounts the pub crawl and final confrontation.
* Explains:
	* Ruanad’s interference triggered the pub’s deadly mechanism.
	* She knocked him unconscious to protect those inside.
	* [[1-Party/Cyinth Agora/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]] then killed him while he was defenceless.
#### **[[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]]**
* Supports the account but reframes Cyinth’s actions:
	* Claims it was **self-defence and defence of others**.
* Reveals:
	* [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/The Pub That Crawls/Boyan\|Boyan]]’s projected memories.
	* Ruanad’s destruction of **[[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Lucid Dreamer\|The Lucid Dreamer]]** and **[[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Rambling Toad\|The Rambling Toad]]**.
	* His plans to exploit the pub’s planar abilities.
* Offers to take Investigator Vell to **The Pub That Crawls**.
#### **[[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]]**
* States he was not present at the moment of death.
* Admits initial disapproval of Cyinth’s actions.
* Concedes that, given Ruanad’s actions, they **may have been justified**.
#### **[[1-Party/Atudark/Atudark\|Atudark]]**
* Claims to have been **too drunk to remember anything**.
* Provides no useful testimony.

### Return to The Pub That Crawls
* [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]] summons **[[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Pub That Crawls\|The Pub That Crawls]]** to [[2-World/Places/Central Campus\|Central Campus]].
	* The pub staggers into [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Archway Commons\|Archway Commons]] and settles.
* Redrnic, Lyra, and Investigator Vell enter.
* Inside:
	* Cyinth greets them calmly with tea.
	* She warns Vell not to drink anything else present.
* Cyinth’s account:
	* Ruanad had sent another professor (unnamed) to follow them.
	* His ignorance of the pub’s rules would have led to everyone being killed.
* [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/The Pub That Crawls/Boyan\|Boyan]] confirms her version of events.
	* Provides a **vial containing a glowing silver thread**:
	    * Extracted memories from Ruanad prior to his death.
* Investigator Vell thanks them and departs, stating he may return with further questions.
* The party remains briefly before returning the pub to the **Detention Bog**.

### Exams and Aftermath
* End-of-term exams proceed:
	* Several students perform poorly due to recent stress and events.
* Afterwards, Professor [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Quandrix/Senestrine Darou\|Senestrine Darou]] confronts the party:
	* Demands to know what they told the investigator.
* The party reassures her:
	* They did **not reveal her involvement**.
* Darou, conflicted, asks if she should come forward:
	* The party suggests it likely won’t matter — the memories may already expose her.
* She decides to **speak with the investigator regardless**.

### The Whalebucks Incident
* The party regroups at a campus [[2-World/Businesses/Whalebucks Coffee Co.\|Whalebucks]].
* During conversation:
	* **Drinks mysteriously appear** at their table.
* No one claims responsibility.
* Cyinth drinks first:
	* Experiences a vivid **flashback of killing Ruanad**.
* She then drinks Lyra’s:
	* Hears overlapping voices — testimonies from students and staff.
* [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]] drinks:
	* Feels an overwhelming presence.
	* Briefly glimpses **something watching the pub**.
* [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]]:
	* Overwhelmed, crushes her cup in her hand, spilling the drink.

### Something Watching
* Concerned, Red insists they check on **[[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Pub That Crawls\|The Pub That Crawls]]**.
* The party travels to the [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Detention Bog\|Detention Bog]]:
* Red remains on high alert.
* On arrival:
	* Cyinth enters the pub.
	* Red scans the treeline, sensing something.
* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] takes flight:
	* Spots [[2-World/People/Other/Unspecified/Arcturus Vell\|Investigator Vell]] concealed among the trees.
	* Acknowledges him silently.
	* Tells Red there is nothing there.
* Red, unconvinced:
	* Charges a nearby tree — only to realise his mistake.
* The party regroups inside the pub.

### Closing Moments
* Within **[[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Pub That Crawls\|The Pub That Crawls]]**, the party:
	* Talks.
	* Drinks.
	* Begins to process recent events.
* Plans for the future are cautiously discussed.
* The investigation continues in the background.
* Whatever is tied to the pub… may not be finished with them yet.

## Ongoing Threads
* **The Investigation** – Investigator Vell now holds testimonies *and* extracted memories.
* **Cyinth’s Actions** – Justified or not, the consequences are still unfolding.
* **Professor Senestrine Darou** – Has chosen to come forward — outcome unknown.
* **The Whalebucks Drinks** – Unexplained, powerful, and deeply personal in effect.
* **Something Watching** – Red’s vision suggests an external force observing the pub.
* **The Pub That Crawls** – Still active, still mysterious, and possibly still a target.
* **Fractured Trust** – The party remains together… but not united.
