## Summary
The crawl continues at **The Frothing Flask**, where the party must navigate a magical bridge puzzle overseen by a many-armed construct bartender. After successfully solving the challenge through a mixture of ingenuity and mild rule-bending, they receive guidance for the next stop: **The Lucid Dreamer**. There, the students must command the stage to earn their drink. What follows is an unforgettable performance involving purple transformations, Russian roulette, heroic storytelling, and a captivated audience — until Academy constructs abruptly shut the establishment down. With disciplinary action looming, the party makes a hasty escape through a secret exit into the [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] night.

### The Frothing Flask
* The party arrives at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Frothing Flask\|The Frothing Flask]], a tavern overgrown with moss and glowing blue runes.
* Inside, the room is divided into small islands separated by flowing streams.
* Waterfalls veil narrow bridges, briefly parting only as travellers pass.
* At the centre stands the construct bartender [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/The Pub That Crawls/Vani\|Vani]], a four-armed automaton serving drinks.
* Vani explains the challenge:
	* Each bridge must be crossed **once and only once**.
* The party begins navigating the puzzle:
	* [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]] uses flight and [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/T.1.T.4.N\|T.1.T.4.N]] to bypass some of the waterfalls.
	* [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] discovers a passage through an upstairs storeroom that allows the rest of the group to complete the route.
* With the puzzle completed, Vani congratulates the party.
* When asked about the next location, she advises that at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Lucid Dreamer\|The Lucid Dreamer]] they must **command the stage with a captivating performance**.

### Arrival at The Lucid Dreamer
* The party travels to [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Lucid Dreamer\|The Lucid Dreamer]] and finds a long queue outside.
* Preparing for the evening’s performance, they change into their outfits.
* Suraax steps down the side of the building where a staff member is smoking and asks for privacy.
* Shortly after, strange tearing and chewing sounds are heard.
* When [[1-Party/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] looks back, Suraax has transformed into a **vibrant purple**, her shed skin nowhere to be seen.

### Bypassing the Queue
* The group realises they will not reach the stage quickly if they wait.
* Suraax attempts to convince the bouncer they should be let in because they are participating in **The Pub That Crawls**.
	* The bouncer refuses.
* Cyinth creates a distraction using **alchemist’s fire**, allowing the group to slip inside.
* They are required to check their belongings at the entrance.
	* Suraax manages to keep her axe.

### Commanding the Stage
* Inside, the tavern is packed so tightly that moving through the crowd is difficult.
* Cyinth attempts to intimidate the crowd using *Thaumaturgy*, but the effect is undermined by her small stature.
* Suraax offers to lift her up — giving Cyinth the height needed to reach the bar.
* Cyinth orders a **Sunset Liquor**.
* The bartender calls the manager, [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/The Pub That Crawls/Baz\|Baz]], who reiterates the rule:
	* To earn the drink, the party must command the stage.
* Cyinth takes the stage:
	* [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/T.1.T.4.N\|T.1.T.4.N]] joins her.
	* She draws her revolver and begins playing **Russian roulette with herself**.
	* After the first round she confirms gambling is legal, then invites the crowd to place bets.
	* The crowd becomes fully invested as the spectacle continues.
* Baz declares that Cyinth has successfully commanded the stage.

### Suraax’s Story
* Baz invites Suraax to perform next.
* Suraax tells the story of how she acquired her axe.
* The audience reacts with awe as she recounts the tale.
* Satisfied, Baz invites the party back to the bar and prepares a **Sunset Liquor** for each of them.

### Academy Intervention
* As the drinks are served, screams erupt from the main room.
* Ten large constructs enter the tavern.
* In unified metallic voices they announce:
> “Due to reports of activities disruptive to the academic institution, this pub is now closed by order of Vice Head Teacher [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Administration/Ricard Ruanad\|Ricard Ruanad]]. Please halt immediately so you may be processed for proper disciplinary action.”
* The constructs form a perimeter as patrons attempt to flee.
* Several are restrained as the constructs begin enforcing the closure.

### Escape into the Night
* [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/The Pub That Crawls/Baz\|Baz]] quickly gathers the party.
* He offers them an escape route through a **secret storage-room exit**.
* The party follows him through the hidden passage.
* Moments later, they emerge back into the night air of [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] — ahead of the pursuing constructs.

## Ongoing Threads
* **Vice Dean Ruanad’s Order** – The Academy is now actively intervening in the crawl.
* **Construct Enforcement** – The golems appear to be targeting disruptive students specifically.
* **Baz’s Assistance** – The Lucid Dreamer’s manager may now be implicated in helping them escape.
* **The Crawl Continues** – Two pubs down, but the remaining challenges may grow more dangerous.
* **Cyinth’s Performance** – Public Russian roulette may have drawn more attention than intended.
* **Suraax’s Transformation** – Her sudden purple coloration and shedding raise new questions about what exactly happened outside the tavern.
