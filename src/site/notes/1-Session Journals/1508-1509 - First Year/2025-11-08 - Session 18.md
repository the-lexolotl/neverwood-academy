## Summary
A festive Midwinter Feast quickly turns from cosy celebration to icy catastrophe when a mysterious snow elemental crashes through the Great Hall and steals the Academy’s treasured Star of Remembrance — right as Marionette and Glue are halfway through stealing it themselves. The party launches into a chaotic campus-wide chase that leads them deep into a worsening blizzard, where they take refuge in a frozen hunting hut hiding a grim secret, a dead hunter, and a chilling diary that suggests the elemental may not be acting alone.

### The Midwinter Feast
- The party begins in the decorated Greathall for the annual Midwinter Feast. A towering Christmas tree stands at the front of the hall, topped with a radiant **Star of Remembrance**.
- Students mingle, eat, and enjoy the festivities.
- Lyra pulls aside [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Lysander Frostbane\|Lysander Frostbane]] and warns him that trouble tends to follow the party — so he should stay alert.
- Marionette eyes the glowing star with intent and asks [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Grayson Wildemere\|Grayson Wildemere]] about it.
    - Grayson explains that the Star of Remembrance is a magical orb tjmhat stores memories of past Midwinter celebrations.
- Marionette and Glue decide to steal it.
    - They begin climbing the tree.
    - Glue is assisted by his octopus [[1-Party/Archived/Guiloip Guhloo/Buhuiop\|Buhuiop]] and his familiar [[1-Party/Archived/Guiloip Guhloo/Cynthia\|Cynthia]].

### The Star is Stolen
- Just as they attempt to grab the star, a massive **ice and snow elemental** bursts through the hall doors.
- The creature beelines for the star with terrifying speed.
- The party races to intercept it — but the elemental reaches the tree first and tears the Star of Remembrance free.
- It escapes out of the Greathall and across campus, the party in hot pursuit.
- The chase ends when the elemental takes to the sky, disappearing into the storm.
- Glue alerts a Campus Guide Automaton, ensuring the staff are informed before the group heads out after the elemental.

### Into the Frozen Forest
- The party follows its tracks into the snow-covered woods beyond campus.
- The weather grows steadily worse the further they travel, wind and snow beating against them.
- After a long march through the blizzard, they discover a small wooden **hunting hut**.
- They decide to take shelter inside.

### The Frozen Hut
- The interior is completely flash-frozen — walls, floor, furniture, everything coated in ice.
- A corpse lies on the floor: a hunter seemingly frozen solid by the same force that took the star.
- To keep warm, the party constructs a fire.
- Inspecting the corpse, they carefully retrieve a small diary from its frozen grip: **the [[2-Player Handouts/Others/Hunters Journal\|Hunters Journal]]**.
- After reading it, Glue drags the frozen body to the washroom to… _dispose of it_ (eat it).

### Nightfall in the Blizzard
- Exhausted and half-frozen, the party settles in for the night.
- They agree to rest in shifts, posted at watch, knowing the elemental — or whatever drove it — could still be near.

### Ongoing Threads
- **The Stolen Star of Remembrance** – The Academy’s most cherished Midwinter relic has been taken by an ice elemental. Why was it targeted?
- **The Ice Elemental’s Escape** – It fled beyond campus and into the deep forest. What lies further ahead in the storm?
- **The Frozen Hunter** – The corpse and diary suggest the elemental has attacked before — or is part of something larger.
- **The Hunter’s Journal** – What secrets or warnings does it contain about the creature or the forest?
- **Marionette & Glue’s Heist Attempt** – Their attempted theft may complicate later explanations with staff — if anyone learns about it.
- **Sheltering in the Hut** – A temporary refuge, but danger still waits in the blizzard beyond.
