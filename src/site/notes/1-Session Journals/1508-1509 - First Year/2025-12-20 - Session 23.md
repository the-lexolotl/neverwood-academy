## Summary
Breakfast with giants turns abruptly into chaos when Aurum Aethelos awakens in a foul mood and mistakes Atudark for a mouse. As the kitchen is reduced to rubble, the party scatters, hides, and improvises — culminating in Harry climbing _inside_ a raging giant’s ear to buy time. While Aurum rampages, the rest of the group uncovers a hidden study, befriends an emotional-support golden goose, and makes off with magical loot. The students escape down the beanstalk with treasures in hand — and the looming certainty that the Academy will have _opinions_ about what they’ve done.

### Breakfast with the Giants
- The party finishes breakfast with [[2-World/People/Other/Teanna Aethelos\|Giantess Teanna]] in the giant home.
- An alarm clock goes off upstairs, followed by heavy footsteps descending the stairs.
- Panic ensues as the party scrambles to hide:
    - [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] hides under a bowl.
    - [[1-Party/Guiloip Guhloo/Guiloip Guhloo\|Glue]] dives into a bag of flour.
    - [[1-Party/Marionette Moonbeam/Marionette Moonbeam\|Marionette]] and [[1-Party/Marionette Moonbeam/Shroom\|Shroom]] hide beneath the oven.
    - [[1-Party/Atudark/Atudark\|Atudark]] hides in a cupboard — but is spotted mid-sprint by the giant.
- The giant, [[2-World/People/Other/Aurum Aethelos\|Aurum Aethelos]], enters the kitchen and begins shouting at his wife about “mice in the house,” clearly referring to Atudark.

### Aurum’s Rage
- Aurum flies into a rage and starts smashing up the kitchen.
- The violence flips the bowl Harry is hiding under, exposing him.
- The party attempts to flee the kitchen amid the destruction.
- Before Marionette escapes, [[2-World/People/Other/Teanna Aethelos\|Teanna]] urgently explains that Aurum will calm down if someone brings him his **emotional support goose** — _the Golden Grimmhen_.

### The Upstairs Heist
- The party rushes upstairs:
    - Glue flies on the back of his familiar [[1-Party/Guiloip Guhloo/Cynthia\|Cynthia]].
    - Everyone else climbs the stairs on foot.
- **Harry stays behind** in the dining room to distract Aurum.
    - He heckles the giant, then performs the unthinkable — climbing up Aurum’s body and into his **ear canal**.
    - Aurum panics, roaring and thrashing as he tries to remove Harry from his ear.
- Upstairs, the rest of the party follows the sound of soft harp music.
- Glue discovers a study and pulls a book from the shelf, opening a hidden door.

### The Golden Grimmhen
- Beyond the secret door, the party finds:
    - A massive **golden goose** sitting atop several gold eggs.
    - A harp playing gentle music by itself.
    - A large chest containing a heavy leather pouch.
- Glue befriends the Golden Grimmhen.
- He takes the **harp** and the **leather pouch**.
- With Aurum still raging downstairs, the party decides to flee immediately.

### Escape Down the Beanstalk
- The group races back downstairs and out the front door.
- [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] shouts to Harry that they’re leaving.
- Harry climbs out of Aurum’s ear canal and sprints after the others as fast as he can.
- With their stolen loot in tow, the students escape back down the **giant beanstalk**.
- As they descend, they discuss how they might deal with Academy staff — and the consequences of growing a massive beanstalk into a giant’s home.

### Ongoing Threads
- **The Golden Grimmhen** – An emotional-support goose of immense value and significance. What happens when the giants realise it’s gone?
- **Stolen Relics** – The self-playing harp and leather pouch are likely magical — and likely missed.
- **Aurum Aethelos’s Wrath** – Harry’s ear-canal gambit will not be forgotten.
- **The Beanstalk Problem** – The Academy will inevitably respond to a giant beanstalk, theft from giants, and several broken rules.
- **Giant Politics** – Teanna’s quiet assistance may complicate future interactions with her husband — and with giant society at large.
