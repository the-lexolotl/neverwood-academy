## Summary
With the chaos of the previous tenday settling into uneasy normalcy, **Term 6** begins — the final stretch of the students’ first academic year at **Neverwood Academy**. While lessons resume and exams are completed, the shadow of the ongoing investigation lingers. Students attempt to refocus on their studies, social lives, and future placements, but the presence of [[2-World/People/Other/Unspecified/Arcturus Vell\|Investigator Vell]] serves as a constant reminder that consequences are still unfolding.
As the term progresses, results are finalised and **summer work experience assignments** are distributed — setting the stage for what comes next.

### Return to Routine
* Students return to academic life with the start of **Term 6**.
* Those who:
	* Joined late, or
	* Missed previous exams
	    are given the opportunity to **resit** their missed exams.
* Much of the term is spent:
	* Studying
	* Attending lessons 
	* Preparing for final assessments

### A Watchful Presence
* Throughout the term, students occasionally spot [[2-World/People/Other/Unspecified/Arcturus Vell\|Investigator Vell]]:
	* Interviewing staff and students.
	* Quietly observing from a distance.
* His continued presence reinforces that the investigation is **ongoing**.

### Personal Pursuits
* [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]] searches the [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Biblioplex\|Biblioplex]] for texts on **blood curses**:
	* Finds nothing within the sections he has access to.
	* Suggests restricted or hidden knowledge within the library.

### Student Life
* Despite everything, students make time to reconnect:
	* Meeting at [[2-World/Businesses/Whalebucks Coffee Co.\|Whalebucks]].
	* Spending time in the **First Year Common Room**.
* Social moments provide brief relief from recent tension and pressure.

### Exams Conclude
* Students sit their resit exams:
	* Overall performance is **strong**, despite recent disruptions.
* Discussion continues around upcoming assessments, including:
	* The final “A is for Ankheg” exam, focusing on Otyughs.

### Work Experience Assignments

* **Summer work experience assignments** are distributed by campus guide automatons.
