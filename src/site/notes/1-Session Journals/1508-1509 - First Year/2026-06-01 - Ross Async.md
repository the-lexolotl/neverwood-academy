## Summary
During the break between major events, [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] withdraws from social life and throws himself into magical study and work. Through scroll scribing, experimentation, and the decoding of a mysterious spellbook, he begins engaging with unstable and unfamiliar magic tied to a group known as **The Silent Equation**.

This culminates in a clandestine meeting with [[2-World/People/Other/Unspecified/Professor Delta\|"Professor Delta"]], where Harry delivers completed work in exchange for payment, further deepening his involvement in something he does not yet fully understand.

### Withdrawal and Focus
* Following recent events, Harry becomes increasingly isolated:
	* Distances himself from others, particularly after anger surrounding [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]]
	* Focuses almost exclusively on:
	    * Attending classes
	    * Studying
	    * Deciphering the mysterious spellbook
* Outside of academics:
	* Spends time stewing on unresolved emotions
	* Begins producing spell scrolls for sale with the help of Cadoras

### Scroll Scribing Business
* Over several months:
	* Cadoras brings **33 scroll requests**
	* Increasing demand from:
	    * Upperclassmen
	    * Students requesting **modified or custom spells**
* Harry chooses to:
	* Attempt modifications himself rather than outsource
	* Occasionally seek help when needed
* Outcomes:
	* Most modifications succeed
	* One request (modified *Chaos Bolt* for performance use) proves difficult
* After failed attempts:
	* Cadoras is unable to assist
	* [[1-Party/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] is consulted but cannot help 
	* Harry eventually turns to [[1-Party/H'er/H'er\|H'er]]:
	    * Pays **20gp** for assistance
	    * Completes the scroll at a **5gp loss**
* Despite setbacks:
	* The business proves highly profitable
	* Total earnings: **396gp**

### Deciphering the Spellbook
* Harry continues working through the spellbook’s pages using **Arcana checks and experimentation**
#### Page 2 – *Constant Zero*
* Enchantment-based magic:
	* Removes energy from living systems
	* Described as “reducing variance to null”
* Notes suggest:
	* A philosophy of stillness through subtraction
* Successfully copied to a scroll

#### Page 3 – *Recursive Function*
* Illusion magic with fractal, self-repeating runes 
* Noted as:
	* Slightly unstable
	* Based on recursive manipulation of reality
* Inscription:
	* “Reality is an iterative function; loop long enough, and it rewrites itself.”
* Successfully copied

#### Page 4 – *Mean of Averages*
* Abjuration-style stabilising magic:
	* Balances harm and protection
* Harry tests the spell on himself:
	* Feels temporarily resilient
	* Still takes damage, but reduced
* Insight:
	* Not true immunity — more like **damage smoothing or mitigation**
* Successfully copied

#### Page 5 – *Fractal Bloom*
* Hypnotic geometric patterns:
	* Difficult to study directly
* Attempts:
	* Magnification reduces hypnosis but distorts the spell
	* Library research fails
* With Cadoras’ help:
	* Successfully deciphered and copied
* Noted phrase:
	* “All colour is chaos seeking symmetry.”

#### Page 6 – *Limit Approaching Zero*
* Incomplete spell:
	* Appears to invert gravitational principles
* Harry:
	* Uses *Magnify Gravity* as a reference
	* Completes the spell through experimentation
* Final note:
	* “When gravity is cancelled, so too is consequence.”
* Spell is:
	* Successfully completed
	* Added to both scroll and spellbook

### Costs and Commitment
* Total scribing costs:
	* **275gp** invested into spell production
* Despite the expense:
	* Harry prioritises magical knowledge over wealth

### The Meeting with Delta
* Harry arranges a meeting with [[2-World/People/Other/Unspecified/Professor Delta\|Professor Delta]]:
	* Location: **Cultivarium on Quandrix campus**
	* Cadoras observes from a distance under magical concealment
* Harry delivers the completed scrolls:
	* Delta provides a pouch containing:
	    * **375gp** (costs + 100gp reward)
* Conversation reveals:
	* Delta speaks cryptically about:
	    * “Solving the weave”
	    * Harry as a “variable”
	* Confirms:
	    * The spellbook is Harry’s to keep
	    * He will be contacted again
	* Harry presses for answers:
		* Receives none of substance

### Attempted Confrontation
* As Delta departs:
	* Harry attempts to use *Lightning Lure* to reveal his identity
* Result:
	* Delta resists the spell
	* Escapes without revealing his face
* Harry responds:
	* Openly expresses distrust

## Ongoing Threads
* **The Silent Equation** – A mysterious group studying or attempting to “solve” the weave
* **Delta** – An unknown figure manipulating and testing Harry
* **The Spellbook** – Contains unstable, modified, and philosophically driven magic
* **Unstable Magic** – Many spells show signs of alteration and unpredictability
* **Harry’s Isolation** – Emotional withdrawal and increasing obsession with magic
* **Cadoras’ Involvement** – Acting as assistant, collaborator, and lookout
* **Fractal & Zero-Based Magic** – Recurring themes of recursion, balance, and nullification
* **Trust and Secrecy** – Harry actively hiding his work from staff and most peers
