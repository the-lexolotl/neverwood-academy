## Summary
The final days of **Year 1** at **Neverwood Academy** are marked by exams, celebration, and unexpected chaos. As students prepare for their *“A is for Ankheg: A Complete Guide on 26 Classic Monsters”* final, a quiet request from [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Witherbloom/Verelda Lang\|Verelda Lang]] sets up one last task.
What begins as an end-of-year festival at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/The Rose Stage\|The Rose Stage]] quickly spirals into panic when a rampaging owlbear interrupts the performance - raising new concerns about strange magical interference on campus.
The following day, the students travel to [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Sedgemoor\|Sedgemoor]] to assist Professor Lang, uncovering unusual creatures, dangerous wildlife, and a mysterious diary tied to a familiar name. Only after their return does the long-running investigation finally reach its dramatic conclusion.

### Final Lessons & A Request
* Students spend their final days:
	* Attending lessons
	* Studying for their **Ankheg course final exam**
* During class, [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Witherbloom/Verelda Lang\|Verelda Lang]] approaches the group:
	* Requests their help gathering **spell components** in [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Sedgemoor\|Sedgemoor]] on the final day of term
	* The students agree

### The Final Exam
* The group completes their final exam:
	* **No incidents occur**
	* A rare moment of calm before the term’s end

### The Rose Stage Festival
* Students attend the **end-of-year festival** at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/The Rose Stage\|The Rose Stage]]:
	* Watching [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] perform in *[[2-Quests/The Merchant of Moons\|The Merchant of Moons]]*
* Lyra, though performing well, remains:
	* Nervous
	* Alert to the crowd

### A Disruption Unfolds
* Mid-performance, Lyra spots a **suspicious figure** in the crowd:
	* Signals to her friends using magic
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] and [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]] respond:
	 * Harry communicates via *Message*
	 * Cyinth identifies the same figure
* The figure slips into the **backstage prop area**:
	 * Cyinth pursues from above
	 * Harry follows

### The Owlbear Incident
* While searching backstage:
	* Harry and Cyinth encounter a **dazed owlbear**
	* The creature is startled and bursts onto the stage
* Panic erupts:
	* Audience and performers scatter
**Response:**
* [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] attempts to calm the owlbear
* [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]] charges in, attacking it
* The owlbear retaliates, injuring Red
* Professor [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Silverquill/Luc Brandford\|Luc Brandford]] intervenes:
	* Subdues and captures the creature

### The Black Goo
* After the incident:
	* A strange **black substance** is found:
	    * On the stage
	    * On prop room door handles
* [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Silverquill/Luc Brandford\|Luc Brandford]] examines it:
	* Identifies it as **eldritch balm** used by the academy
	* Notes:
	    * It should not cause this kind of behaviour
	    * Suggests something has **corrupted its source**

### Preparing to Leave
* That evening:
	* Students pack and prepare to leave for the summer
* The next morning:
	* They meet Professor Lang at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Sedgemoor\|Sedgemoor]]

### A Warning from Luc Brandford
* On the way, they encounter [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Silverquill/Luc Brandford\|Luc Brandford]]:
	* Explains:
	    * The spring used to create the balm may be **corrupted**
	* Asks [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] to assist:
	    * Provides a **flask of holy water**
* Party dynamics:
	* Lyra and Suraax argue over who carries it
	* Red questions its origin and requests his own supply

### Into Sedgemoor
* The group meets [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Witherbloom/Verelda Lang\|Verelda Lang]]:
	* Receives [[2-Player Handouts/Others/Professor Lang's List\|a list of ingredients]]
* They venture into the wetlands to gather materials

### Strange Encounters
* At research huts:
	* A **worg** emerges from a tree stump:
	    * Demands: *“Give me your magic!”*
	* Lyra and Cyinth take cover
	* Suraax attempts conversation
	* The worg eventually loses interest and retreats
* The group gathers:
	* Ingredients
	* Supplies, including:
	    * Harvesting pouches
	    * A **potion of healing**

### The Snake Attack
* While crossing water:
	* [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]] slips and disturbs **venomous snakes**
	* He is bitten and **knocked unconscious**
* [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]]:
	* Heals [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]]
	* Helps drive off the snakes

### The Willow Tree & The Diary
* Searching for the final ingredient:
	* The group locates a **large willow tree** near running water
* While harvesting:
	* [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]] collects bark 
	* [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] discovers a **buried diary**
* The diary bears the name:
	* **Murgaxor**
* Tension arises:
	* Lyra insists it is important
	* Suraax refuses to hand it over
	* Cyinth takes and reads it before returning it

### Return to Professor Lang
* The group delivers the gathered ingredients:
	* Professor Lang thanks them
* Suraax attempts to hand over the diary:
	* Lyra and Cyinth intervene 
	* The diary is **kept within the group**

### The Investigation Concludes
* Upon returning to [[2-World/Places/Central Campus\|Central Campus]], the investigation reaches its end:
	* Students witness [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]] and Deputy Head Teacher [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Administration/Ricard Ruanad\|Ricard Ruanad]] being escorted off campus by agents of the [[Arcane Council\|Arcane Council]]
	* Ricard is somehow **alive again**, despite prior events
* Both are **arrested**:
	* Ricard’s fate remains unknown
	* Cyinth is to be transferred to [[The Kindled Mind Project\|The Kindled Mind Project]]
* The moment marks a heavy and uncertain end to the academic year

### End of Term
* With the arrests concluded, students prepare to depart:
  * Leaving [[2-World/Places/Central Campus\|Central Campus]] behind for their **summer assignments**
