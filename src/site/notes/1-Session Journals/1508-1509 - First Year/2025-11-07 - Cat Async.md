## Summary
A search for coin and curiosity draws Marionette into Neverwood’s shadowy underbelly — a labyrinth of ivy, ink, and intrigue. A cryptic thieves’ cant message leads her to the cunning [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Ricard Ruanad\|Ricard Ruanad]] and his “lost item” in the forbidden Root Archives. What begins as a simple job quickly becomes a descent into danger, as Marionette battles living vines, a flood that hungers, and her own better judgment — emerging with secrets, silver, and an unexpected companion: a terrified, talking mushroom.

### The Offer from R.R.
- Restless and short on funds, Marionette scours campus notice boards for work, finding a subtle thieves’ cant message: _“Want a job? — R.R.”_
- Checking the faculty roster, she narrows the initials to two suspects: [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Quandrix/Renald Rothton\|Renald Rothton]] and [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Ricard Ruanad\|Ricard Ruanad]].
- Ronald’s classroom yields nothing, but Ricard’s office door bears his initials carved into the wood — a clear sign to those who know how to look.
- Unable to see inside, Marionette climbs a drainpipe to the window. When she slips, she narrowly avoids a deadly fall by **Fey Stepping** through the air — landing ungracefully on Ruanad’s desk.

### Secrets in the Office
- Searching the room, Marionette finds a notebook with a missing page and a to-do list mentioning “talk to Vigilius.”
- Using graphite and cunning, she takes a rubbing of the torn page, revealing two key phrases: **“Root Archives”** and **“Old Witherbloom.”**
- She picks the lock, slips out unseen, and sends a message through a campus guide automaton:  
    _“Interested in your job. — M.M.”_
- That evening, a note slides under her dorm door:  
    _“I lost something down in the Root Archives. Locate it for me and you shall be rewarded handsomely. — R.R.”_

### Descent into the Root Archives
- Marionette infiltrates the old Witherbloom wing of [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Widdershins Hall\|Widdershins Hall]], picking through rusted locks and creeping ivy.
- The **Root Archives** lie below — flooded, tangled with moving vines, and thick with damp decay.
- She proceeds carefully, noticing signs of a previous disturbance: scorched vines and broken roots.
- When a tendril lashes around her leg, she slices it free and presses deeper, discovering an antechamber dominated by a mass of writhing vegetation guarding a sealed chest.

### Fire and Sap
- Testing the creature, Marionette tosses a rock across the room — the vines twitch in response.
- With practiced precision, she **catapults a vial of oil**, igniting it with her lantern. The blast fills the chamber with light and smoke, burning the monstrous vines to ash.
- Inside the freed chest, she finds **financial ledgers signed by Ricard Ruanad** and a **vial of shimmering silver liquid.**

### The Shroom Thief
- On her way out, a small **mushroom-like creature** waddles into her path. It freezes in terror when she speaks.
- Using _Speak with Animals_, Marionette coaxes the frightened creature — nicknamed [[1-Party/Marionette Moonbeam/Shroom\|Shroom]] — into conversation. It warns her of lurking dangers: living vines, sentient water, and ancient roots that hunger.
- The sound of trickling water grows into a rushing flood. The liquid on the floor begins to rise and _move_. Grabbing Shroom, she races for the exit as the water surges into monstrous form.
- At the top of the stairs, she slams the archive door shut and notices droplets leaking through the keyhole — a slow, ominous drip. Thinking quickly, she **freezes the door**, trapping the watery horror below.

### A Thief and Her Apprentice
- Realising she forgot to relock the door, Marionette returns — only for the door to _knock back._ She wisely retreats, sealing it again with frost.
- Shroom trembles in her hands, unsure if it’s been kidnapped or rescued. Marionette assures the little creature it will be “trained as a thief.”
- She delivers the recovered ledgers and silver vial to Ricard’s office, leaving them with a simple note — and keeps Shroom as her newest, most unusual partner in crime.

### Ongoing Threads
- **Ricard Ruanad’s Deal** – The sly professor’s “lost item” has been recovered, but his motives — and the purpose of the silver vial — remain unclear.
- **The Root Archives** – Once again disturbed, the archives now contain a lurking water-creature sealed beneath frost. How long that seal will hold is anyone’s guess.
- **Financial Ledgers** – The ledgers bearing Ricard’s name could prove valuable — or dangerous — should they ever resurface.
- **Shroom** – A timid, sentient fungus now in Marionette’s care, eager (or terrified) to learn the ways of thievery.
- **The Campus Network** – The thieves’ cant marking suggests a hidden undercurrent of illicit trade and favours running through Neverwood’s halls — and Marionette has just joined its ranks.
