## Summary
After recovering from their earlier battles, the students continue their exploration of the catacombs beneath [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]. Their search leads them through ancient tombs tied to the Hallwinter family, where they encounter a lost spirit and ultimately help restore her identity.
With new information about cult activity and dangers ahead, the party presses deeper, battling undead and uncovering a sealed passage that leads further underground. Exhausted, they choose to rest before continuing.

### Back Into the Catacombs
* After resting in the tunnel, the students:
	* Regain their strength
	* Continue exploring the catacombs beneath [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]
* They pass through a **secret door**:
	* Entering a tomb chamber
	* Begin searching the area

### The Hallwinter Tomb
* The party discovers a sarcophagus:
	* Carved with **two women gazing over a city**
	* Marked with a **family crest**
* [[1-Party/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] recognises the crest:
	* It belongs to the **Hallwinter family**
* [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis]] finds:
	* A shield bearing the same crest
* Eremis, with help from [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]]:
	* Opens the sarcophagus
	* Prods the skeletal remains to confirm they are truly dead

### The Forgotten Spirit
* The party enters a **rotunda**:
	* They encounter a ghostly humanoid in plate armour
	* Its head is replaced by a **featureless, glowing orb**
* Attempting conversation:
	* The spirit has **no memory of its identity**
	* It pleads with the party for help
* The party agrees to assist:
	* Then continues exploring

### The Second Tomb
* In another chamber, the party finds:
	* A second sarcophagus
	* Carvings of the same two women, now **exploring a forest**
	* Several **arcane runes**
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] and [[1-Party/Milfjord Goodleaf/Milfjord Goodleaf\|Milfjord]] search the room:
	* They find:
		* A **gold locket**
		* A **spell scroll**
	    * Several sticks of **incense**
* Milfjord attempts to smoke the incense
* Harry opens the locket (after some difficulty):
	* Inside is:
	    * A portrait of a woman
	    * The inscription: *“My dearest Chanelle”*
* Eremis once again:
	* Opens the sarcophagus
	* Prods the remains

### Reuniting the Dead
* [[1-Party/Milfjord Goodleaf/Milfjord Goodleaf\|Milfjord]]:
	* Removes the skeletal remains from the sarcophagus
	* Carries them back to the first tomb
* He places the remains together:
	* Arranging them in a **loving embrace**
* The party pieces things together:
	* They return to the spirit

### Chanelle Hallwinter
* The party asks if the spirit is [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/Chanelle Hallwinter\|Chanelle Hallwinter]]
* Upon hearing her name:
	* The spirit transforms
	* Her form becomes that of a **woman with a restored face**
* Chanelle:
	* Thanks the party
	* Shares what she knows

**Key Information:**
* **Grey-cloaked figures** have passed through recently
* One chamber ahead has been **overrun by zombies**
* The party bids her farewell:
	* And continues onward

### The Zombie Crypt
* The party enters the warned chamber:
	* Confronts a **horde of zombies**
* The fight is efficient:
	* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] uses *Moonbeam* to great effect
	* [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]] cuts down enemies with his greataxe
* After clearing the room:
	* They discover it is a **crypt containing a shrine to Oghma**

### Restoring the Shrine
* The shrine is in disrepair
* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]], [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]] and [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis]]:
	* Clean and restore it
	* Rededicate it to **Oghma**
* After doing so:
	* They feel the **source of the undead disturbance has been resolved**

### The Sealed Door
* Continuing deeper, the party finds:
	* A large **double door of green-tinged stone**
* Opposite the door:
	* A carving of a **grinning skull**
	* One socket holds a jewelled eye
	* The other is empty
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]]:
	* Places the desecrated eyeball from [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/Delvin Fearnehart\|Delvin Fearnehart]] into the empty socket
* Result:
	* A hidden mechanism triggers
	* A **staircase opens behind them**, leading downward

### End of Session
* [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis]], tired and hungry, suggests resting
* The party:
	* Returns to the crypt
	* Sets up camp
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]]:
	* Casts an **alarm spell** for safety
* The group:
	* Cooks food
	* Rests for the night
