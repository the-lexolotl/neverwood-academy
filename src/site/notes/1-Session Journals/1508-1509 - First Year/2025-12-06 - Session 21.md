## Summary
A quiet afternoon of research in the Biblioplex spirals into botanical body horror, campus-wide theft, and yet another escalating crisis involving magic beans. What begins as idle curiosity ends with Glue growing a beanstalk out of his mouth, medical staff wrestling plant matter from a screaming student, and the party ultimately unleashing a second beanstalk that tears through Kollema Hall and carries them screaming into the sky — setting the stage for consequences far beyond Neverwood’s walls.

### Research in the Biblioplex
* After classes, the students meet in the [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Biblioplex\|Biblioplex]] to study.
* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] continues researching truth spells and magic of candour.
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] searches for information on the **Star of Remembrance**.
* [[1-Party/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] checks out a tome on healing magic.
* [[1-Party/Guiloip Guhloo/Guiloip Guhloo\|Glue]] stacks books into a pile and begins *nesting*.
* [[1-Party/Marionette Moonbeam/Marionette Moonbeam\|Marionette]] spends time pickpocketing students across campus and returns with:
	* An all-you-can-eat voucher for [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Bow's End Tavern\|Bow's End Tavern]]
	* Some Coin
	* **Three small beans**

### The First Bean
* Curious, Marionette asks Glue if he knows what the beans are.
* Without looking up from his book, **Glue swallows one**.
* Almost immediately, Glue feels something rising in his throat.
* As he opens his mouth to speak, the **tip of a beanstalk erupts from his mouth**.
* Marionette screams, drawing the attention of **Professor [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Witherbloom/Tivash\|Tivash]]**.
* Seeing the situation, Tivash orders a nearby **Cogwork Archivist** to take Glue to the medical bay.
* The automaton scoops Glue up and rushes him to the medical wing of [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Kollema Hall\|Kollema Hall]], with Marionette following.

### Medical Horror
* Glue is taken straight into a procedure room as staff attempt to remove the still-growing beanstalk.
* Hearing alarming noises, Marionette rushes inside.
* She witnesses multiple medical staff pulling the beanstalk from Glue’s mouth, his jaw stretched at an inhuman angle — and promptly faints.
* The staff successfully remove the beanstalk and seal it inside a **large metal box** for disposal.
* Marionette is discharged later that evening.
* Glue remains overnight for observation and is released the following morning.

### Regrouping & a Bad Idea
* The next morning, the students regroup in the first-year common room.
* They exchange updates, commiserate with Glue, and debrief the incident.
* Glue expresses curiosity about the remaining beans and suggests planting them in [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/The Cultivarium\|The Cultivarium]].
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]], [[1-Party/Marionette Moonbeam/Marionette Moonbeam\|Marionette]], and [[1-Party/Atudark/Atudark\|Atudark]] agree to go with him.
* The rest of the party stays behind to study.

### Experiments in the Cultivarium
* Upon arriving at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/The Cultivarium\|The Cultivarium]]:
	* Glue and [[1-Party/Marionette Moonbeam/Shroom\|Shroom]] dig a hole.
* Harry asks to examine one of the beans.
	* Marionette hands one over.
	* Harry sits cross-legged in the dirt, chanting as he attempts to [[3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/identify-xphb\|Identify]] it.
* Growing impatient, Glue buries the **second bean** and waters it with sparkling water from [[2-World/Businesses/Whalebucks Coffee Co.\|Whalebucks]].
* Seconds later, **eight frogs crawl out of the soil**.
	* Shroom gleefully pokes them.
	* Each frog transforms into a different small creature.
	* One tries to lick Harry mid-spell — Marionette yanks him out of the way.
* Moments later, all the creatures vanish in a puff of **pink smoke**.
* Harry fails to determine the properties of the third bean and hands it back to Glue.

### The Treant
* Glue plants and waters the **third bean**.
* A [[3-Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/plant/treant-xmm\|Treant]] erupts from the ground and immediately runs into the main greenhouse.
* The students chase it but quickly lose sight of it among the plant beds.
* Marionette eventually spots the treant and chases it outside.
* She snaps her whip around one of its limbs, tripping it.
* Glue rushes forward, begging Marionette not to hurt his “son.”
* Marionette makes the treant swear:
	* To stay hidden
	* To cause no trouble
* The treant reluctantly agrees and is released.

### Back to the Medical Wing
* Marionette and Glue suggest checking whether the original beanstalk is still in the medical bay.
* Needing a reason to return, Marionette feeds Glue a magical plant from the Cultivarium.
* Glue immediately develops **orange stripes**.
* The group rushes to the medical wing in [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Kollema Hall\|Kollema Hall]].
* The bay is quiet, staffed only by a single automaton.
* The automaton treats Glue, injecting an antidote for the plant toxin.

### The Second Beanstalk
* While Glue is treated, Marionette, Harry, and Atudark sneak into the rear procedure room.
* They find a **large metal box**.
	* Too heavy to move, they remove the lid instead.
* Instantly, a massive **still-growing beanstalk** erupts from the box.
* The stalk smashes through the ceiling, continuing to grow upward.
* Hearing the crash, Glue and the automaton rush toward the noise.
* Glue sticks out his leg, tripping the medical bot and sending it crashing to the floor.
* In the procedure room:
	* The beanstalk is tearing through the roof.
	* Marionette clings to it, riding it upward.
* She shouts for the others to jump on before they’re caught.
* **Glue, Harry, and Atudark leap onto the beanstalk**, climbing aboard as it bursts out of the building and carries them high into the sky.

### Ongoing Threads
* **The Magic Beans** – Unstable, unpredictable, and incredibly dangerous. Where did they come from, and are there more?
* **The Runaway Treant** – A sentient plant-creature now loose in the Cultivarium, bound only by a promise.
* **Medical Staff Suspicion** – A second beanstalk incident in Kollema Hall is unlikely to go unnoticed.
* **Lyra’s Ongoing Research** – Her work into truth magic continues in parallel, unresolved and troubling.
* **The Rising Beanstalk** – The party is now literally leaving Neverwood behind… and no one knows where the beanstalk leads next.
