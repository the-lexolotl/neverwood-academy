## Summary
A quiet day of homestudy turns into a deepening mystery as the students reconvene in the Biblioplex to unravel the secrets of the **Pub That Crawls**. Old rumours resurface, maps go astray, and animated books take offence at Suraax’s curiosity. The discovery of a hidden notebook — and the revelation of heat-reactive secrets within — gives the party their first concrete sequence of pubs. As plans solidify, concerns grow: each stop may bring its own trials, and time is not on their side. By night’s end, a risky solution presents itself — a teleporter, a shopkeeper, and a fixed date that will launch the crawl in earnest.

### Homestudy at the Biblioplex
* The students are given a day dedicated to **homestudy**.
* They meet in the [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Biblioplex\|Biblioplex]] to catch up and investigate notes found on the [[2-Player Handouts/Others/Neverwinter Pubs, Inns & Taverns\|Neverwinter Pubs, Inns & Taverns]] leaflet.
* Lyra and Cyinth discuss the leaflet’s sketchy markings.
* Suraax mentions she has also heard **rumours of a mysterious pub** in Neverwinter.
* Cyinth sends her **Homunculus Servant** to fetch a map of Neverwinter.
	* The servant returns with a **map of Candlekeep instead**, having failed its task.
* Cyinth asks [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/General/Isabrough\|Isabrough]] for assistance and retrieves the correct map.

### Maps, Flyers, and First Clues
* While waiting for Cyinth to return, Lyra notices a flyer advertising **Grifft**, a new griffin taxi service operating in and around Neverwinter.
	* She removes the flyer and tucks it into her bag for later.
* With the Neverwinter map on the table, the party begins **plotting points** from the leaflet sketch.
* Suraax searches the shelves for additional reference material.

### The Animated Book Incident
* While searching, Suraax is **attacked by a swarm of animated books**.
* She swiftly dispatches them with her axe.
* Among the remains, she notices a book titled:
  **[[2-Player Handouts/Others/Notes on The Pub that Crawls Compiled by E. Zenithon.\|Notes on The Pub that Crawls Compiled by E. Zenithon.]]**
* Recognising its importance, Suraax brings the book back to the table.
* The group begins deciphering the cryptic notes inside.

### Heat and Hidden Text
* A **blank page** in the notebook draws the party’s attention.
* Closer inspection reveals a **small symbol** in the corner.
* The group deduces the symbol indicates the page should be **heated**.
* Wanting to avoid trouble in the Biblioplex, Cyinth suggests relocating to her dorm room.

### Mead, Music, and Revelation
* In Cyinth’s dorm, the group shares some mead.
	* This is **Suraax’s first ever drink**, and it hits her particularly hard.
* Once settled, Lyra applies heat to the blank pages.
* Hidden text is revealed, reading:
  > *“Sequence matters. Wandering precedes reflection.
  > Falsehood precedes drink.
  > Drink precedes Dreaming.
  > Dreaming precedes meandering.”*
* After some debate — and Suraax singing **“The Old Crawl Song”** she found tucked into the back of the notebook — the party determines the correct order of the pubs:
	  1. [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Tall Tale\|The Tall Tale]]
	  2. [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Frothing Flask\|The Frothing Flask]]
	  3. [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Lucid Dreamer\|The Lucid Dreamer]]
	  4. [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Rambling Toad\|The Rambling Toad]]
	  5. [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Beauty’s Mirror\|The Beauty’s Mirror]]

### Planning the Crawl
* Cyinth consults her copy of the **[[2-Player Handouts/Others/Folly Guide - Neverwinter\|Folly Guide - Neverwinter]]**.
* Lyra reads the reviews aloud and voices concern:
	* Each pub appears to present its **own unique challenge**.
	* Not all of them may be safe — or suitable — for every party member.
* The group realises that travelling to Neverwinter on foot would take **several days**, causing them to miss too much class.
* They explore alternative travel options:
	* Flying and mundane transport are ruled out.
	* Someone suggests campus **teleportation pads** might connect off-site.

### Securing the Teleporter
* Acting on this idea, Suraax runs off to find [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Administration/Zoei\|Zoei]].
* Zoei explains that the **student shop** has a teleporter that can sometimes be used, if the shopkeeper is persuaded.
* Suraax heads straight to the student shop.
* With Lyra’s help — and an exchange of coin — the group secures access to the teleporter for the **15th of Tarsakh**.
* Exhausted from planning, drinking, and revelations, the party calls it a night.

### Ongoing Threads
* **The Pub That Crawls** – The sequence is known, but each stop promises unique trials and dangers.
* **E. Zenithon’s Notes** – Who compiled them, and how much more remains hidden?
* **Grifft Flyer** – A griffin taxi service may offer alternatives — or complications — in Neverwinter.
* **Teleportation Access** – The student shop teleporter provides fast travel, but may come with strings attached.
* **Suraax and Mead** – Her first drink was memorable… and may not be her last.
