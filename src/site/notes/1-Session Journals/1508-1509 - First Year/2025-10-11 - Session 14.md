## Summary
A lesson in magical zoology takes a fiery turn when the students face off against a Lesser Celestial Chick in their *A is for Ankheg* practical. Feathers, flames, and flailing ensue as the group learns that teamwork and chaos often go hand-in-hand at Neverwood. Just a few days later, the Winter Solstice Dance descends into scandal, music, and mischief when the infamous Hellhound Gang performs a song so indecent it sends one student running from the hall in tears — and sparks a new quest for the party: mending broken hearts through rock and roll.

### A is for Ankheg
* The party attends their *A is for Ankheg* practical class at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Neverwood Stadium\|Neverwood Stadium]], led by Professor [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Witherbloom/Verelda Lang\|Verelda Lang]], Professor [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Quentis Orvlo\|Quentis Orvlo]], and groundskeeper [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Yedora\|Yedora]].  
* On the way, Harry asks several classmates if they’ve seen any chickens or birds recently.  
* Glue jokes that the only bird he’s seen is Cyinth, which earns him an unimpressed look.  
* Growing impatient, Cyinth storms ahead and demands the professors hurry up. The staff tell her to sit down — which she eventually does.  
* Professor Lang explains the assignment: a group combat exercise against a *Lesser Celestial Chick*. Cyinth’s earlier outburst earns her group the first match slot.

### The Celestial Chick Battle
* Cyinth, Harry, H’er, Vigilius, Lyra, and Glue enter the pitch to face the radiant bird.  
* H’er opens with *Magic Missile*, drawing the creature’s attention.  
* Harry and Glue attempt to snare the chick, but it takes flight before they can spring the trap.  
* Lyra and Cyinth take to the skies, launching *Guiding Bolts*.  
	* Lyra’s shot hits its mark, illuminating the chick in radiant light.  
	* Cyinth’s overcharged bolt backfires, knocking her unconscious and sending her plummeting.  
* Glue dives forward to catch Cyinth mid-fall.  
* Vigilius hurls Harry onto the chick’s back in a bold move. The chick lets out a booming *cluck*, flinging Harry to the ground and knocking him out.  
* Vigilius grapples the creature, exchanging a flurry of blows. The chick retaliates with a fiery breath, but Vigilius holds on.  
* With one final strike, Vigilius defeats the celestial bird.  
* Professor [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Witherbloom/Verelda Lang\|Verelda Lang]] rushes to the field, with Lyra’s help, to heal the injured students. She praises the group’s teamwork and perseverance despite the chaos.  
* The students return to their dorms to rest and recover.

### The Winter Solstice Dance
* A few days later, the Academy hosts its annual [[2-Player Handouts/Academy Events/Winter Solstice Dance\|Winter Solstice Dance]], filling the halls with light, laughter, and music.  
* Students mingle, dance, and enjoy the festive atmosphere — until a group of 3rd years bursts in and takes the stage.  
* They introduce themselves as [[2-World/Groups/The Hellhound Gang\|The Hellhound Gang]], a notorious student band known for their risqué performances.  
* Their first song, *“The Ballad of Chasey Lain,”* sends the hall into uproarious cheers and laughter.  
* However, whispers spread as students point toward a 4th-year girl who flees the hall in tears.

### Cyinth vs. The Hellhound Gang
* Outraged by the vulgar lyrics, Cyinth storms the stage and confronts the band’s frontman, [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1506/Jimmy Pyre\|Jimmy Pyre]].  
* She attempts to slash him with her talons — Jimmy dodges, and she instead tears his shirt, exposing his chest to the crowd’s delight.  
* Jimmy tells her to step off stage, promising to talk later, before launching into their next song: *“The Bad Touch.”*  
* Watching the chaos, Glue uses his magic to make Jimmy wet himself mid-performance.  
* Unfazed, the band continues, with drummer [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1506/Infernal Houndfist\|Infernal “Jarex” Houndfist]] pulling a Neverwood flag from his pocket and urinating on it for dramatic effect.  
* Glue retaliates again, magically forcing the rest of the band to soak themselves as the crowd roars with approval.  
* After the set, Jimmy explains to Cyinth that the song was meant as a love confession to [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1505/Chaselienne Lain\|Chaselienne "Chasey" Lain]], a fourth-year student he’s been infatuated with.  
* Cyinth calls him disgusting and tears his trousers in anger, leaving part of his muscular thigh exposed. The band awkwardly tries to defend him.

### The Search for Chasey
* Concerned for [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1505/Chaselienne Lain\|Chasey Lain]], Harry and Lyra leave the dance to search for her.  
* Outside, Harry uses *Detect Magic* and spots a trail of illusion magic leading toward [[2-World/Places/Silverquill Campus\|Silverquill Campus]].  
* Glue questions a Campus Guide Automaton, confirming that Chasey passed by recently.  
* The party follows the trail to [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Grandloft Hall\|Grandloft Hall]], where they find the [[2-World/Businesses/Whalebucks Coffee Co.\|Whalebucks]] closed.  
* Some of the group help themselves to coffee, leaving coins on the counter in apology.  
* Lyra eventually finds Chasey crying in a storage cupboard. H’er joins her to comfort the distraught student.  
* Chasey confides that she actually has a crush on Jimmy and was initially thrilled to hear he’d written her a song — until she heard how vulgar it was, and how public the performance became.  
* Though embarrassed, she admits she understands it’s his “musical style,” and only wishes she’d heard it privately first.  
* She asks the party to convince Jimmy to apologise, suggesting he make a grand romantic gesture.  
* The group agrees to help mend the rift between the two students.
