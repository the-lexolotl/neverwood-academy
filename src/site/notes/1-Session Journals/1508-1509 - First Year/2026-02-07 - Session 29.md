## Summary
Tensions flare and voices echo through the ankheg tunnels — and the hive answers. What begins as shouting across stone corridors escalates into a brutal confrontation with a massive ankheg that splits the party in two. Steel is shattered, skeletons rise, and teamwork (barely) prevails. With their objective deemed “sufficiently complete,” the students return to the surface — some triumphant, some mourning damaged constructs, and some immediately making questionable post-combat lifestyle choices.

### Rumbling in the Dark
- The students remain spread throughout the tunnels.
- [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] and [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]] begin shouting at one another from opposite ends of the cavern network.
- The raised voices echo through the stone — followed by a low, ominous rumble.
- [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] urges the group to retreat before something worse happens.
- As the party attempts to regroup and head for the entrance, the ground **erupts**.

### The Hive Responds
- A **massive ankheg** bursts up through the earth, splitting the tunnel and trapping the party on opposite sides of a wall of debris.
- The creature seizes Suraax in its mandibles.
- [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/T.1.T.4.N\|T.1.T.4.N]] attempts to strike the ankheg — only further enraging it.
- The ankheg:
    - Hurls Suraax to the ground.
    - Bites into T.1.T.4.N and tears the steel defender in two.

### Desperate Measures
- Lyra keeps her distance, attacking with **Spiritual Weapon** from relative safety.
- [[1-Party/Archived/Atudark/Atudark\|Atudark]] raises **three skeletons** from the earth, forming a grim barrier between himself and the creature.
- Suraax regains her footing and hacks at the ankheg with her axe.
- Atudark commands his skeletons to press the attack.
- Despite the chaos — and the loss of T.1.T.4.N’s body — the party eventually brings the creature down.

### Aftermath Underground
- [[1-Party/Archived/Cyinth Agora/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]] rushes to the remains of T.1.T.4.N.
    - She salvages the power core and critical components needed for reconstruction.
- [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1506/Calypso Virellis\|Calypso Virellis]] — who has been standing at the ready, prepared to intervene if necessary — congratulates the students on their combat effectiveness.
- She declares the exercise complete:
    - While they did not locate the **claustral chamber** or destroy ankheg eggs,
    - Their efforts will likely satisfy Academy expectations.
- Calypso escorts the students out of the tunnels and instructs them to clean up before their next lesson.

### Debrief in the Common Room
- The students return to the first-year common room and compare stories with their peers.
- [[1-Party/Milfjord Goodleaf/Milfjord Goodleaf\|Milfjord]] offers Suraax his pipe.
- She hesitates — then accepts.
- After a few draws:
    - Suraax begins giggling uncontrollably.
    - Atudark joins her.
- Meanwhile:
    - Cyinth and Lyra decide to skip their next class to spend time playing with the dormitory cats.
    - The rest of the group heads onward to their scheduled lessons.
- Suraax and Atudark struggle — unsuccessfully — to compose themselves.

## Ongoing Threads
- **T.1.T.4.N Rebuild** – Cyinth has salvaged the power core, but reconstruction will require time, materials, and skill.
- **The Claustral Chamber** – The egg chamber remains undiscovered. The ankheg problem may not be fully resolved.
- **Tunnel Acoustics (Again)** – The party’s shouting directly triggered a hive response.
- **Calypso’s Assessment** – How much did the third-year truly observe? And what report will she give Professor Lang?
- **Substances & Exhaustion** – Recreational choices after combat may carry mechanical or academic consequences.
- **Hive Awareness** – A large, aggressive ankheg responded with precision. Was it random… or defensive leadership?
