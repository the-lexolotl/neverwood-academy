## Summary
The students descend into the lower levels of the catacombs and uncover a hidden cult temple. While investigating, they discover evidence of a far-reaching cult network dedicated to a mysterious figure known as **“[[2-World/People/Neverwinter/The Whispered One\|The Whispered One]]”**, before interrupting a dark ritual led by [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/Zalryr\|Zalryr]].
A chaotic battle follows as the party attempts to subdue the cultists alive. In the aftermath, a terrifying apparition manifests, marking the party as targets of a greater power. With their prisoners secured and strange magical charms in hand, the students return to [[2-World/Groups/Wintershield Watchmen\|The Wintershield Watchmen]].

### Descent Into the Temple
* The students awaken early:
	* [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis]] prepares breakfast
	* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] conducts a final search of the upper catacombs
* They descend into the lower level:
	* Entering a **temple-like chamber**
* At the far end:
	* A large carved hand holding an eyeball rests on a raised dais
* Eremis attempts to remove the eyeball:
	* It does not budge
	* [[1-Party/Milfjord Goodleaf/Milfjord Goodleaf\|Milfjord]] warns the group not to tamper with the structure

### Signs of the Cult
* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] scouts ahead:
	* Spots a **grey-robed cultist**
	* Hears multiple voices nearby
* The party chooses caution:
	* Avoids immediate confrontation
	* Moves in the opposite direction

### The Cult Library
* The group enters a large library:
	* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] searches the shelves
	* Finds a **small lockbox**
* On a central table:
	* Notebooks and torn pages detail:
	    * Experiments and movements by [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/Zalryr\|Zalryr]]
	    * A **wider cult network spanning [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]] and beyond**
	    * References to a leader/deity known as **“The Whispered One”**
* The writings suggest:
	* Zalryr is part of a **much larger organised structure**, not an isolated cell
* Attempting to open the lockbox:
	* [[1-Party/Milfjord Goodleaf/Milfjord Goodleaf\|Milfjord]] triggers a trap and is sprayed with liquid
* [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]]:
	* Smashes the box open with his greataxe
* Result:
	* More liquid spills out
	* Milfjord identifies it as **healing potion residue**
	* At least two potions were contained inside

### The Ritual Chamber
* Hearing voices ahead, [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis]] sneaks forward:
	* Enters a cavern filled with **sickly green light and whispers**
* She observes:
	* Zalryr leading a ritual
	* Two cultists within a glowing runic circle
* Zalryr commands:
	* “Release your secrets… become stronger.”
* The cultists transform:
	* Their bodies warp into **shadowy entities**

### The Fight Begins
* [[1-Party/Milfjord Goodleaf/Milfjord Goodleaf\|Milfjord]] wildshapes into a giant centipede:
	* Moves into the chamber
* Eremis casts **Zone of Silence**:
	* Attempting to disrupt the ritual
* [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]] charges in:
	* Greataxe swinging
	* Rage boiling
* Party objective:
	* Subdue cultists alive for [[2-World/Groups/Wintershield Watchmen\|The Wintershield Watchmen]]

### Chaos in the Catacombs
* One cultist escapes into the tunnels:
	* Alerting unseen allies
* Reinforcements begin to move through the area
* The party remains partially unaware due to the silence field
* Key actions:
	* [[1-Party/Vigilius Palamas/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] knocks out a cultist
	* Milfjord pursues fleeing enemies:
	    * Discovers additional cultists deeper in the tunnels
	    * Attempts to herd them into [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]]’s *Moonbeam*
* Cultists attempt to flank through side passages

### Breaking the Ritual
* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] investigates the runic circle:
	* Determines energy is channelled through surrounding pillars
* He directs the party:
	* Destroy the floor and destabilise the structure
* [[1-Party/Eremis Varr/Eremis Varr\|Eremis]], Lyra, and Harry:
	* Crack the stone beneath the ritual circle
* Consequence:
	* A beam of unstable energy erupts from above
	* Injuring several party members

### The Apparition
* As the final cultist falls unconscious:
	* The ritual collapses
* Shadowy smoke rises:
	* Forming an **emaciated skull with a single glowing green eye**
* The entity speaks:
	* Addressing Zalryr
	* Then turning its attention to the party

> “You have brought me new… points of interest… I have my eye on you.”

* The apparition:
	* Passes through each party member with a whispered scream
	* Explodes into shadow and vanishes

### Aftermath and Return to the Watchmen
* The party secures the unconscious cultists and returns them to [[2-World/Groups/Wintershield Watchmen\|The Wintershield Watchmen]]
* Upon debrief, they are paid for the successful operation:
	* **100gp each** for completing the assignment
	* An additional **140gp total**, split between them
	    * (Payment of **20gp per cultist returned alive**)
* The Watchmen confirm:
	* The cultist captures are considered a success
	* The intelligence gathered from the catacombs is of high importance
* The mysterious charms recovered from the apparition are retained by the party for further study
