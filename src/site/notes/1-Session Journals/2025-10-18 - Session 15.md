## Summary
After the chaos of recent weeks, the group attempts to mend the rift between Jimmy Prye and Chasey Lane. What begins as a well-meaning plan for reconciliation quickly spirals into arguments, romance, and a public altercation that lands several students back in the Head Teacher’s office. Meanwhile, a new student, Vith Pahlok, makes his presence loudly known — and manages to make several enemies before the day is over.

### The Biblioplex Meeting
* The party meets **[[2-World/People/Students/1506/Jimmy Pyre\|Jimmy Pyre]]** in the Biblioplex to discuss how he might make things right with **[[2-World/People/Students/1505/Chaselienne Lain\|Chaselienne "Chasey" Lain]]**.  
* After a fair amount of bickering, the group begins forming a plan to help Jimmy apologise properly.  
* Their conversation is interrupted by **[[1-Party/Group 3/Vith Pahlok\|Vith Pahlok]]**, the new student, who butts in confidently, claiming he knows everything about wooing women.  
* Vith begins bragging about his supposed fame and his father’s wealth — until **[[1-Party/Group 2/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]]** swiftly cuts him down.  
	* She points out that she’s on the **Neverwood [[2-World/Groups/Extracurricular Club/Mage Tower Cheer Squad\|Mage Tower Cheer Squad]]** and has *never once* seen him play.  
	* Lyra also makes a point of noting her own family’s wealth, leaving Vith visibly deflated.

### Secret Affairs and Side Quests
* While the others argue, **[[1-Party/Group 2/H'er\|H'er]]** slips away to the artifact storage cupboard to make out with **[[2-World/People/Students/1508/Elandra Stormwhisper\|Elandra Stormwhisper]]**.  
* **[[1-Party/Group 1/Guiloip Guhloo\|Glue]]**, meanwhile, tries his luck flirting with the Head Librarian, **[[2-World/People/Staff/Isabrough\|Isabrough]]**, eventually convincing her to meet him for dinner at **[[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Bow's End Tavern\|Bow's End Tavern]]** around 9 p.m.

### Flowers for Forgiveness
* The group finally agrees on a plan — Jimmy should give Chasey flowers as part of his apology.  
* They travel to **Quandrix’s [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/The Cultivarium\|Cultivarium]]** to find something suitable.  
* **[[1-Party/Group 2/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]]** casts *Detect Magic* to ensure the flowers they pick are safe (and non-lethal).  
* The party collects several bouquets — some for Jimmy, others for their own loved ones — and returns to the Biblioplex.  
* **Harry** hands Jimmy his bouquet and offers some gentle advice on how to talk to women.  
* Jimmy thanks him but grumbles in disappointment that the flowers aren’t carnivorous or capable of breathing fire.

### The Confession Gone Wrong
* **[[2-World/People/Students/1505/Chaselienne Lain\|Chaselienne "Chasey" Lain]]** enters the Biblioplex. Jimmy rushes to meet her, clutching his flowers and rehearsing his apology.  
* Just as things begin to go well, **Vith** appears — and immediately *charms Chasey in front of Jimmy*, attempting to steal her away.  
* The rest of the group rushes over to intervene, trying to escort Chasey and Jimmy away before things escalate.  
* A fight breaks out in the middle of the Biblioplex.  
* **Professor [[2-World/People/Staff/Lorehold/Osgir, the Reconstructor\|Osgir, the Reconstructor]]** arrives swiftly, breaks up the altercation, and drags everyone involved to the **Head Teacher’s Office**.

### The Head Teacher’s Office
* **Head Teacher [[2-World/People/Staff/Taiva Loreweaver\|Taiva Loreweaver]]** questions the group about both the fight *and* the earlier break-in to the student records room.  
* **Vigilius**, previously interrogated by **[[2-World/People/Staff/Silverquill/Mavinda Sharpbeak\|Professor Sharpbeak]]** and **Deputy Head [[2-World/People/Staff/Ricard Ruanad\|Ricard Ruanad]]**, insists his story — that everyone was involved — is the truth.  
* **[[1-Party/Group 2/Cyinth Agora\|Cyinth]]** counters, claiming that only she and [[1-Party/Group 1/Vigilius Palamas\|Vigilius]] were responsible. The rest of the group deny all involvement.  
* Taiva pushes for confessions but no one cracks.  
* Given Vigilius’ existing **academic probation** and Cyinth’s extensive disciplinary record — damage to school property, forge facilities, possession of contraband, and general insubordination — Taiva passes judgement:  
	* **Vigilius** receives an *additional week of probation*.  
	* **Cyinth** is *suspended for three tendays*.

### Consequences
* The Head Teacher dismisses all students *except* **H’er** and **Vith**.  
* **Lyra** remains behind long enough to argue passionately about the unfairness of the punishments but gets nowhere.  
* **Vith** is sternly reprimanded for his conduct since arriving on campus — he is **banned from the next Mage Tower game** and given **three weeks of detention**.  
* **H’er** receives a quieter warning about her *“public displays of affection.”* Embarrassed, she blushes and leaves quickly.  
* The party returns to their dorms — some to wallow, some to reflect, and others to begin packing for the long suspension ahead.

### Ongoing Threads
* **Chasey & Jimmy’s Relationship** – Though the apology didn’t go to plan, the spark between them may yet survive — if Vith keeps out of it.  
* **Vith’s Behaviour** – His arrogance and interference have already made him enemies and earned the Head Teacher’s attention.  
* **Cyinth’s Suspension** – With three tendays away from campus, what will she get up to — and will she even come back?  
* **Vigilius’ Probation** – Another week added to his record puts him one step closer to expulsion.  
* **H’er & Elandra’s Secret** – Their relationship is now on the staff’s radar — can they keep it quiet next time?  
* **Glue & Isabrough’s Dinner Date** – The librarian actually said yes. What could possibly go wrong?
