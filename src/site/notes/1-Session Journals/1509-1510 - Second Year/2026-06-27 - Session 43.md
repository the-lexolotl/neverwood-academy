## Summary
What begins as an intriguing guest lecture quickly spirals into mechanical chaos as second-year students journey into Neverwinter Woods to attend a class hosted by Professor Manthrak. Signs of disturbance in the forest foreshadow disaster, and upon arrival, the students find his workshop in ruins and a rogue construct tearing it apart. After surviving the encounter, they uncover clues pointing to a greater catastrophe - one that leads them up the hill to a towering, out-of-control structure rolling endlessly across the landscape. Tasked with fixing the situation, the students risk life and limb to enter the rotating tower and stabilise its volatile core, proving their resilience in the face of unpredictable magical experimentation.

### Journey to Manthrak’s Tower
* All second-year students are invited to attend a guest lecture by [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/General/Manthrak\|Professor Manthrak]].
* The class is set to take place at **[[2-World/Points of Interest/Manthrak's Tower\|Manthrak's Tower]]**, located in [[2-World/Places/Neverwinter Woods\|Neverwinter Woods]].
* Students prepare accordingly:
	* Sensible clothing.
	* A hearty breakfast before departure.
* As they travel through the woods, they notice:
	* The **animals are unusually spooked**.
	* An uneasy atmosphere settles over the group.
* Despite the tension, the students continue toward the tower.

### The Ruined Workshop
* As they ascend the hill, a **deep, rumbling noise** becomes audible.
* The students discover Manthrak’s external workshop:
	* A small wooden shack in **complete disarray**.
	* The door hangs off its hinges.
	* Debris is scattered across the ground.
	* The sounds of destruction echo from within.
* Entering the shack, the students encounter:
	* A **large construct** made of mismatched mechanical parts.
	* The creature is actively smashing the workshop.

### The Construct Fight
* The party engages the rogue construct immediately.
* Tactics include:
	* Attempting to burn it.
	* Covering the floor in grease
	* Striking with [[1-Party/Redrnic Udertaken/Redrnic Udertaken\|Redrnic]]’s axe.
* The fight is chaotic, with **multiple students sustaining injuries**.
* Despite the danger, the group successfully destroys the construct.

### Clues in the Wreckage
* After the battle, [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] searches the workshop:
	* Finds various **tinkering tools**.
	* Discovers a **large, unidentified 12-inch crystal**.
* A broken chalkboard reveals a hurried message:
	* *“TOWER — CONTROL LOST — TEAPOT — DO NOT—”*
* The message suggests:
	* Professor Manthrak fled the workshop.
	* The true problem lies further up the hill.

### The Rolling Tower
* Following the rumbling, the students reach the source:
	* A **stone tower rolling endlessly on its side**.
	* Windows and doors rotate in and out of view as it turns.
	* A **venting hole** emits thick white steam.
	* The surrounding area is devastated:
		* Uprooted trees.
		* Churned earth.
* A high-pitched **keening sound** emanates from within the tower.
* Nearby, the students find [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/General/Manthrak\|Professor Manthrak]]:
	* Clearly distressed but unharmed.
	* He explains:
		* He was experimenting with a **sentient teapot**.
		* The experiment went disastrously wrong.
* He asks the students to **enter the tower and fix the problem**.

### Entering the Tower
* The students devise methods to get inside the rotating structure:
	* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] flies through the exposed opening.
	* Others carefully time their entry, risking being crushed.
* One by one, they successfully make it inside.

### The Unstable Core
* Inside the tower:
	* A large **crystal floats at the centre** of the room.
	* It emits the high-pitched keening noise.
* The tower continues rotating during the task, creating constant instability.
* The objective:
	* Turn **three valves** connected to the crystal.
* Challenges include:
	* Losing footing due to rotation.
	* Being struck by **bursts of energy** from the crystal.
	* Navigating the shifting environment.
* Notable moment:
	* [[1-Party/Harry Blackstone/Harry Blackstone\|Harry]] briefly ends up **hanging from the ceiling** during the chaos.
* Through coordination and persistence, the students:
	* Successfully turn all three valves.
	* **Stabilise and disarm the crystal**.

### Ongoing Threads
* **The Sentient Teapot** – The source of the disaster remains unclear. What exactly went wrong?
* **Professor Manthrak’s Methods** – His experiments appear highly unstable — and potentially dangerous.
* **The Crystal Core** – What was the crystal powering, and is it truly safe now?
* **Forest Disturbance** – The frightened wildlife suggests wider consequences beyond the tower.
* **Student Resilience** – The group’s ability to adapt under extreme conditions marks a significant step in their second-year development.
