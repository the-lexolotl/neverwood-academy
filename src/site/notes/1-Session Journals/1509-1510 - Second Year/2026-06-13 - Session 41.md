## Summary
The start of the second year at Neverwood Academy began with a formal campus-wide orientation at Archway Commons, where students were welcomed back and introduced to looming institutional developments. Headteacher [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Taiva Loreweaver\|Taiva Loreweaver]] addressed the student body with warnings of a temporal anomaly in the east wing of Torus Hall, alongside the announcement of the 48th Triennial [[2-Player Handouts/Neverwinter Mage Battle\|Mage Battle of Neverwinter]]. Deputy Headteacher [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Administration/Brindlewick Cogspindle\|Brindlewick Cogspindle]] followed with a more administrative introduction, confirming his availability during office hours. Students were then dispatched with fourth-year guides to tour the academy’s college common rooms, where social dynamics, minor chaos, and early recruitment discussions began to take shape.

### Archway Commons Orientation
The returning students gathered in Archway Commons for the official start of the academic year.
* [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Taiva Loreweaver\|Taiva Loreweaver]] welcomed students back to the Academy
	* Warned of a temporal anomaly in the east wing of Torus Hall
	* Announced the 48th Triennial Mage Battle of Neverwinter
* [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Administration/Brindlewick Cogspindle\|Brindlewick Cogspindle]] introduced himself as deputy headteacher
	* Noted availability during office hours for student concerns
* Students were assigned fourth-year guides and directed to tour the common rooms

### Lorehold Common Room
The group began their tour in Lorehold, where academic curiosity quickly took hold.
* Redrnic discovered a book concerning blood curses
* Brief conversation took place between Redrnic and [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1506/Orin Blackstone\|Orin Blackstone]]
* [[1-Party/H'er/H'er\|H'er]] found interest in a book on timepieces
* [[1-Party/Suraax Smith/Suraax Smith\|Suraax]] and Ferbinious became caught in an increasingly circular discussion about apples and “big reds”

### Prismari Common Room
The Prismari common room proved more chaotic and socially dynamic.
* Redrnic stole a pink cushion from the seating area
* Ferbinious struggled with the kitchen counter due to height, prompting Suraax to give him a piggyback
* H'er reconnected with [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Elandra Stormwhisper\|Elandra Stormwhisper]], discussing events from the summer break

### Quandrix Common Room
The Quandrix space was noted for its organisation and magical animal care resources.
* Students explored the facilities and observed extensive pet care equipment
* Redrnic swapped the stolen pink cushion for a green one

### Silverquill Common Room
A light-hearted theft escalated into a chaotic chase through the Silverquill common room.
* Redrnic replaced the green cushion with a blue seat cushion
* [[1-Party/Lyra Everlight/Lyra Everlight\|Lyra]] noticed and attempted to intervene, initiating a chase
* Redrnic fled upstairs, with Lyra in pursuit
* He leapt from the upper-floor balcony in an attempt to escape
	* Lyra followed using flight magic to intercept him
	* Redrnic landed head-first onto the cushion, his horns embedding into the floor beneath
	* Lyra grabbed his ankle, causing a burn injury
* Redrnic freed himself and retreated to the kitchen area
	* Used Suraax as a temporary shield
	* Suraax redirected healing magic from her axe into her fists and struck Redrnic’s ankle
* With his ankle stabilised, Redrnic escaped the common room with the blue cushion

### Witherbloom Common Room
The Witherbloom common room was marked by an overwhelming sensory environment and increasing social manipulation.
* The room was filled with smoke from magical herbs and abundant snacks
* Students experienced mild passive intoxication over time
* Redrnic joined a group of students and hid the blue cushion beneath an orange beanbag
* He attempted to recruit others into a broader cushion-swapping scheme across colleges
* Lyra joined the same group and attempted to disrupt his plan
Meanwhile, deeper in the common room:
* Suraax visited the grow room and spoke with [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Melwythorn\|Melwythorn]] and [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Mirella Blackbriar\|Mirella Blackbriar]]
	* Suraax insulted [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1508/Melwythorn\|Melwythorn]] by calling her a “plant”, then quickly apologised
	* The conversation shifted to the upcoming academic year

### Evening at Bow’s End Tavern
As evening fell, the students gathered socially at Bow’s End Tavern to unwind before classes resumed.
* The group met a fellow student, [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1507/Pyrus Flintbrand\|Pyrus Flintbrand]], who discussed participation in the Mage Battle of Neverwinter
* Informal discussions began regarding potential team formations and synergies
* Pyrus, Suraax, and Lyra discussed romantic interests
* Pyrus introduced Lyra to another student, [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1507/Ivy Drake\|Ivy Drake]]
	* Ivy spoke briefly with Lyra before offering to buy her a drink
* The group spent the remainder of the evening relaxing and socialising before the start of term

* Silverquill Chase Aftermath – Lyra’s pursuit and the balcony jump resulted in minor injuries and unresolved tension.
