## Summary
The second year begins in earnest as students return to classes, reconnect with friends, and are quickly thrown into a practical challenge designed to test far more than their academic knowledge. Under Professor Xyla Langning’s guidance, the cohort is split into teams and placed into identical pocket-dimension escape rooms, where puzzles, teamwork, and communication prove just as vital as magical ability. While not without struggle, all groups eventually succeed - earning rewards, recognition, and a clear message about the expectations for the year ahead. The day ends on a lighter note at Bow’s End Tavern, where a new rumour begins to circulate across campus.

### A New Academic Year
* Students begin their **second year** at the Academy.
* Time is spent reconnecting with friends and classmates after the summer break.
* A sense of renewed energy — and higher expectations — settles over the cohort as classes resume.

### Professor Langning’s Challenge
* At the end of the tenday, Professor [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Lorehold/Xyla Langning\|Xyla Langning]] sets the class a practical test:
	* Apply the **foundational knowledge** gained during the first tenday.
	* Complete a specially designed **escape room challenge**.
* Students are divided into groups and ushered through magical portals.
* Each group is transported into a **pocket dimension** created by Langning.

### The Escape Room Trial
* All groups arrive in identical environments:
	* A **square room** with a central table.
	* A **door on each of the four walls**.
* The objective: solve a sequence of puzzles and escape before the other teams.
* The challenge tests multiple skills:
	* Problem-solving and magical knowledge.
	* **Map reading and spatial awareness**.
	* **Communication and teamwork**.
* Many groups struggle initially, finding the puzzles more complex than expected.
* Professor Langning provides occasional hints to keep progress moving.
* After persistence and cooperation, **all groups successfully escape** unharmed.

### Rewards and Recognition
* Prizes and **Group Cup points** are awarded to the fastest teams.
* Professor [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Lorehold/Xyla Langning\|Xyla Langning]] expresses strong approval of the cohort:
	* She notes their intelligence and adaptability.
	* She shows enthusiasm for their development over the coming year.
* The exercise establishes a clear tone:
	* Second year will demand **collaboration, critical thinking, and applied skill**.

### Evening at Bow’s End
* Students spend the evening unwinding at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Bow's End Tavern\|Bow's End Tavern]].
* Conversations shift from the day’s challenge to campus gossip.
* A rumour begins to circulate:
	* A **visiting professor** is expected to arrive at the Academy soon.

## Ongoing Threads
* **The Visiting Professor** – Who are they, and what will they bring to Neverwood?
* **Second Year Expectations** – The increased difficulty and emphasis on teamwork suggest a more demanding academic year.
* **Cohort Potential** – Professor Langning’s confidence hints that this group may stand out among previous years.
* **Group Dynamics** – The escape room highlighted strengths — and weaknesses — in how students work together.
* **Pocket Dimensions in Teaching** – Langning’s methods raise questions about how far practical magical instruction will go this year.
