## Summary
After a long tenday of exams, essays, and magical exhaustion, the students of Neverwood Academy finally had a chance to breathe. They gathered at **Bow’s End Tavern** for food, laughter, and a well-earned evening of relaxation — until the kitchen quite literally boiled over.

### Session Events
* The students spent the tenday focused on **studying and completing their winter exams**, each coping with the academic stress in their own way.  
* Once exams concluded, they made their way to **Bow’s End Tavern** to unwind. Between hearty food, drinks, and friendly chatter, spirits began to lift.  
* During the meal, **Lyra** produced a mysterious leaflet titled *“[[2-Player Handouts/Neverwinter Pubs, Inns & Taverns\|Neverwinter: Pubs, Inns & Taverns]]”* — it had appeared on her desk that morning. The paper was covered in **handwritten notes and strange markings**, hinting that if one could drink all five signature drinks from the listed taverns, a *sixth, hidden pub* might be revealed.  
* As the party studied the map and speculated on its meaning, **screams erupted from the kitchen**.  
* **Chef [[2-World/People/Staff/Curtie Axechucker\|Curtie Axechucker]]** burst out, shouting about *“spicy steam”* attacking people before rushing back in, her braids flying behind her.  
* The party raced to assist, finding the kitchen in chaos — **boiling pots overflowing**, **steam hissing**, and **four steam mephits** swirling above the stoves.  
* **Glue** quickly condensed one mephit, forcing it back into its pot and freezing it solid. He then hurried to help **[[2-World/People/Students/1507/Zeraphis Duskrain\|Zeraphis "Zera" Duskrain]]**, a student chef lying unconscious on the floor.  
* **Harry** absorbed the burning heat of another mephit with **Absorb Elements**, but the creatures retaliated, scalding several party members with bursts of steam.  
* Thinking creatively, **Marionette** grabbed one of the boiling pots and plunged it into a nearby barrel of water, creating an explosion of steam that burned her hands severely. She passed out from the pain.  
* As Glue struggled to drag [[2-World/People/Students/1507/Zeraphis Duskrain\|Zeraphis]] to safety, frustration got the better of him. He unleashed a spell that **obliterated a nearby mephit**, triggering a **chain reaction** — each one exploding in turn until the kitchen was left a dripping, hissing ruin.  
* Bruised, burned, and thoroughly done with the evening’s chaos, the students decided it was best to call it a night and **return to their dorms**, hoping tomorrow would be quieter.

### Ongoing Threads
* The mysterious **Neverwinter tavern leaflet** — what is the hidden sixth pub, and why was it left for Lyra?  
* **Marionette’s injuries** may take time to heal.  
* **Zeraphis’s condition** remains uncertain after being caught in the steam attack.  
* The **pattern of magical accidents** around the students continues — coincidence, or something more deliberate?
