## Summary
After a cold, miserable night in the hunting hut the students are rescued and thrust straight into campus bureaucracy — medical checks, bed rest, and a brutal detention copying the *Compendium of Campus Conduct*. While most endure punishment, Harry and Glue slip out, assemble a ragged rescue party (including reluctant members of **[[2-World/Groups/The Hellhound Gang\|The Hellhound Gang]]**), and follow the trail into the storm. They catch the wintergeist in the forest — the **Star of Remembrance** glowing from inside the creature — and fight a chaotic, painful battle that leaves friends injured, the elemental dissipated, and the star still missing. The party ends the day sheltered in a giant oak by a kindly forest guardian named [[2-World/People/Other/Vormor\|Vormor]], who promises to guide them to the wintergeist’s lair in the morning.

### Found and Returned
* The students wake in the hunting hut cold, hungry, and stiff from the previous night.
* Voices echo through the blizzard — Neverwood staff searching the woods and calling names.
* Staff find the group and escort them back to the Academy.
* On return: medical check-ups for each student, then sent to bed to recover.

### Detention: The Compendium of Campus Conduct
* The next morning they attend detention run by **Professor [[2-World/People/Staff/Silverquill/Fain\|Fain]]** and **Construct [[V339\|V339]]**.
* Punishment: copy the *entire Compendium of Campus Conduct* by hand.
* Several party members tackle the copying head-on while **Glue** and **Harry** stage a distraction:
	* Glue and Harry create a *cloud of fog* to divert V339’s attention and slip out of the detention room.

### Glue’s Distraction (and Excess)
* Glue heads to the [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Bow's End Tavern\|Bow's End Tavern]] and drinks himself into oblivion.
* Rumours begin to spread on campus that several students are still missing out in the woods.

### Harry’s Rescue Plan
* Distrusting that staff are doing enough, **Harry** resolves to rescue the still-missing students himself.
* He recruits **Glue**, **Lyra**, and **[[1-Party/Group 1/Atudark/Atudark\|Atudark]]** for help.
	* **Lyra** refuses to go — she chooses to remain on campus, insisting it isn’t her problem.
	* **Atudark** and Glue agree to follow Harry.
* Short on numbers, Harry asks members of **The Hellhound Gang** if they’ll help “make bonfires in the forest.”
	* **[[2-World/People/Students/1506/Jimmy Pyre\|Jimmy Pyre]]** declines — putting [[2-World/People/Students/1505/Chaselienne Lain\|Chasey Lain]] first.
	* **[[2-World/People/Students/1506/Denis Dragontongue\|Denis “Bluntfang” Dragontongue]]**, **[[2-World/People/Students/1506/Infernal Houndfist\|Infernal “Jarex” Houndfist]]**, and **[[2-World/People/Students/1506/Lupus Thunderpelt\|Lupus Thunderpelt]]** agree and set off with the party.

### Preparing for the Cold
* The rescue party collects rations and cold-weather supplies from campus before heading into the woods toward the wintergeist’s last known direction.
* The blizzard bites deeper as they march; wind and snow make travel slow and dangerous.

### Lyra’s Research and Late Departure
* Meanwhile, **Lyra** spends hours in the library researching *sigils and the magic of candour*, desperate for a solution to whatever is afflicting her.
* She notices an advertisement in a local guide for a wizard offering magical services at a “fair” price, which troubles her.
* Growing concerned for her friends, Lyra eventually rushes out several hours later, hoping to fly above the treeline and catch up.

### The Wintergeist Appears
* As the party presses on, the temperature plummets; wind spirals around them.
* The wintergeist forms in front of them — the **Star of Remembrance** glowing embedded deep within its icy form.
* Combat erupts:
	* **Atudark** lunges repeatedly to retrieve the Star, only to be flung away by spiralling winds and shards of ice.
	* **Harry** adopts a strange tactic: thinking warm, comforting thoughts — which draws the creature’s attention and buys time.
	* **Glue** reaches the fallen Atudark and stabilises him with medicinals from his pouch (herbs he’d been saving), briefly reviving him.
	* Atudark is struck again by ice, sneezes a shower of glitter, then collapses unconscious in the snow.
* Injured and struggling, the Hellhound Gang members judge the fight too dangerous and attempt to retreat.
	* Harry persuades them back into the fight; they land several blows.
* The wintergeist suddenly dissipates as quickly as it formed, scattering weapons it had been carrying but leaving **no trace of the Star**.
* Harry deduces the Star must have been *intertwined* with the creature — bound into it by some form of magic.

### Aftermath & Lyra’s Arrival
* Lyra sees a burst of icy light above the treetops and flies toward it — finding the party battered and injured.
* She tends wounds and heals as best she can, then sends **The Hellhound Gang** back to the Academy.
	* She warns them that Harry was hunting a dangerous creature and that they had been misled into helping him.
* In private, Harry presses Lyra about the odd behaviour she’s shown recently.
	* Lyra brushes him off, but when she lies the sigil on her arm sears with pain — a painful tell that flares whenever she lies. 
	* Concerned, Harry probes further; Lyra deflects and ultimately convinces him to drop it (for now).

### The Oak House & Vormor
* Exhausted, injured, and chilled to the bone, the group spots a massive oak studded with little windows.
* Lyra circles and peeks into the panes — she sees a half-giant contentedly sipping a hot drink and reading a book.
* She knocks; a large, friendly man answers and invites the students in, worried they’ll freeze to death.
* He introduces himself as **[[2-World/People/Other/Vormor\|Vormor]]**, guardian of the forest.
	* Vormor feeds the party roast beef and pie, warms them, and tends to their injuries and spirits.
	* He offers to take them to the wintergeist’s lair in the morning, attempting to reassure and placate the worried students.
* The party sleeps in the house for the night. **Glue** ends up sleeping with Vormor.

### Ongoing Threads
* **The Missing Star of Remembrance** – The Star was bound to the wintergeist. Where did it go when the creature dissipated? Is it hidden, stolen, or transformed?
* **The Wintergeist’s Origin** – Why did the elemental form with the Star inside it? Who or what bound them together?
* **Lyra’s Sigil** – The sigil that causes pain when Lyra lies: what is its origin, and what secret is it connected to?
* **Harry’s Recklessness** – Harry’s refusal to trust staff and lone-wolf rescue attempts have drawn in dangerous allies and risks — how will this affect campus politics?
* **The Hellhound Gang’s Involvement** – The Gang aided briefly and returned to campus; they were lied to. Will they spread the true/false story, or become new enemies/allies?
* **Vormor, Guardian of the Forest** – A potential ally with knowledge and shelter. Can the party trust him to lead them safely to the wintergeist’s lair?
