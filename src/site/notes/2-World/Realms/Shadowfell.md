## Description
The **Shadowfell** is a dark and desaturated reflection of the [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]], existing as both an echo and a transitive realm within the wider cosmology. It mirrors the geography of the Material world, but in a diminished, decaying form, where light is muted, colour is drained, and emotion is dulled.

It is a plane defined not only by physical darkness, but by metaphysical weight—where despair, apathy, and memory shape existence as strongly as terrain.

## Cosmological Structure
### Echo Plane
The Shadowfell is one of the primary **echo planes**, alongside the [[2-World/Realms/Feywild\|Feywild]]:
* It reflects the [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]] in structure and geography
* Locations correspond loosely to their Material counterparts
* Differences arise through decay, ruin, or exaggeration of negative aspects

Where the [[2-World/Realms/Feywild\|Feywild]] amplifies life and emotion, the Shadowfell suppresses and diminishes them.

### Planar Layering
The Shadowfell is not uniform, but composed of:
* **Shadow Echo Regions** mirroring the Material world
* **Deep Shadow** areas where the connection weakens and reality becomes unstable
* Embedded demiplanes such as the [[2-World/Realms/Domains of Dread\|Domains of Dread]]

## Physical Properties
### Light and Environment
* Illumination is perpetually dim or twilight-like
* Colours appear washed out or monochrome
* The sky is often overcast or obscured

### Terrain Distortion
* Familiar landscapes appear ruined, abandoned, or eroded
* Settlements may exist as empty shells or corrupted versions
* Natural features are often exaggerated toward bleakness

### Emotional Influence
Creatures within the Shadowfell are affected by:
* Apathy and emotional numbness
* Loss of motivation and hope
* Heightened despair over time

This influence is gradual but persistent, shaping behaviour and perception.

## Planar Traits
### Coexistence
The Shadowfell overlaps the [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]]:
* Travel between the two can occur at **thin points**
* Locations often correspond geographically
* Passage may be achieved through magic, portals, or natural anomalies

### Shadow Crossing
Certain locations allow easier transition:
* Areas of death, ruin, or strong negative emotion
* Ancient battlefields or abandoned cities
* Magical sites tied to shadow energy

### Magical Effects
* Necromantic magic is often enhanced
* Radiant effects are diminished or suppressed
* Illusions and shadow-based magic are more potent

## Inhabitants
The Shadowfell is inhabited by a mixture of:
* Native shadow entities
* Undead creatures
* Mortals trapped or residing within shadowed settlements

### Native Presence
Creatures of the Shadowfell are typically:
* Tied to shadow or darkness
* Emotionally detached or predatory
* Adapted to the plane’s dim environment

### Mortal Settlements
Some settlements persist within the Shadowfell:
* Often located at stable planar intersections
* Inhabitants may be long-term residents or trapped travellers
* Culture tends toward survivalism and emotional detachment

## Notable Locations
### [[2-World/Realms/Domains of Dread\|Domains of Dread]]
A cluster of demiplanes embedded within the Shadowfell, each ruled by a Darklord and governed by the **Mists**.

### Shadowed Echoes
Reflections of major [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]] locations:
* Cities appear as ruins or haunted remnants
* Natural landmarks are distorted and desolate

### Deep Shadow Regions
Areas where:
* The connection to the Material Plane weakens
* Geography becomes unstable
* Reality behaves unpredictably

## Interaction with Other Planes
### The [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]]
* Closely linked through coexistent geography
* Most common point of planar interaction

### The [[2-World/Realms/Feywild\|Feywild]]
* Opposite echo plane in cosmological balance
* Represents life, light, and emotional intensity

### The [[2-World/Realms/Ethereal Plane\|Ethereal Plane]]
* Indirect interaction through planar boundaries
* Serves as an alternative transitive route

## Planar Travel
### Entry
Access to the Shadowfell is possible through:
* Shadow crossings at thin locations
* Magical spells and rituals
* Planar portals or artefacts

### Movement Within the Plane
* Travel mirrors Material geography but may be distorted
* Distances can be inconsistent
* Landmarks may shift subtly over time

### Exit
Leaving the Shadowfell:
* Typically requires retracing entry points
* May be hindered by disorientation or planar interference
* Magical means are often required

## Cosmological Role
The Shadowfell serves as:
* A **reflection of mortality, decay, and entropy**
* A **balance to the vitality of the [[2-World/Realms/Feywild\|Feywild]]**
* A **transitive layer** connecting to deeper planar structures

It is both a place and a condition of existence, embodying the inevitable decline of all things.

## Variations by World
While consistent across most cosmologies:
* The Shadowfell’s intensity varies depending on proximity to strong emotions or events
* Some worlds exhibit stronger or weaker connections
* Certain regions become stable enough to support long-term habitation

## Influence on the Material Plane
The Shadowfell exerts subtle influence on the [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]]:
* Areas of despair or death may thin the boundary
* Undead manifestations often correlate with Shadowfell proximity
* Long-term exposure can alter both land and inhabitants
