## Description
The **Domains of Dread** are a collection of isolated demiplanes bound together within a greater structure embedded in the [[2-World/Realms/Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]]. Each domain is a self-contained realm shaped by the will and curse of a singular ruler known as a **Darklord**, whose personal failings and crimes define the nature of the land.
These domains are not naturally formed planes, but rather constructed prisons, drawing fragments of worlds—primarily from the [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]] - into a closed and controlled cosmological system governed by the enigmatic **Dark Powers**.
The Domains of Dread function simultaneously as places of existence, punishment, and narrative entrapment, where reality itself reinforces cycles of horror, obsession, and despair.

## Cosmological Structure
### Demiplanar Composition
The Domains of Dread are not a single continuous plane, but a **cluster of discrete domains**, each separated by supernatural barriers formed from sentient, reactive mist.
Each domain:
* Exists independently with its own geography and internal logic
* Cannot be traversed freely without passing through the Mists
* Is governed by its own set of metaphysical rules dictated by its Darklord

### The Mists
The defining feature of the Domains:
* Act as both **barriers and conduits**
* Can isolate a domain completely or allow passage between domains
* Are capable of abducting creatures from other planes, particularly the [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]]
* Obscure distance, direction, and time

### The Dark Powers
The unseen entities responsible for the creation and maintenance of the Domains:
* Their nature is unknown and unknowable
* They select and imprison Darklords
* They manipulate reality to ensure continued suffering
* They do not communicate directly, but act through circumstance and control

## Planar Traits
### Closed Planar System
* Conventional planar travel (to the [[2-World/Realms/Astral Plane\|Astral Plane]] or other realms) is largely impossible
* Spells that would allow escape typically fail or are redirected
* Each domain can be sealed entirely by its Darklord

### Mutable Reality
* Geography, weather, and even history may shift subtly
* Events often repeat in cycles tied to the Darklord’s curse
* The environment reinforces narrative themes (tragedy, horror, obsession)

### Magical Distortion
* Divination magic is unreliable or manipulated
* Teleportation is restricted or redirected
* Necromantic and illusion-based magic often function with increased potency

## Domain Classification
Domains vary widely in structure and theme, but broadly fall into several categories:

### Gothic Domains
* Feudal structures, cursed nobility, decaying civilisations
* Example: [[2-World/Regions/Ravenloft\|Ravenloft]]

### Wilderness Domains
* Dominated by hostile environments and primal threats
* Limited civilisation

### Urban Domains
* Dense populations, intrigue, paranoia, and social decay

### Surreal Domains
* Reality behaves inconsistently
* Logic and physical laws are unreliable

## Inhabitants
### Darklords
Each domain is ruled by a single Darklord:
* Bound permanently to their domain
* Granted near-total control within it
* Unable to leave under any circumstances

Their curse typically involves:
* Eternal denial of their greatest desire
* Repetition of past failures
* Awareness of their imprisonment

### Domain Populations
* Some inhabitants are original abductees from other worlds
* Others are **domain-born**, created or altered by the Dark Powers
* Many are unaware of the true nature of their reality

## Interaction with Other Planes
### The [[2-World/Realms/Shadowfell\|Shadowfell]]
* The Domains exist within the Shadowfell but are **not freely accessible from it**
* Share its bleak tone but are more structured and controlled

### The [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]]
* Primary source of new inhabitants
* Individuals may be drawn into the Domains without warning

### Other Planes
* Interaction is rare and inconsistent
* Planar boundaries are controlled by the Dark Powers

## Planar Travel
Travel within and beyond the Domains is governed by strict rules:

### Entry
* Most commonly through involuntary abduction by the Mists
* Rarely through magical anomalies or artefacts

### Internal Travel
* Possible between domains via the Mists
* Direction and destination are not guaranteed

### Exit
* Almost universally impossible
* May occur only through:
	* Fulfilment of specific narrative conditions
	* Intervention by powerful external forces
	* Rare failures in the Dark Powers’ control

## Cosmological Role
The Domains of Dread occupy a unique position in planar theory:
* A **constructed prison system** rather than a natural plane
* A closed loop of moral consequence and narrative punishment
* A region where metaphysics are dictated by will and suffering rather than natural law

They represent a fundamental anomaly within the multiverse.
