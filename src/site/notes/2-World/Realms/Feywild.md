# Description
The Feywild is a vibrant and magical plane existing alongside the Material Plane as a heightened reflection of nature, emotion, and primal magic. Every forest, mountain, river, and ruin within the Feywild appears more vivid, dangerous, and alive than its counterpart in the mortal world.
Beauty and terror coexist throughout the Feywild, where powerful emotions and ancient magic shape reality itself.

### Overview
The Feywild is a realm of endless forests, enchanted courts, ancient ruins, and strange creatures governed by emotion, stories, bargains, and supernatural rules.
Time and distance behave unpredictably. Travellers may spend days within the Feywild only to discover years have passed upon returning home — or the reverse.
The plane is strongly associated with wonder, passion, trickery, and dangerous beauty.

## Physical Properties
The Feywild resembles the Material Plane but appears exaggerated and dreamlike.
Common features include:
* Eternal twilight skies
* Oversized flora and fauna
* Living landscapes
* Emotionally reactive environments
* Distorted time flow

Natural beauty and magical energy permeate nearly every part of the plane.

## Planar Adjacency
The Feywild closely overlaps with the Material Plane.
Crossings often appear near:
* Ancient forests
* Stone circles
* Moonlit lakes
* Fey-touched ruins
* Powerful magical sites

Certain locations become easier to cross during solstices, equinoxes, or magical events.

## Magic on the Plane
Magic in the Feywild is highly emotional and instinctive.
Common magical effects include:
* Illusions
* Enchantment magic
* Shapechanging
* Glamours
* Wild magical phenomena

Spells connected to nature and emotion are often strengthened within the plane.

## Inhabitants
The Feywild is home to:
* Archfey
* Eladrin
* Pixies
* Satyrs
* Hags
* Dryads
* Fey beasts

Many creatures follow ancient customs involving hospitality, names, promises, and bargains.

## Cosmological Role
The Feywild represents heightened life, emotion, creativity, and primal nature within the multiverse.
It serves as:
* A mirror of mortal nature
* A source of primal magic
* A realm of stories and legends
* A domain of powerful fey courts

The plane stands in contrast to the Shadowfell, which reflects decay and despair.

## Variations by World
Different worlds experience the Feywild differently.
In [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]], ancient forests and ruins often contain hidden crossings into the plane. Locations such as [[2-World/Places/Neverwinter Woods\|Neverwinter Woods]] are known for fey influence.
Some settings portray the Feywild as whimsical and beautiful, while others emphasise its danger and alien morality.

## Planar Travel
Travellers reach the Feywild through:
* Fey crossings
* Ancient rituals
* Powerful magic
* Moonlit gateways
* Archfey bargains

Surviving the Feywild requires caution, diplomacy, and respect for its strange supernatural customs.
