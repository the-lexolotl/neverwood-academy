# Description
Fistandria’s Pocket Dimension is a self-contained demiplane created through advanced arcane manipulation, originating from modifications to a *Mordenkainen’s Magnificent Mansion* spell. Unlike temporary conjurations, this space has been made permanent through Fistandria’s enchantments, allowing it to function as a stable extraplanar hub.

### Overview
The demiplane serves as both sanctuary and crossroads. While its interior is calm and structured, its boundary is marked by a swirling indigo miasma that separates it from the void of extraplanar space.

## Physical Properties
The interior environment is entirely artificial but highly stable:
- Gravity and atmosphere mimic the Material Plane  
- Climate is controlled and unchanging  
- Space is finite but comfortably expansive  

Beyond the mansion lies a perimeter of arcane mist, forming a soft boundary to the plane.

## Planar Adjacency
This demiplane does not naturally border other planes, but instead connects through:
- Anchored portals  
- Spell-linked thresholds  
- Arcane attunement points tied to Fistandria’s work  

## Magic on the Plane
Magic is deeply woven into the fabric of the demiplane:
- The entire space is sustained by persistent enchantment  
- Conjuration and teleportation effects are more stable within its bounds  
- Disruption of its magical core could destabilise the entire space  

## Inhabitants
- Arcane servants and unseen attendants  
- Occasional scholars, mages, or invited guests  
- No naturally occurring ecosystems  

## Cosmological Role
The demiplane functions as:
- A **mobile arcane base of operations**  
- A **research nexus** linked to major centres of learning  
- A **safe haven** removed from Material Plane threats  

## Variations by World
While similar to other demiplanes created by powerful mages, Fistandria’s is unusually stable and interconnected.

## Planar Travel
Access is typically achieved via:
- Linked portals across [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]]  
- Magical keys or attuned items  
- High-level teleportation magic
