# Description
### Overview
This vast cloudbound demiplane is home to the Storm King **[[2-World/People/Other/Up the Beanstalk/Aurum Aethelos\|Aurum Aethelos]]** and his giant kin. Unlike constructed demiplanes, it is a naturally occurring pocket of reality shaped by elemental air and ancient giant magic.

## Physical Properties
The realm consists of:
* Endless skies in shades of gold, blue, and storm-grey
* Massive cloud formations solid enough to support cities and structures
* Floating landmasses drifting slowly through the firmament

Gravity is weaker than on the [[2-World/Realms/Prime Material plane\|Material Plane]], allowing for great leaps and slow descents.

## Planar Adjacency
The realm is rarely accessible, but known entry methods include:
* Enormous **magical beanstalks** reaching into the sky
* Giant-crafted portals or rituals

## Magic on the Plane
Air and storm magic are greatly enhanced. Lightning, wind, and weather-based spells are especially potent.

## Inhabitants
* **Cloud Giants** – artisans, nobles, and scholars
* **Storm Giants** – including [[2-World/People/Other/Up the Beanstalk/Aurum Aethelos\|Aurum Aethelos]] himself
* Lesser servants, creatures of air, and possibly bound elementals

## Cosmological Role
The realm serves as a **giant stronghold and royal domain**, removed from mortal politics yet occasionally influencing events below.

## Planar Travel
Travel is difficult without giant guidance. Those who fall from the realm risk plummeting back to the Material Plane—if they are not caught by drifting cloud formations.
