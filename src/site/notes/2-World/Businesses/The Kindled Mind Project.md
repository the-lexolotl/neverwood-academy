# History
## Founding & Early Development (late 1490s DR)
The **Kindled Mind Project** was founded by [[2-World/People/Other/Halvrix Solthane\|Dr. Halvrix Solthane]], a self-proclaimed specialist in behavioural arcana and cognitive restoration. Originally operating as a small, private practice, Solthane’s work focused on “correcting maladaptive magical expression” in individuals who struggled with control, discipline, or social integration.

Early records describe a series of experimental therapies combining traditional counselling with low-level enchantment magic. While controversial among academic circles, these methods gained quiet support from certain noble families and local authorities seeking solutions for individuals deemed “difficult” or “dangerous.”

## Expansion & Institutional Adoption (1500–1510 DR)
As demand grew, Solthane formalised the programme into a scalable model, allowing contracted facilities to operate under the **Kindled Mind** name. Partnerships were established with regional authorities, private academies, and judicial bodies across [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]], positioning the programme as an alternative to expulsion, imprisonment, or exile.

During this period, the programme introduced structured “rehabilitation tracks,” each tailored to specific behavioural or magical concerns. Official literature emphasised personal growth, emotional stability, and reintegration into society.

## Standardisation & Oversight (1510–Present DR)
Under the leadership of [[2-World/People/Other/Maris Velloran\|Maris Velloran]], the programme underwent further expansion and standardisation. Intake procedures, treatment cycles, and reporting structures were unified across all sites, ensuring a consistent experience regardless of location.

While marketed as a compassionate and effective intervention, the programme has faced intermittent criticism regarding its opacity, high costs, and strict confidentiality policies. Official statements maintain that these measures are necessary to protect participant privacy and therapeutic integrity.

# Programme Structure
The Kindled Mind Project operates through a series of controlled, residential interventions designed to “guide participants toward stability and self-mastery.”

## Intake & Assessment
- Referrals accepted from families, academies, and judicial bodies  
- Initial evaluations assess behavioural patterns, magical aptitude, and “risk factors”  
- Participants are assigned to tailored development tracks  

## Core Methods
### Behavioural Conditioning
Structured routines reinforce compliance, discipline, and adherence to programme expectations. Progress is measured through observable behavioural changes and responsiveness to instruction.

### Enchanted Therapy Sessions
Participants undergo guided sessions incorporating enchantment magic to encourage reflection, emotional processing, and receptiveness to suggestion.

### Memory Reframing
Selective memory alteration is employed in certain cases to “reduce harmful cognitive patterns” and support long-term behavioural change.

### Emotional Regulation
Magical techniques are used to dampen or amplify emotional responses as required, promoting stability and reducing disruptive impulses.

### Arcane Restraint & Sigil Work
Runic markings, enchanted restraints, and controlled environments are used to limit magical output and ensure participant safety.

### Wild Magic Suppression
Specialised wards and rituals stabilise unpredictable magical surges, particularly in high-risk individuals.

### Experimental Treatments
Certain facilities employ emerging or proprietary techniques. Details of these methods are restricted to authorised personnel.

## Daily Life
- Highly structured schedules with minimal unsupervised time  
- Monitored interactions between participants  
- Restricted communication with external contacts  
- Regular evaluations and progress reporting  

# Reputation
Publicly, the Kindled Mind Project is regarded as a valuable resource for individuals in need of guidance and support. Many institutions recommend it as a preferable alternative to harsher disciplinary measures.
Privately, some critics have raised concerns regarding:
- The programme’s lack of transparency  
- Reports of unusually compliant or withdrawn graduates  
- The high financial burden placed on families and sponsors  
- The broad criteria used to determine eligibility  

Despite this, the programme continues to operate widely, supported by contracts, endorsements, and a steady demand for its services.

# Services
The Kindled Mind Project provides structured behavioural and arcane intervention services.

## Residential Programmes
- Full-time, live-in rehabilitation environments  
- Tiered based on severity and magical risk  
- Duration varies depending on “progress”  

## Contracted Placements
- Agreements with academies and judicial systems  
- Used as an alternative to expulsion or incarceration  
- Includes detailed reporting to sponsors  

## Family & Sponsor Liaison
- Regular updates provided to guardians or institutions  
- Carefully managed communication channels  
- Reintegration planning upon completion  

## Specialist Tracks
- Magical instability management  
- Behavioural correction  
- Social reintegration  
- “Advanced cognitive restructuring”
