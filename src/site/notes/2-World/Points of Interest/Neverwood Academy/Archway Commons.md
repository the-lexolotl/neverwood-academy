# Description
Just south of the [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Biblioplex\|Biblioplex]], Archway Commons unfolds beneath a graceful star-shaped arch that curves overhead like a smaller reflection of the Dawnbow. The park is carefully landscaped yet deliberately open, blending trimmed lawns, winding paths, and scattered groves of flowering trees. Though designed as a place of rest and reflection, it has become one of the most socially vibrant areas on campus.
Commonly referred to simply as “the Commons,” the space is especially popular among first-year students, who gather here in groups to study, relax, or explore campus life together. On warm days, the lawns fill with picnics, animated discussions, and spontaneous magical displays as students test minor spells in a safe, informal environment. Couples often wander the paths beneath the arch, lending the Commons a reputation as a favoured dating spot.
The park is also a flexible venue for student organisations. Clubs frequently host outdoor meetings, performances, and casual competitions here, ranging from live-action roleplaying events to friendly sporting matches. In the evenings, softly glowing lanterns embedded in the trees give the Commons a gentle, dreamlike quality, encouraging lingering conversations long after classes have ended.
