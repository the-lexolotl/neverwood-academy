# Description
Located on the outer reaches of Witherbloom territory, Wiltroot Hall is a deceptively tranquil academic building surrounded by dense, tangled vegetation. Though often overlooked due to its remote position, it plays an important role in experimental botany and field-based magical research.
The hall itself is partially overgrown, with roots and vines incorporated into its architecture in ways that suggest deliberate integration rather than decay. Inside, classrooms and laboratories are designed to accommodate living specimens, some of which extend beyond their containment with minimal concern.
Despite its isolated reputation, Wiltroot Hall is considered safe and stable under normal conditions. However, its proximity to wild growth zones means that conditions can shift rapidly, requiring students to remain alert to sudden ecological or magical changes.
