# Description
At the centre of Witherbloom lies Widdershins Hall, a sprawling bog-built structure that appears to have grown organically from the surrounding [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Sedgemoor\|Sedgemoor]] wetlands. Constructed from interwoven timber, living wood, and enchanted marshstone, it blends seamlessly into its environment.
The hall is composed of interconnected pods linked by suspended walkways that sway gently with the movement of the swamp. Inside, communal spaces are warm and dimly lit, filled with hanging herbs, brewing stations, and experimental botanical specimens. The air is thick with the scent of damp earth and alchemical concoctions.
Widdershins serves as both classroom and gathering space, where students study herbalism, symbiosis magic, and the more unpredictable aspects of swamp ecology. It is a place where nature and academia are not separated, but intertwined.
