# Description
The Cultivarium is a radiant, sun-drenched botanical expanse at the heart of the Quandrix campus, where plant life grows in deliberate fractal patterns and animal forms repeat themselves in mathematically precise variations. Paths wind through spiralling hedgerows, mirrored gardens, and endlessly repeating floral structures that shift subtly as they are observed.
Originally designed as a controlled environment for studying magical growth, the Cultivarium has gradually evolved into an ecosystem where growth magic is amplified and stabilised. Spells cast within its boundaries tend to produce exaggerated but predictable results, making it a favoured site for botanical experimentation and biological modelling.
Many Quandrix students use the Cultivarium as a living laboratory, observing how mathematical principles manifest in living systems. Despite its beauty, the area requires careful regulation, as unchecked growth can quickly lead to recursive overexpansion of plant life or unintended ecological loops.
