# Description
Kollema Hall rises from the end of [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Effigy Row\|Effigy Row]] as a grand tiered amphitheatre carved into natural stone. It serves as one of the primary lecture spaces for Lorehold studies, accommodating large audiences for historical discourse, archaeological briefings, and public academic presentations.
At its centre stands a towering statue of Kollema, an early monastic scholar whose teachings shaped the foundation of Lorehold philosophy. The statue is both monument and magical conduit, occasionally resonating with residual historical echoes during particularly significant lectures.
The hall’s stepped design allows students to gather in vast numbers while maintaining clear sightlines to the central speaking platform. It is a place where history is not only studied but performed, debated, and, at times, seemingly brought back to life through magical resonance.
