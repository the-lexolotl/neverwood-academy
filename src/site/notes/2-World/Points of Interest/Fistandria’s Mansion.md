# Description
Fistandria’s Mansion is a permanent manifestation of a *Mordenkainen’s Magnificent Mansion* spell, transformed through powerful enchantments into a lasting structure within her personal demiplane.

The mansion is luxurious but restrained, reflecting refinement rather than excess.

Constructed from pale stone blocks, the structure is open and airy, with wide interior spaces designed for comfort and contemplation. Hardwood floors run throughout, softened in key chambers by large, richly woven carpets.

### Interior Features
- **Bedrooms** — Warm and quiet, designed for rest and privacy  
- **Study** — A centre of arcane research and scholarship  
- **Dining Room** — Spacious and formal, suited to hosting guests  
- **Trophy Room** — Displaying artefacts and accomplishments  

Furniture throughout is crafted from deep brown wood, paired with brass and bronze fixtures. Doors are made of ironbound oak, adding a subtle sense of durability beneath the elegance.

Lighting is primarily provided by oil lamps, casting a warm, steady glow that enhances the mansion’s calm atmosphere.

### Exterior
Beyond the mansion lies a boundary of swirling indigo miasma, hovering roughly 20 feet from the structure on all sides.

Creatures entering the mist experience:
- A growing sense of unease  
- Subtle disorientation  
- Heightened awareness of the unnatural environment  

This effect intensifies over the first minute of exposure, discouraging exploration beyond the mansion’s immediate safety.
