# Description
This squat pub has seen better days. The low pitched roof is missing shingles, the beer garden in back is overgrown, and the wraparound veranda is
covered in a constant silty grime. Still, this hole in the wall has its own appeal, especially for those who want to conduct their business in the shadows. The
Rambling Toad’s amenities may be sparse, but the regular clientele like it that way; it means less outsiders poking around.
