# General

**Location:** [[2-World/Places/Blacklake District.md|Blacklake District]],[[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter.md|Neverwinter]]
**Category:** Shop

Situated in the more practical stretches of the [[2-World/Places/Blacklake District\|Blacklake District]] in Neverwinter, **Carver & Thread** is the sort of shop that assumes you know what you’re about — or are willing to learn quickly.

The frontage is sturdy rather than charming. The paint is intact, the hinges are oiled, and the sign above the door reads:

> **“We have it or you don’t need it.”**

Inside, the shop is clean, well-organised, and refreshingly direct. Rope is coiled properly. Rations are sealed against damp. Potions are stored behind the counter but labelled clearly. Nothing glitters unnecessarily.

There is no upselling. There are no decorative flourishes. Everything has a purpose.

### Stock & Services
Carver & Thread provides dependable equipment for fieldwork, travel, and adventuring, including:
* Rope (hemp and silk)
* Rations (standard and long-journey packs)
* Torches, lanterns, oil
* Component pouches and common spell materials
* Bedrolls, packs, waterskins
* Climbing gear
* Potions of Healing (limited stock; kept secured)

If you need something obscure, Bessa will tell you where to try next — assuming it exists.

## Reputation Among Students
* The reliable stop before field assignments
* The place to quietly replace something you broke
* The shop that will not judge you for buying three healing potions and nothing else

Students who arrive overconfident tend to leave slightly better equipped.
Students who arrive underprepared tend to leave better informed.
