# Description
This building seems to flow out of the ground like a rolling wave, a burst of magma, or both. More than anything it resembles a giant nautilus shell
encrusted with iridescent coral. It offers intoxicating drinks to spur the imagination as well as stimulating coffees and teas for those who are ready to sharpen their senses after a long night. The pub even sports a dedicated studio for the artistically inclined to use whenever inspiration may strike, and art works ranging from traditional to exotic line the walls and decorate the long communal tables.
