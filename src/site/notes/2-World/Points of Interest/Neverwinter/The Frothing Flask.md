# Description
This building looks like a pillar hewn from the earth, and one would scarcely guess it was a pub if not for the etching above the only door depicting a mug
bubbling over. The Frothing Flask is for those who desire to carry out an intellectual debate over drinks or to continue their conjurings after leaving campus. It has all the finest enchantments to ensure a pleasant drinking and dining experience.
