# Description
Located along one of the immaculate, flower-box-lined streets of the [[2-World/Places/Protector's Enclave\|Protector's Enclave]] in Neverwinter, The Silver Shuttle caters to those who wish to look intentional.
The frontage is pristine: polished glass, understated silver lettering, and seasonal window displays featuring elegantly dressed mannequins posed mid-conversation. Inside, the atmosphere is calm, controlled, and faintly perfumed with pressed linen and cedarwood.
This is not an armourer.
It is not a costume shop.
It is an establishment.

## Services
The Silver Shuttle specialises in:
- Smart travelling attire (weather-appropriate and enchantment-compatible)
- Formal wear for galas, performances, and exclusive venues
- Cloaks, gloves, hats, and refined accessories
- Discreet armour integration beneath formal garments
- Alterations while you wait (premium fee applies)

Students preparing for an evening at The Lucid Dreamer would find themselves particularly well served here.
Davo does not “do rustic”. He does, however, do “refined adventurer”.

### Reputation Among Students
* The place to go before a formal event
* A trial by fire for anyone attached to their “signature look”
* Surprisingly affordable if you avoid the imported silks

Students who endure Davo’s scrutiny often leave looking markedly better — and standing noticeably straighter.
