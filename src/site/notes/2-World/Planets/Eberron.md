## Description
The world of **Eberron** is a vast and dynamic setting defined by its blend of magic, industry, and ancient mystery. Unlike many worlds within the multiverse, it exists as a singular, self-contained planet within a unique planar arrangement, where twelve surrounding planes influence reality in cyclical patterns.

Eberron is a world shaped not by divine decree, but by mortal ambition, arcane innovation, and the remnants of forgotten empires.

## Planet Details
Eberron is a fully realised world with diverse environments and continents:

* Vast kingdoms and city-states across [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]
* Ancient elven and draconic civilisations in [[Aerenal\|Aerenal]] and [[Argonnessen\|Argonnessen]]
* Harsh wilderness and ruins in [[Xen'drik\|Xen'drik]]
* Psionic societies in [[Sarlona\|Sarlona]]
* Frozen and inhospitable regions such as [[Frostfell\|Frostfell]]

Magic is deeply integrated into infrastructure, communication, warfare, and commerce.

## Continents
### [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]
The most politically and culturally diverse continent, home to the Five Nations and major powers shaped by the aftermath of the Last War.
### [[Xen'drik\|Xen'drik]]
A land of ancient ruins and lost empires, once home to the colossal giants of antiquity.
### [[Sarlona\|Sarlona]]
A continent dominated by psionic traditions and monastic societies.
### [[Aerenal\|Aerenal]]
An elven homeland steeped in tradition and ancestral reverence.
### [[Argonnessen\|Argonnessen]]
A draconic realm governed by ancient and enigmatic dragonkind.
### [[Frostfell\|Frostfell]]
A remote polar continent of extreme cold and isolation.

## Oceans

The seas of Eberron are vital trade routes and mystery-laden expanses:
* The Thunder Sea (between [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] and [[Xen'drik\|Xen'drik]])
* The Barren Sea (north of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]])
* Southern Oceanic expanses surrounding [[Aerenal\|Aerenal]] and [[Argonnessen\|Argonnessen]]

## Global Characteristics
* High prevalence of arcane industry (“magewright” crafts)
* Widespread use of elemental binding for transport and infrastructure
* Dragonmarked Houses influencing global commerce and governance
* The aftermath of the Last War shaping modern geopolitics

## Major Threats
* Lingering consequences of the Last War
* Aberrant magical phenomena
* Ancient ruins and awakened primordial forces
* Planar instabilities from orbiting planes such as [[Xoriat\|Xoriat]] and [[Mabar\|Mabar]]
