# General Info
The **Sword Coast** is the storied north western shore of **[[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]]**, stretching along the **Sea of Swords** from the great trade city of **Baldur’s Gate** in the south to the pirate-haunted port of **Luskan** in the north. It is one of the most iconic regions in the *Forgotten Realms*. This rugged land is a crossroads of civilisation and wilderness — home to bustling cities, deep forests, forgotten ruins, and countless opportunities for adventure. The coast’s name, according to the traveller Volothamp Geddarm, comes from the dangers that stalk its roads and waves — “where every traveller should keep a sword close to hand.”

# **Geography**
The Sword Coast occupies a long, varied stretch of terrain bordered by the **Sea of Swords** to the west and the **Western Heartlands** and **The North** to the east. The climate is temperate and wet, with mild summers, cool winters, and frequent fog rolling in from the sea.

## **Notable Regions**
* **The North** – The upper reaches of the Sword Coast, including **[[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]**, **Luskan**, and **Icewind Dale**. A frontier land of hardy folk, frost giants, and ancient ruins.
* **The Sword Coast Proper** – The central coastal strip anchored by **Waterdeep**, the “City of Splendors,” and its surrounding baronies.
* **The Western Heartlands** – A patchwork of fertile plains and merchant cities such as **Baldur’s Gate** and **Elturel**, connecting the Sword Coast to Amn and Tethyr.
* **The Dessarin Valley** – A lush inland region crossed by rivers and trade roads, home to farming villages and mysterious ruins.

## **Geographical Features**
* **Sea of Swords** – The vast, storm-tossed ocean west of [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]], scattered with rocky islands and plagued by pirates.
* **High Forest** – One of the last great elven woodlands of the North, filled with ancient magic and primeval beasts.
* **Sword Mountains** – Jagged peaks sheltering monsters, hidden dwarven halls, and ancient ruins such as Gauntlgrym.
* **Mere of Dead Men** – A vast, fetid swamp north of Waterdeep, haunted by undead and the ghosts of lost kingdoms.

# **Major Cities and Settlements**

* **Waterdeep** — The City of Splendors; a cosmopolitan hub of trade, politics, and magic.
* **[[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]** — The Jewel of the North; famed for its artisans and the magically warm Neverwinter River.
* **Baldur’s Gate** — A thriving port known for its merchant class, corruption, and strategic position on the Chionthar River.
* **Luskan** — A northern city of pirates and sellswords, ruled by the mysterious Arcane Brotherhood.
* **Daggerford** — A smaller duchy on the Trade Way, serving as a vital link between Waterdeep and Baldur’s Gate.
* **Icewind Dale** — A frigid frontier beyond the Spine of the World, home to the Ten-Towns and hardy settlers.

# **Common Races of the Sword Coast**

The Sword Coast’s cities and roads are melting pots of peoples and cultures. No single race dominates entirely; rather, a shifting mix of humans and non-humans defines its character.

| Race                       | Presence               | Notes                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Humans**                 | Widespread             | Found in every city and hamlet; divided among diverse cultures such as the Illuskans, Chondathans, and Tethyrians.                        |
| **Elves & Half-Elves**     | Common                 | Descendants of ancient empires like **Illefarn**; many dwell in or near the **High Forest** or among humans in Waterdeep and Neverwinter. |
| **Dwarves**                | Common                 | Especially shield and gold dwarves from **Mithral Hall**, **Citadel Adbar**, and **Gauntlgrym**.                                          |
| **Halflings**              | Frequent               | Found along trade routes and in rural settlements; well-liked for their charm and steadiness.                                             |
| **Gnomes**                 | Uncommon               | Often artisans or inventors from **Lantan** or rock gnome enclaves.                                                                       |
| **Tieflings & Dragonborn** | Growing                | Once rare, now increasingly common, especially in cosmopolitan centers.                                                                   |
| **Half-Orcs**              | Common in the frontier | Often integrated into frontier life; respected for strength and endurance.                                                                |

Other peoples — such as genasi, aasimar, and tabaxi — can be found in the largest cities, reflecting the region’s position as a crossroads of planar and trade routes.

# **Culture and Politics**
The Sword Coast is **not a unified nation** but a collection of independent city-states, duchies, and alliances.
Trade binds these powers more than laws or loyalty. Merchant caravans and ships carry goods and rumours from Amn to Icewind Dale, while adventurers, mercenaries, and pilgrims wander freely.

## **Major Powers**
* **The Lords’ Alliance** – A loose confederation of city-states (including Waterdeep, [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]], and Daggerford) united for mutual defense and trade stability.
* **The Harpers** – A semi-secret network of spies and idealists striving to keep power in balance and oppose tyranny.
* **The Zhentarim** – Mercenaries and smugglers seeking control through wealth and influence.
* **The Arcane Brotherhood** – A cabal of powerful mages based in Luskan’s Host Tower, often meddling in northern affairs.
* **Cult of the Dragon** – Worshipers of dragons and dracoliches who rise and fall in influence through the ages.

# **History**
## **The Dawn Ages**
Before recorded history, the lands along the Sword Coast were home to the **creator races** — dragons, giants, and early elves. The elven kingdom of **Illefarn** once ruled the area around modern Waterdeep, while dwarves of **Delzoun** carved deep halls beneath the North. These empires fell long before the rise of humankind.

## **Age of Human Expansion**
Human civilisation spread from the south after the fall of **Netheril**. Kingdoms rose and fell across the Heartlands, and settlements took root along the coast.
By the late centuries of Dalereckoning, the trade cities of Waterdeep, Baldur’s Gate, and [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] emerged as centers of power, each ruled by its own councils, dukes, or masked lords.

## **Recent Centuries**
Many cataclysms have scarred and reshaped the region:

* **The Spellplague (1385 DR)** – A world-shattering magical disaster that altered geography and magic itself.
* **The Sundering (1480s DR)** – A divine realignment that restored much of the old world’s balance, returning lands and gods thought lost.
* **The Rise of Tiamat (1489 DR)** – The Cult of the Dragon attempted to summon the Queen of Dragons; thwarted by heroes of the Lords’ Alliance.
* **The Storm King’s Thunder (1490s DR)** – The Ordning of giants shattered, bringing chaos to the North and its trade routes.
* **The Descent into Avernus (1492 DR)** – Baldur’s Gate became embroiled in infernal schemes that nearly drew the city into Hell itself.

In the current age — around **1508 DR** — the Sword Coast remains a realm of fragile peace, renewed opportunity, and endless adventure.
