## Description
**Ravenloft** is a Domain of Dread centred upon the land of **Barovia**, a bleak and isolated valley surrounded by impassable mountains and enveloped by supernatural mists. It is ruled by **Strahd von Zarovich**, a vampire lord whose curse defines the nature of the domain.
Ravenloft is one of the most well-known and stable domains within the [[2-World/Realms/Domains of Dread\|Domains of Dread]], often considered the archetypal expression of gothic horror within the demiplane.

## Geography
### Regional Overview
Ravenloft consists primarily of a single valley enclosed by the **Balinok Mountains**, with all routes beyond blocked by the Mists.
The land is characterised by:
* Dense forests
* Cold, overcast weather
* Limited arable land
* Constant atmospheric oppression

### Notable Regions
#### [[Barovia\|Barovia]]
A decaying village near the eastern gates of the domain, marked by despair and stagnation.

#### [[Vallaki\|Vallaki]]
A fortified town attempting to impose order and forced optimism through strict governance.

#### [[Krezk\|Krezk]]
An isolated settlement reliant on self-sufficiency and seclusion.

#### Castle Ravenloft
The seat of Strahd’s power, overlooking the valley from a sheer cliff. It dominates both the physical and psychological landscape.

## Political Structure
### Absolute Rule
Ravenloft is ruled entirely by **Strahd von Zarovich**:
* No formal government exists beyond his authority
* Local leaders (burgomasters) govern settlements at his discretion
* All power ultimately derives from Castle Ravenloft

### Local Governance
* Each settlement maintains a burgomaster
* Authority is limited and subject to Strahd’s will
* Law enforcement is inconsistent and often ineffective

## Culture
### Social Conditions
Life in Ravenloft is defined by:
* Fear of the unknown and the supernatural
* Resignation to suffering
* Isolation from the outside world

### Belief Systems
* Worship of the **Morninglord** persists in diminished form
* Reverence for **Mother Night** exists in rural traditions
* Religious practices are often tied to protection from darkness

### Daily Life
* Agriculture is limited and unreliable
* Trade is minimal and localised
* Travel between settlements is dangerous

## History
### Mortal Origins
Strahd von Zarovich was once a mortal warlord who conquered the valley of Barovia and established his rule.

### The Curse
Through dark pacts and personal betrayal:
* Strahd became a vampire
* The land was drawn into the [[2-World/Realms/Domains of Dread\|Domains of Dread]]
* Ravenloft became both his kingdom and his prison

### Cycles of Repetition
The domain is defined by recurring patterns:
* Strahd’s pursuit of lost love
* The arrival of outsiders drawn into the Mists
* Repeated failure and tragedy

## Economy
### Resources
* Subsistence farming
* Limited livestock
* Small-scale craftsmanship

### Trade
* Minimal inter-settlement trade
* No external trade due to isolation

## Inhabitants
### Population
* Predominantly human
* Many are descendants of original inhabitants
* Some are drawn from other worlds

### Social Structure
* Peasant majority
* Small ruling class within settlements
* No upward mobility

## Threats
### Strahd von Zarovich
* Omnipresent ruler
* Capable of observing and influencing events across the domain

### Environmental Threats
* Dense forests
* Harsh weather
* Limited resources

### Supernatural Dangers
* Undead creatures
* Predatory beasts
* The Mists themselves

## Role within the Domains of Dread
Ravenloft serves as:
* One of the most stable and defined domains
* A central example of Darklord-driven reality
* A frequent point of entry for individuals drawn into the demiplane

It is often considered the **core domain** of the [[2-World/Realms/Domains of Dread\|Domains of Dread]] due to its prominence and structure.
