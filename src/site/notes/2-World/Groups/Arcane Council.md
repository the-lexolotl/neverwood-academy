# Description
## **Overview**
The **Arcane Council** is a governing body of powerful spellcasters, scholars, and arcane institutions operating primarily within [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]], with its centre of influence based in [[2-World/Hubs/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]]. It exists to regulate, preserve, and oversee the use of magic across allied cities and affiliated institutions.
Unlike civic governments such as the [[2-World/Groups/Lords' Alliance\|Lords' Alliance]], the Arcane Council focuses exclusively on magical governance, research oversight, and containment of dangerous arcane phenomena.
Its authority is recognised in major magical centres, though enforcement varies significantly outside cosmopolitan regions.

## **Structure & Organisation**
The Arcane Council is composed of senior arcane practitioners drawn from major institutions.

### **Core Structure**
* **Archmages of the Council** – Senior members overseeing policy and magical law
* **Institutional Representatives** – Delegates from academies, guilds, and towers
* **Field Arcanists** – Operatives tasked with investigation and containment
* **Lorekeepers** – Archivists responsible for magical research and records

Membership is based on demonstrated magical expertise and political standing within recognised arcane circles.

## **Primary Institutions**
The Council maintains ties with:
* Mage towers of [[2-World/Hubs/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]]
* The arcane academies of [[Silverymoon\|Silverymoon]]
* Independent wizard enclaves across the Sword Coast
* Research archives and magical libraries in allied cities

It does not control these institutions directly, but exerts influence through licensing, regulation, and knowledge sharing.

## **Duties**
The Arcane Council is responsible for:
* Regulation of dangerous or unstable magic
* Oversight of planar research and experimentation
* Licensing of high-level arcane practitioners in member cities
* Investigation of magical anomalies and catastrophes
* Containment of forbidden or corrupted magical artefacts

It also acts as an advisory body during major magical crises affecting [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]].

## **Magical Enforcement**
The Council enforces its rulings through:
* Licensed arcanists and sanctioned enforcers
* Arcane containment wards and binding sigils
* Cooperation with civic authorities such as the [[2-World/Groups/Lords' Alliance\|Lords' Alliance]]
* Deployment of specialised response teams in magical emergencies

Direct enforcement power is limited, relying heavily on cooperation with regional governments.

## **Political Influence**
The Arcane Council holds significant influence over:
* Magical trade and enchantment licensing
* Distribution of arcane knowledge and scrollcraft
* Regulation of teleportation circles and planar research
* Identification and suppression of illicit magical experimentation

Its authority is strongest in cities with established arcane infrastructure, particularly [[2-World/Hubs/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]].

## **Reputation**
* **Arcane institutions:** Viewed as necessary for stability and knowledge preservation
* **Civic governments:** Respected but occasionally resented for interference
* **Common populace:** Often regarded as distant or mysterious
* **Unlicensed practitioners:** Seen as restrictive or threatening

The Arcane Council is widely regarded as a stabilising force, though its secrecy and selective enforcement generate suspicion in less regulated regions.
