# Description
The Neverwood Iron-Lifters Society is dedicated to the pursuit of strength, endurance, and physical resilience. Members train using a combination of traditional methods and magically enhanced techniques, pushing the limits of what the body can achieve.
Workouts often incorporate enchanted equipment, resistance spells, and controlled magical augmentation, allowing members to develop both physical and arcane strength. Despite its intense reputation, the Society fosters a supportive environment where progress is celebrated and discipline is encouraged.
For many, the Iron-Lifters Society is as much about mental fortitude as it is about physical power.

## Membership
Students may join the Neverwood Iron-Lifters Society by signing up during the Freshers Fair or at any time upon request.
New members are introduced to safe training practices and gradually build their strength over time. More experienced members may pursue specialised training routines or assist others in achieving their goals.
Participation can be casual or highly dedicated, depending on individual ambition.
