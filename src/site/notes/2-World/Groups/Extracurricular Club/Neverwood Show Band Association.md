# Description
The Neverwood Show Band Association brings together musicians, performers, and magical sound-shapers to create vibrant and dynamic musical experiences. Performances often combine traditional instrumentation with arcane effects, resulting in soundscapes that are both heard and felt.
From structured concerts to spontaneous performances across campus, the Association plays a central role in the cultural life of the Academy. Members frequently collaborate with other clubs, contributing music to festivals, theatre productions, and ceremonial events.
Innovation is highly encouraged, with many members experimenting with new forms of magical expression through music.

## Membership
Students may join the Neverwood Show Band Association by signing up during the Freshers Fair or at any time upon request.
Musicians of all skill levels are welcome, whether they specialise in traditional instruments, voice, or magical sound manipulation. Members may perform, compose, or assist with production and coordination.
Opportunities for performance are frequent, with standout members often featured in major Academy events.
