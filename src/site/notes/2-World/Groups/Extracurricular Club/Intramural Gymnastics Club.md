# Description
The Intramural Gymnastics Club is dedicated to the pursuit of agility, balance, and physical precision, often enhanced through subtle applications of magic. Members train in a variety of disciplines, from traditional floor routines and apparatus work to enchanted performances that defy gravity and physical limitation.
While the Club maintains a strong focus on discipline and technique, it also embraces creativity. Routines may incorporate illusion magic, levitation, or elemental effects, transforming athletic performance into something closer to art.
The Club regularly hosts showcases and participates in Academy events, where their performances are both admired and eagerly anticipated.

## Membership
Students may join the Intramural Gymnastics Club by signing up during the Freshers Fair or at any time upon request.
New members are introduced to foundational techniques and safety practices before progressing to more advanced routines. Training is structured but supportive, allowing students to develop at their own pace.
Members who demonstrate exceptional skill may be selected to perform in official Academy events or represent their cohort in inter-club competitions.
