# Description
The Intramural Silkball Club centres around one of Neverwood’s most popular and fast-paced sports. Silkball is a dynamic game that combines precision, teamwork, and controlled magical influence, often played in shifting arenas where the environment itself may change mid-match.
Club sessions focus on both individual skill and coordinated team play, with drills designed to improve reaction time, accuracy, and strategic thinking. Matches can be intense and highly competitive, drawing large crowds of spectators from across the Academy.
For many students, Silkball is more than just a sport—it is a source of pride, rivalry, and camaraderie.

## Membership
Students may join the Intramural Silkball Club by signing up during the Freshers Fair or at any time upon request.
Players of all experience levels are welcome, with training sessions tailored to develop both beginners and seasoned competitors. Members may participate in casual matches or try out for more competitive teams within the Club.
Regular tournaments and inter-cohort games provide opportunities for recognition and advancement.
