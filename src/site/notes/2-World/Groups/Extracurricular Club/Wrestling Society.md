# Description
The Wrestling Society offers students the opportunity to engage in controlled combat, combining physical technique with discipline and, at times, carefully regulated magical enhancement. Matches emphasise skill, control, and respect, rather than brute force alone.
Training sessions focus on strength, grappling techniques, and defensive manoeuvres, with members learning how to safely apply and counter various holds. Competitive bouts are a popular spectacle, drawing enthusiastic crowds and fostering friendly rivalries.
The Society values both physical prowess and sportsmanship, ensuring that competition remains both challenging and fair.

## Membership
Students may join the Wrestling Society by signing up during the Freshers Fair or at any time upon request.
New members are taught foundational techniques and safety rules before participating in matches. More experienced wrestlers may refine their style, compete regularly, or assist in training others.
Participation is open to all, with an emphasis on respect, discipline, and personal improvement.
