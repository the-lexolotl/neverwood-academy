# Description
The Neverwood Times is the Academy’s primary student-run publication, responsible for reporting on news, events, and developments across campus. From official announcements to investigative pieces and social commentary, the paper plays a key role in shaping student awareness and opinion.
Its writers are known for their curiosity and persistence, often pursuing stories that others might overlook—or prefer to keep hidden. While some articles focus on light-hearted topics and student life, others delve into more serious matters, occasionally uncovering truths with far-reaching consequences.
The Times operates at the intersection of information and influence, making it one of the most quietly powerful organisations within the Academy.

## Membership
Students may join the Neverwood Times by signing up during the Freshers Fair or at any time upon request.
Members may take on roles as writers, editors, illustrators, or researchers, depending on their interests and skills. New contributors are encouraged to submit articles and gradually take on greater responsibility.
Those who demonstrate initiative may lead major investigations or help shape the editorial direction of the publication.
