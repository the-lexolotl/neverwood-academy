# Overview
Neverwood’s most hands-on guild, the Adventuring Guild sends students beyond the safety of campus to face real threats, recover lost artefacts, and gain practical experience in the field.

# Player Information
### Base Pay
6gp per tenday
### Job Pay 
Varies based on mission difficulty and outcome
### Async Commitment
None – all guild activity is tied to session play.
### Group Cup Opportunities
- Completing missions
- Discovering locations and secrets
- Defeating threats
- Strong teamwork and leadership

# Purpose
- Undertake field missions
- Investigate threats
- Recover artefacts
- Provide practical combat experience

# Membership
Membership is open to second-year students and above.
Spaces are limited, and all applicants must submit a contract outlining their willingness to undertake potentially hazardous duties.
Applications are reviewed at the beginning of the academic year. Selection prioritises adaptability, resilience, and a demonstrated ability to work within a team under pressure.

# Roles & hierarchy 
- **Initiate Adventurer** – Newly accepted members, assigned to supervised parties
- **Guild Operative** – Trusted members capable of standard missions
- **Senior Operative** – Experienced adventurers, often party leaders
- **Party Leader** – Coordinates missions and team strategy
- **Guildmaster (Student Lead)** – Oversees operations, liaises with faculty

# Perks
- Access to academy-issued gear
- High-reward mission opportunities
- Practical experience toward studies
- Reputation with external factions
- High Group Cup earning potential

# Description
The Adventuring Guild serves as [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwood Academy\|Neverwood Academy]]’s primary outlet for practical field experience. Founded to bridge the gap between theoretical study and real-world application, the guild dispatches student parties to investigate threats, recover artefacts, and assist with matters deemed too dangerous - or too inconvenient - for standard faculty involvement.
The guild has a long and storied history within the academy, with many notable adventurers beginning their careers within its ranks. While officially sanctioned, its operations are often loosely supervised, leading to a reputation for both heroism and chaos in equal measure.
The culture within the guild is one of camaraderie forged under pressure. Success is celebrated loudly; failure is... survived, where possible.
