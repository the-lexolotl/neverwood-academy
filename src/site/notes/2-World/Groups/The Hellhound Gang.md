# Description
A notorious student band from [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwood Academy\|Neverwood Academy]], known for their crude lyrics, magical pranks, and pyrotechnic performances that usually end with someone on fire (often intentionally). Their shows blur the line between music and chaos magic. Their slogan:

> “If it howls, it’s probably us.”

**Origin:** They met in a detention study hall for “inappropriate spell lyrics.”

**Genre:** “Infernal Funk-Punk.”

**Notable Song Titles:**
- “The Bad Hex Touch”
- “Fireball Water Burn”
- “Foxtrot Unicorn Chimera Kraken”
- “Hooray for Boobies of the Basilisk”
- “Along Comes Manticore”

**Reputation:** Professors call them “a cautionary tale of arcane irresponsibility.” Students call them “legends.”

## Membership
The Hellhound Gang maintains a semi-closed lineup led by third-year tiefling bard [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1506/Jimmy Pyre\|Jimmy Pyre]]. While the group occasionally invites guest performers during campus festivals, true membership is reserved for those who share the band’s reckless spirit and survive at least one of their notoriously dangerous rehearsals. New hopefuls are “tested” in impromptu jam sessions that double as duels of magical endurance. Most never make it past the second chorus.

## Hierarchy
The Hellhound Gang operates under what they call a “pack order,” though it mostly serves as an excuse for shouting contests during practice. [[Jimmy Pyre]] acts as the group’s self-declared Alpha Howl, directing performances and writing lyrics. [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1506/Infernal Houndfist\|Infernal “Jarex” Houndfist]] handles logistics and “security,” while [[Lupus Thunderpelt]] oversees creative presentation. [[DJ Dirgebyte]] maintains the band’s arcane equipment, and [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Students/1506/Denis Dragontongue\|Bluntfang Dragontongue]] claims to be the group’s “spiritual advisor,” a title no one has dared to challenge.
Authority in the Hellhound Gang is fluid - leadership shifts mid-song, and disputes are settled through impromptu solos or magical duels.
