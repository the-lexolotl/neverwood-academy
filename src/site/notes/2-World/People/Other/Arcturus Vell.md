# Biography
Investigator Arcturus Vell is a seasoned agent of a distant magical council, called upon when arcane incidents escalate beyond local control. Specialising in magical mishaps, unexplained phenomena, and crimes of a supernatural nature, Vell has built a reputation for quiet efficiency and relentless precision. He is known to arrive without warning, already informed, and already forming conclusions.
Reserved and methodical, Vell conducts his investigations with an almost clinical detachment, favouring careful observation over overt displays of authority. His questioning style is calm but unyielding, often allowing silence to do as much work as his words. Those who encounter him quickly learn that nothing escapes his notice—and that he is just as interested in inconsistencies as he is in facts.
Though he rarely reveals his findings outright, one truth is widely understood: if Arcturus Vell has been called, something has gone very, very wrong.
