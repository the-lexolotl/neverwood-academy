# Biography

## Early Involvement (1480–1490 DR)
Maris Velloran first entered **[[2-World/Businesses/The Kindled Mind Project\|The Kindled Mind Project]]** as a participant rather than a practitioner. Records indicate she was enrolled following repeated behavioural incidents involving unstable magical output and refusal to comply with civic arcane regulation.

Her time in the programme is heavily redacted in official records.

What is known is that she:
- Completed the programme unusually quickly  
- Was marked as “exceptionally responsive to restructuring”  
- Was immediately retained as an administrative assistant upon release  

## Rise Within the Programme (1490–1505 DR)
Under the direct mentorship of [[2-World/People/Other/Halvrix Solthane\|Dr. Solthane]], Maris transitioned from clerical work into operational oversight. She proved highly effective in:
- Intake coordination  
- Sponsor relations  
- Facility standardisation  
- Contract negotiation with external authorities  

Her ability to present the programme in an appealing, structured manner made her indispensable to its expansion.

## Appointment as CEO (1505 DR–Present)
Following internal restructuring, Maris Velloran was appointed Chief Executive Officer of [[2-World/Businesses/The Kindled Mind Project\|the Kindled Mind Project]] network.

Under her leadership, the programme expanded significantly across Faerûn, with increased integration into:
- Judicial systems  
- Academic institutions  
- Private noble contracts  

She is widely credited with transforming the programme from a niche doctrine into a large-scale institutional service.

# Personality & Reputation
Public perception describes Maris as:
- Calm, articulate, and highly professional  
- Compassionate toward “troubled individuals”  
- An effective and modern reformer  

Within the project itself, she is regarded very differently:
- Uncompromising in enforcement of compliance metrics  
- Emotionally distant in private settings  
- Capable of swift, quiet administrative decisions that reshape entire facilities  

She is often described by internal staff as:
> “Always smiling, even when nothing about the situation is funny.”

# Physical Description
- Half-elf with refined, symmetrical features  
- Dark hair, usually worn in a neat professional style  
- Immaculate attire — tailored robes blending arcane and bureaucratic design  
- Eyes are sharp, observant, and rarely show visible emotion  
- Carries herself with controlled, deliberate precision in all movement  
- Voice is calm and measured, never raised unnecessarily  

She gives the impression of someone always already three steps ahead of a conversation.
