# Biography
Coriander is a loyal homunculus who serves Freyot, attending to his needs and assisting with his enigmatic magical experiments. Meticulous and discreet, Coriander navigates both the mundane and arcane tasks of their master’s domain with quiet efficiency, ensuring that Freyot’s work proceeds without interruption or error.
