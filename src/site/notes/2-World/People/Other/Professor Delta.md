## Appearance:
An elegant, humanoid, draped in robes of muted teal lined with fractal thread that seems to shift when viewed peripherally. They move with deliberate precision, as if every gesture were calculated for efficiency.

## Personality:

Delta is _eerily calm_, speaking in academic cadence and metaphor. They never lie outright — instead, they reframe truth into mathematical inevitability.

> “If the equation predicts collapse, then collapse must be beautiful.”  
> “You may call it evil. I call it reduction.”
