Krzrzrgh the Fat-bellied is the plump and jaded leader of a Xvart settlement, presiding over their encampment from a throne fashioned of rat bones and old pots. Though their appearance and title suggest fearsome authority, Krzrzrgh is easily enchanted by pretty things—anything that shines, sparkles, or smells sweet captures their attention, regardless of its actual worth. Their peculiar tastes are well-known among the Xvarts, making Krzrzrgh as charmingly unpredictable as they are notoriously stubborn.
