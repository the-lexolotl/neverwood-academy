# Biography
Ebeneezer the Strange has been a wizard for longer than most people have been alive, and he has never been subtle about it. Trained in formal arcane study, he spent decades moving between magical institutions, towers, and travelling circles, teaching, researching, and occasionally causing quiet scandals through sheer eccentricity.
While some colleagues dismissed him as odd or overly dramatic, Ebeneezer’s grasp of magical theory has always been impeccable. His reputation is that of a wizard who refuses to do things “the normal way,” not out of rebellion, but out of conviction. To him, magic is a form of expression as much as a tool, and he believes spells should reflect the caster’s identity.
In his younger years, Ebeneezer lived openly and passionately, forming close bonds with lovers, companions, and fellow scholars of all genders. Though many of those relationships now exist only in memory, he speaks of them warmly and without regret. Age has slowed his body but sharpened his perspective, and he remains deeply engaged with the world, curious, affectionate, and opinionated.
Today, Ebeneezer is best known as a brilliant but unconventional wizard whose advice is invaluable—if one is willing to tolerate his commentary, flair, and complete disregard for brevity.

## Appearance
Ebeneezer the Strange is a short, elderly human man with a carefully cultivated look that blends age, confidence, and theatrical flair. His hair is long and silvery white, usually tied back with ribbon, wire, or an arcane trinket he insists is “very important.” His beard is neatly trimmed and shaped with clear intention, more stylised than traditional.
He dresses in layered wizard’s robes of rich, clashing colours—velvets, silks, and embroidered fabrics that feel slightly out of fashion but worn with absolute confidence. Wide sleeves, dramatic hems, scarves, rings, and enchanted jewellery are all part of his daily attire. His spectacles are always perched low on his nose, and at least one item he wears serves no practical purpose beyond being interesting.
He smells faintly of lavender, old parchment, and residual magic.

## Personality
Ebeneezer is openly eccentric, expressive, and theatrical. He speaks with flourish, enjoys dramatic pauses, and is quick to comment on spellwork, presentation, or personal style. He is emotionally perceptive, openly affectionate, and lightly flirtatious in a way that is warm rather than invasive.
Though playful and occasionally exasperating, he is not foolish. He notices details others miss and has little patience for sloppy thinking or uninspired magic. Beneath the performance is a kind, principled man who values authenticity, creativity, and self-expression.
