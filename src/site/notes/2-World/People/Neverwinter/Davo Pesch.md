# Biography
Davo Pesch is fastidiously dressed at all times. Crisp cuffs. Immaculate tailoring. Shoes polished to a reflective sheen. His hair is styled with deliberate precision, and he carries measuring tape like a duellist carries a rapier.
He is attentive to the point of intensity.
Davo has an opinion about every garment in his shop — and several not currently in it. He will share those opinions freely. His tone is never cruel, merely *firmly corrective*.
Despite his theatrical sighs at scuffed boots or mismatched leathers, he is genuinely skilled. His advice is excellent. His alterations are precise. His eye for proportion and colour is exceptional.
He finds standard adventurer attire “tragic, structurally confused, and emotionally non-committal.”
He is, however, delighted by the challenge.

> “Right, so — full plate to a nightclub. Interesting. Let’s see what we can do.”

### Personality
* Intensely observant
* Honest to the point of blunt
* Deeply passionate about presentation
* Secretly thrilled by dramatic transformations
