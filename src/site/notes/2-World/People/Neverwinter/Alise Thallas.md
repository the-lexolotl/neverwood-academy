# Biography
Alise Thallas was a bard of remarkable presence and effortless charm, known as much for her shifting appearance as for her silvered words. A changeling from a noble background, she moved through Neverwood Academy’s social circles with practiced ease, reinventing herself as circumstances required. To Alise, identity was not something fixed, but something to be performed—and refined.

Affiliated with Silverquill, Alise believed that words were the most powerful tools a person could wield. She delighted in wit, drama, and spectacle, valuing those who could captivate an audience as much as those who could command a room. Reputation, to her, was a kind of art form, shaped by confidence and presentation rather than truth alone.

[[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Lucid Dreamer\|The Lucid Dreamer]] reflects Alise’s philosophy: a place where performance, illusion, and personality blur together. Her legacy celebrates those who can entertain, adapt, and leave a lasting impression—if only for a single unforgettable night.
