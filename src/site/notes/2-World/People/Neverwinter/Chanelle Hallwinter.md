# Biography
Once a noble knight sworn to defend the realm from encroaching darkness, Chanelle Hallwinter now lingers as a restless spirit, bound to the world by an unfulfilled duty. In life, she stood as a beacon of honour and courage; in death, she is a solemn reminder of sacrifices made in the war against ancient evils.
Chanelle’s spectral form manifests in places where the veil between worlds thins - particularly where the influence of dark magic festers. She retains fragments of her former self: her unwavering sense of justice, her tactical mind, and her deep mistrust of those who traffic in forbidden knowledge.
Though bound by her condition, Chanelle aids those who oppose the rising darkness, offering guidance, warnings, and glimpses into a past long forgotten. She recognises the signs of the enemy’s return - and fears that the battle she once fought is far from over.
