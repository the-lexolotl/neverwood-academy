# Biography
Vani is the ever-present bartender of [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Frothing Flask\|The Frothing Flask]], greeting newcomers and regulars alike with an uncanny familiarity. Created long ago by the wizard Eb Zenithon, she is a sentient construct with a sharp memory, keen insight, and a surprisingly nuanced view of the people around her.

Centuries behind the bar have given Vani a deep understanding of both the founders of the five pubs and the countless students who pass through their doors. She enjoys conversation and hospitality, but beneath her calm demeanour lies a quiet restlessness—a sense that her purpose might one day extend beyond pouring drinks and keeping stories alive.

Though careful about what she reveals, Vani has a habit of nudging curious patrons in interesting directions, especially when she senses genuine determination.
