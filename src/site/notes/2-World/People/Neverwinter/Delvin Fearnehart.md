# Biography
Delvin Fearnehart was a meticulous and quietly relentless investigator, known among his peers for pursuing truths others deemed too dangerous to touch. Operating under deep cover, Delvin infiltrated a shadowy cult devoted to forbidden knowledge, tracing whispers of a growing influence tied to dark, eldritch power.
What began as a routine investigation soon spiralled into something far more insidious. Delvin uncovered fragments of a hidden network—one that spoke in riddles, dealt in secrets, and revered a name long thought buried in myth. Before he could report his findings, however, Delvin was discovered.
His death was swift, brutal, and deliberate—a warning to any who might follow the same path. Yet even in death, Delvin left behind clues: coded notes, half-finished reports, and a trail of secrets waiting to be uncovered. Though silenced, his final investigation may yet prove to be the key to unraveling the cult’s designs.
