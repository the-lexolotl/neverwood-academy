# Biography
Kevori Fearnehart is driven by equal parts grief and determination. The younger sister of [[2-World/People/Neverwinter/Delvin Fearnehart\|Delvin Fearnehart]], she refused to accept the official account of his death, instead choosing to follow the fragments he left behind.
Where Delvin was methodical, Kevori is instinctive—quick-thinking, sharp-eyed, and unafraid to take risks. She has honed her skills as a scout and tracker, navigating both wilderness and city shadows with ease. Though not formally trained as an investigator, her personal stake in the mystery fuels her resolve.
Kevori seeks not only justice for her brother, but the truth he died protecting. She is fiercely loyal to those who aid her cause, but carries a quiet burden: the fear that she may be stepping into the same darkness that claimed Delvin.
