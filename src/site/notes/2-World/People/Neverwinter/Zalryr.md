# Biography
Zalryr is a figure consumed by devotion to a power beyond mortal comprehension. Once an obscure scholar of arcane lore, they became enthralled by forbidden texts that spoke of hidden truths and ultimate knowledge—truths attributed to a being known only in whispers.
Over time, fascination gave way to fanaticism. Zalryr abandoned their former life, gathering followers who shared—or could be persuaded to share—their belief that true enlightenment lies in secrecy, sacrifice, and submission to the unseen.
As leader of a growing sect, Zalryr operates from the shadows, orchestrating rituals, manipulating events, and eliminating threats with cold precision. To them, morality is irrelevant; only knowledge and ascension matter. Those who stand in their way are either converted… or removed.
Zalryr believes themselves chosen—an instrument of a greater will. Whether this belief is delusion or destiny remains to be seen.
