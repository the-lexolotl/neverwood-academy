# Biography
### **Rise to Power (1460–1489 DR)**
Dagult Neverember was born into the minor noble Neverember line, a cadet branch of Waterdhavian aristocracy known for wealth rather than prestige. From an early age, he distinguished himself not through martial prowess or magical talent, but through financial acumen and political instinct.
He rose steadily through the merchant-administrative networks of the [[2-World/Regions/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]], accumulating influence within the **Lords’ Alliance** through trade contracts, military provisioning, and civic reconstruction projects. His defining opportunity came after the devastation of [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]], where he positioned himself as both financier and stabilising authority during the city’s recovery.
By leveraging emergency powers, economic leverage, and strategic alliances, Dagult secured appointment as Open Lord of [[2-World/Hubs/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]]—a position he would hold for over a decade.

### **Open Lord of Waterdeep (1480–1489 DR)**
Dagult Neverember’s tenure as Open Lord was defined by expansionist governance and aggressive financial restructuring. He invested heavily in infrastructure and civic security while extending [[2-World/Hubs/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]]’s economic influence across [[2-World/Regions/The North\|The North]].
However, his administration was increasingly criticised for opacity in financial records and the diversion of funds toward external projects—most notably the continued reconstruction of [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]].
Tensions culminated in 1489 DR, when Dagult was formally removed from office. The dismissal followed sustained allegations of embezzlement and misuse of city funds. The missing treasury accounts were never fully recovered, and responsibility for the losses remains a subject of political dispute and historical speculation.

### **Exile from Waterdeep (1489–1500 DR)**
Following his removal, Dagult retreated to [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] rather than contest his exile. There, he consolidated authority under the title of Lord Protector and doubled down on reconstruction efforts.
This period saw rapid rebuilding across key districts, expansion of trade routes, and the re-establishment of civic order. It also saw increasing centralisation of power under his direct control, with governance becoming more dependent on appointed loyalists and financial oversight tied to his personal administration.

### **Lord Protector of [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] (1500–1509 DR)**
By 1509 DR, Dagult Neverember remains firmly entrenched as Lord Protector of [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]. The city stands as both his greatest achievement and his most contested legacy: visibly restored, economically active, and politically divided.
Supporters credit him with returning [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] to prominence. Critics argue that its stability comes at the cost of autonomy and transparency.

## **Personality & Reputation**
* **Calculated, disciplined, and intensely pragmatic**
* Sees governance as a system of investment and control
* Prioritises stability and legacy over popularity
* Rarely acts impulsively, preferring long-term positioning over immediate gain
Among [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]’s citizens, he is simultaneously regarded as a restorer and a usurper. In [[2-World/Hubs/Waterdeep\|Waterdeep]], his name remains closely associated with scandal, financial irregularity, and political rupture. Within the wider [[2-World/Regions/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]], he is viewed as a powerful but divisive figure whose influence has never fully waned.
