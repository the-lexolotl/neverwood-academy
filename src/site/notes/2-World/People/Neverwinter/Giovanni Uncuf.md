# Biography
Giovanni Uncuf was a brilliant and ambitious student whose talents straddled cunning, charm, and a willingness to take dangerous risks. Originally known for his sharp instincts and opportunism, his interests eventually led him toward powers best handled with care.

Gio believed strength was something to be proven and displayed, and he rarely backed down from a challenge. Though his intelligence was undeniable, his hunger for influence ultimately outpaced his restraint. His name lingers in whispers around [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwinter/The Rambling Toad\|The Rambling Toad]], tied to experiments and decisions that left lasting marks on both the place and those connected to it.
