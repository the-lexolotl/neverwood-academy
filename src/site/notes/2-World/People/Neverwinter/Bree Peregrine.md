# Biography
Bree Peregrine was a visionary artist and elemental druid whose work blurred the line between magic and self-expression. A Prismari student through and through, she believed that true art was inseparable from the artist’s identity, emotion, and intent. Her creations were deeply personal, often infused with fragments of memory, love, and conflict.

Passionate and introspective, Bree formed powerful bonds with those she cared for, drawing inspiration from those relationships as much as from the elements themselves. Though she is no longer alive, her presence remains woven into her work, inviting others to confront questions of identity, reflection, and what it truly means to be a vessel for one’s own story.
