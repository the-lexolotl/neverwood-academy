Greta is a boisterous first-year Lorehold student whose raucous laughter often precedes her arrival. Known as a golden-hearted troublemaker, she has little patience for studying, much to the delight (and exasperation) of the academy librarians. A dedicated member of the [[2-World/Groups/Extracurricular Club/Neverwood Iron-Lifters Society\|Neverwood Iron-Lifters Society]], Greta is already breaking records in powerlifting and weightlifting, and she spends her downtime helping at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Neverwood Stadium\|Neverwood Stadium]] or carousing at [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Bow's End Tavern\|Bow's End Tavern]]  —justifying the labour as part of her training. Despite her mischievous energy, she keeps close ties to her family, regularly writing to her older brother Grog.
