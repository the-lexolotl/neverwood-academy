Dean Valentin is an ancient and enigmatic figure whose mastery of sangromancy allows him to glean visions from the blood he consumes. His research and classes delve into dissection, decay, and the hidden truths of living creatures—often putting him at odds with those who value preservation over exploitation. While his methods are controversial, his knowledge is unparalleled, and even his rivals recognise the insights his work provides. Valentin’s presence in the bayou is both feared and respected, a reminder that wisdom often comes with a shadow.
