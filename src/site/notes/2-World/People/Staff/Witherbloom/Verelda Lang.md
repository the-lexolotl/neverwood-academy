Professor Verelda Lang is a knowledgeable and engaging instructor who guides students through the wonders of magical physiologies in her first-year course, A is for Ankheg. A respected Witherbloom scholar, she extends her teaching across colleges, sharing insights into unusual creatures, fractal life-forms, and artificial beings alike. Her research into the vital functions of living and constructed life informs her hands-on approach, encouraging students to observe, question, and understand the natural and magical world around them.
