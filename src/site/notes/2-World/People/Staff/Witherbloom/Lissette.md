Dean Lissette is a master healer and herbalist whose gentle presence and vibrant teaching style make her beloved by students and staff alike. Her classes favor hands-on learning, where plants, poultices, and magical remedies bloom under her careful guidance, each lesson delivered in soft words full of vivid metaphors. Known for her unwavering respect for life, she believes in preserving the natural world and nurturing all living things. Even when confronting the darker practices of her peers, Lissette uses her knowledge to turn harm into hope, teaching students that compassion and skill can shape a better future.
