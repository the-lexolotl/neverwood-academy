# Biography
Lisette is a human Witherbloom professor of growth and one of the most respected healers in Neverwood. Her reputation is legendary — some say she once cured the Lorehold founder dragon of a secret ailment. Lisette’s lessons favour demonstration over lecture, allowing students to work directly with plants, potions, and living specimens. When she speaks, her words are soft and melodic, rich with metaphors drawn from nature: the wind, the soil, or the slow unfolding of a blossom.

Lisette believes in preserving life in all its forms. She encourages her students to cultivate empathy for both people and creatures, emphasising the importance of understanding before intervention. While she is often horrified by Dean Valentin’s exploitative methods, she pragmatically incorporates the results of his experiments to save or heal future lives. Her classes are gentle yet instructive, leaving students not only knowledgeable but profoundly respectful of the delicate balance of life.
