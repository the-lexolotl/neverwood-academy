Construct V339 is a quirky cogwork archivist tasked with maintaining order within the Biblioplex’s endless shelves. Designed for precision but somehow gifted (or cursed) with personality, V339 has a habit of rambling excitedly about books, authors, obscure editions, and whatever topic has most recently caught its attention.

Easily distracted yet endlessly enthusiastic, the archivist can often be found in mid-sentence wandering off to reshelve something—or to explain, unprompted, the entire publication history of an unrelated tome. Despite its eccentricities, V339 is diligent, kind, and genuinely devoted to the preservation of knowledge. Students quickly learn that while asking V339 a question may take longer than expected, the answer will be thorough, helpful, and delivered with mechanical charm.
