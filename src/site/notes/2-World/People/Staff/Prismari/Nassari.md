# Biography
Nassari is a bold and exuberant genasi who thrives on radical artistic expression. As Dean of Expression, they encourage students to take risks, break rules, and pour their hearts into every creation. Their famous “earthquake project” nearly destroyed three campus buildings, but it remains a legendary example of the power of fearless artistry.

Nassari believes true art is born of passion rather than calculation. They challenge students to treat the world as a canvas, using magic, movement, and emotion to communicate boldly. Their flamboyance and unpredictability provide a stark contrast to the methodical guidance of [[2-World/People/Staff/Prismari/Uvilda Mistcoiler\|Dean Mistcoiler]], and they openly revel in the chaos their creativity inspires.
