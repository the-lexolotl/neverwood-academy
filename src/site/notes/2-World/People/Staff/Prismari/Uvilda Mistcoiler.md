# Biography
Uvilda Mistcoiler, the genasi Dean of Perfection, teaches that mastery is born from control, thoughtfulness, and deliberate action. Her work with elemental forms is legendary, and she can often be found refining her craft in the quiet spaces of her studio, perfecting each motion, stroke, and effect.

She is famous for her incisive critiques, tailoring advice to individual students in a way that subtly reshapes their creative vision. A single suggestion from her can transform an entire piece of work, revealing new perspectives and possibilities. Uvilda believes art begins in the mind, and she expects discipline, patience, and reflection in all she teaches.

While quiet and measured, she is fiercely devoted to her craft and students, though her careful methods often put her at odds with her counterpart, [[2-World/People/Staff/Prismari/Nassari\|Dean Nassari]].
