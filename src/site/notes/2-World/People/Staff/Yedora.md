Yedora is the ancient and patient treant who tends to the grounds of Neverwood Academy, ensuring every tree, path, and garden flourishes. Beyond her duties as head groundskeeper, she serves as the academy’s unofficial undertaker, planting a tree for each mage who passes and preserving their memory in the living landscape. Students and alumni alike speak of her quiet presence with reverence, knowing that under her care, the academy itself becomes a sanctuary for life—and for remembrance.
