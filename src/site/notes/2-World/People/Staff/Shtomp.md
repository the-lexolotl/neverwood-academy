A towering orc with a booming voice and arms like tree trunks, Shtomp runs the Mage Tower’s training sessions with a mix of military precision and surprising warmth. His bark is worse than his bite—students quickly learn that beneath the gruff exterior is a coach who genuinely wants them to succeed. Between drills, he’s often seen tinkering with enchanted gear or swapping jokes with the groundskeepers.
