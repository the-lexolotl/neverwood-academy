Even among Lorehold’s most devoted historians, Professor Osgir’s fascination with relics borders on obsession. Whether it’s a jewelled crown or a battered old shovel, he sees each artifact as a whisper from civilisations long gone. Once notorious for “borrowing” too many discoveries during his student days, Osgir turned his passion into mastery—developing the magical art of duplicative restoration. With a single fragment, he can rebuild a relic in perfect detail—and create a twin to keep for himself. His office changes weekly, a miniature museum displaying everything from demon-slaying blades to delicate elven music boxes, each one humming with history.
