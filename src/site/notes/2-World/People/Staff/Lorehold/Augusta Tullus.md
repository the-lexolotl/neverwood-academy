# Biography
Dean Augusta Tullus is a human scholar whose meticulous mind has earned her a reputation as one of Neverwood Academy’s most respected historians. She specialises in spirit studies, particularly the art of pastraising, the summoning of ghostly apparitions to illustrate historical events. Entire legions of spectral figures can appear in her lecture halls, reenacting battles, councils, and pivotal moments with uncanny detail. Students often leave her classes with an entirely new appreciation for history’s ebb and flow.

Augusta views history as a measured progression towards an ideal state. Mistakes of the past are not simply lessons but building blocks for a more orderly future. She has little patience for improvisation, chaotic experimentation, or unplanned field trips — particularly those championed by her colleague, Dean Plargg. Her students are expected to prepare thoroughly and approach study with care and discipline.

Despite her stern exterior, Augusta is deeply invested in the success of her students. She will quietly mentor those who demonstrate genuine effort, rewarding diligence and initiative with insight and encouragement. Her presence on campus is a stabilising one, and her office is said to contain shelves of arcane manuscripts, preserved artefacts, and personal journals chronicling centuries of careful observation.
