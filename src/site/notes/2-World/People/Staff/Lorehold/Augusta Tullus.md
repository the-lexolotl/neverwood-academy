Dean Augusta Tullus is a disciplined and meticulous scholar, renowned for her expertise in spirit studies and her skill as Lorehold’s most talented pastraiser. She can summon legions of ghostly apparitions to reenact historical events, bringing lessons vividly to life for her students. Tullus believes that history unfolds as an orderly progression, and that understanding past mistakes allows for a more harmonious present. Strict and by-the-book, she frowns upon the reckless excursions favored by Dean Plargg, emphasizing safety, structure, and the measured study of the past.
