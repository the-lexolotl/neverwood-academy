Dean Plargg is a formidable and charismatic figure in Lorehold, celebrated for his mastery of military history and potent scroll magic. Blind since birth, he invented heat lettering, a magical embossing technique that allows texts to be read by touch, reflecting his inventive approach to learning. Plargg teaches that history is unpredictable and filled with war, emphasizing that rewards require risk—and that his students should always be prepared for the unexpected. Unconcerned with attempts to impose order, he often dismisses Dean Tullus’s work as foolhardy, instead championing the wild, chaotic lessons of the past.
