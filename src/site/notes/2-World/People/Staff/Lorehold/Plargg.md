# Biography
Plargg, the broad-shouldered orc Dean of Chaos, is a figure of infectious energy and unapologetic risk-taking. Blind from birth, he has developed a remarkable memory for historical battles, leaders, and strategies, and he created a magical form of tactile reading called heat lettering so he could “feel” the lessons of history. His lectures are a whirlwind of excitement, often involving reenactments of daring military manoeuvres, complete with elaborate magical scroll effects.
Where [[2-World/People/Staff/Lorehold/Augusta Tullus\|Dean Tullus]] sees careful patterns and lessons, Plargg sees the raw, unpredictable flow of history. To him, progress is forged through conflict, chaos, and trial by fire. Students under his guidance are expected to embrace risk and face challenges head-on, whether in the classroom, the battlefield, or field exercises. His excursions are notorious for ending in minor injuries — or at least bruised egos — but they are always unforgettable.
Plargg dismisses attempts to impose order on chaos as folly, yet he has a genuine concern for the safety and growth of his students. Those willing to follow his lessons often emerge braver, sharper, and far more resilient than they imagined possible.
