# Biography
Professor Hazkira Ravenfang is a meticulous scholar of history and astronomy, renowned for her ability to trace patterns across centuries and constellations alike. Though small in stature, she possesses a commanding presence in the lecture hall, guiding students through the orderly march of historical events and the predictable rhythms of the heavens. Hazkira teaches that history and the stars share a common truth: while chaos may appear dominant in the moment, structure and repetition emerge when viewed with patience and perspective.

Her lectures often pair ancient chronicles with star charts, encouraging students to consider how celestial movements have shaped cultures, calendars, and conflicts throughout recorded history. Calm, precise, and unfailingly prepared, Professor Ravenfang believes that understanding the past—and the skies above—allows scholars to anticipate the future with clarity and purpose.
