# Biography
Professor Matreous was a respected Lorehold scholar whose work focused on extradimensional spaces and the unpredictable nature of planar travel. His research explored the instability inherent in portals, pocket realms, and liminal thresholds, emphasizing the dangers as much as the possibilities of traversing beyond conventional reality. In the classroom, he encouraged students to embrace uncertainty, teaching that chaos was not merely disorder, but a force that revealed hidden truths.

On the 6th of Marpenoth, 1508, Professor Matreous was found deceased in his office, bringing a sudden and unsettling end to his work. His death remains a sombre mark on Neverwood Academy’s recent history, and his unfinished research continues to influence ongoing studies within Lorehold. Among students and faculty alike, he is remembered as a brilliant, driven scholar whose curiosity reached far beyond the boundaries of the known world.
