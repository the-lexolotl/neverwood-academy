# Biography
Dean Imbraham is a stern and calculating owlin, whose piercing gaze and habit of fully rotating his head can unsettle even the most confident students. A neutral Quandrix professor of theory, he lives and breathes numbers, rarely leaving the confines of his chambers — some whisper he hasn’t stepped outside in years. His life is devoted to uncovering the underlying structures of magic and reality, often through impossibly complex theorems and abstract equations.

Imbraham’s lectures are precise and demanding, challenging students to think beyond conventional boundaries. He offers “bounties” on unsolved problems, encouraging pupils to contribute to his relentless pursuit of knowledge. Though his methods may seem cold or intimidating, those who engage deeply with his lessons discover a mind capable of unveiling the hidden elegance of the world itself.

Students who thrive under Dean Imbraham learn patience, meticulous reasoning, and the satisfaction of solving problems that appear unsolvable — and the reward of seeing patterns where others see only chaos.
