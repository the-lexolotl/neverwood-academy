# Biography
Professor Senestrine Darou is a brilliant and unsettling presence within Neverwood Academy. A true academic polymath, she has studied across multiple colleges, ultimately finding her focus in extraplanar theory within Quandrix. Her research centres on possibilities most scholars consider impractical, dangerous, or outright impossible.

Darou has little patience for distractions, whether they come in the form of students, colleagues, or institutional politics. She is convinced her work will reshape magical understanding—and expects recognition to follow once she proves herself right. Though she tolerates administrative oversight, she deeply resents interference in her research.

Cold, focused, and intensely driven, Professor Darou embodies the belief that knowledge is worth any personal cost.
