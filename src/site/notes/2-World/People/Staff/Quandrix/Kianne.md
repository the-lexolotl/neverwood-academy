Dean Kianne is a brilliant and eccentric mana scholar whose studies span the most unusual magical phenomena, from snarls to star arches to ancient shrines. A champion of applied magic, she believes that mathematics holds the key to understanding and improving the natural world, emphasizing practical solutions in her teaching. Known for her enthusiasm and lively explanations, she shares her theories with any student or colleague willing to listen—though she often rolls her eyes at ideas she deems too fanciful, especially those of Dean [[2-World/People/Staff/Quandrix/Imbraham\|Imbraham]].
