Professor Orvlo, formerly a Quandrix professor of theory, now teaches a series of general studies courses covering the fundamentals of various magical schools. Fascinated by the subtle details that distinguish each form of magic, he emphasizes careful study and a deep understanding of the basics. Orvlo spends much of his time in the Biblioplex, where he also volunteers as a librarian, detecting and dispelling any student-applied enchantments on the tomes and scrolls. Known for valuing diligence and earnest effort, he has little patience for jokes or attempts to curry favour, expecting students to meet him with respect and focus.
