# Biography
Elandra Stormwhisper is a spirited Prismari student known for her grace, creativity, and infectious enthusiasm. A talented practitioner of dance combat, she combines artistic flair with precision in both performance and study. Warm-hearted and confident, Elandra is open about who she is and embraces life, art, and love with equal passion, making her a beloved presence among her peers.
