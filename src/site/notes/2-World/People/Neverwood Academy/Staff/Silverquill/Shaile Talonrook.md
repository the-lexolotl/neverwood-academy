# Biography
Shaile Talonrook is an owlin and a lawful good Silverquill professor of radiance, renowned for her boundless optimism and gift for speech. A master of literature, she frequently peppers lessons and advice with obscure quotations from long-forgotten texts, lending her words a sense of wisdom and gravitas. Many students leave her office feeling as though they themselves are glowing with purpose and inspiration.

Shaile believes that all people are capable of goodness, and she strives to guide her students towards using their talents to better the world. Her classes blend practical exercises in persuasive speech, storytelling, and magical rhetoric, encouraging students to find their own voices while honing the power of language as a tool for positive change.

She often sparrs intellectually with [[2-World/People/Neverwood Academy/Staff/Silverquill/Embrose Lu\|Dean Embrose Lu]], whose darker philosophy is a foil to her own, yet their debates have forged a uniquely eloquent balance in the Silverquill curriculum. Shaile’s warmth and patience make her approachable, though her presence in the hallways of Neverwood is impossible to ignore: she exudes a quiet authority that compels attention wherever she goes.
