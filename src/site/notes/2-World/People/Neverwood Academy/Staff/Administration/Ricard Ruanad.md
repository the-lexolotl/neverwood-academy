# Biography
Ricard Ruanad was a master of words and wealth. A former Silverquill academic, he had long ago shifted his focus from teaching to something he found far more rewarding: influence. Through shrewd deals, polished rhetoric, and an impeccable network of contacts, Ruanad had secured both his position and a growing personal fortune.

He viewed the academy less as a place of learning and more as a collection of valuable assets waiting to be leveraged. Students, faculty, alumni—everyone had a price, and Ruanad prided himself on figuring out what it was. While he spoke often of discipline and tradition, his loyalty ultimately lay with power and profit.

Polite, calculating, and utterly self-assured, Deputy Head Teacher Ruanad was a reminder that not all threats wore a villain’s face.
