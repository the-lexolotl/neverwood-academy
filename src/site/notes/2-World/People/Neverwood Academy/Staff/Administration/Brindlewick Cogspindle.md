# Biography
Professor Brindlewick Cogspindle serves as [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwood Academy\|Neverwood Academy]]’s Deputy Head Teacher, arriving in the wake of a recent administrative scandal that left the position abruptly vacant. Formerly a senior lecturer at [[Morgrave University\|Morgrave University]] in [[Eberron\|Eberron]], he brings with him a reputation for precision, discretion, and an uncompromising approach to academic order.
Measured in both word and action, Brindlewick is known for his meticulous oversight and near-impossible attention to detail. He has quickly established himself as a stabilising force within the academy, reviewing policies, refining procedures, and ensuring that both staff and students adhere to the standards expected of Neverwood.
Though outwardly courteous and composed, there is an intensity behind his work that few can ignore. Brindlewick values structure, accountability, and control, believing that a well-regulated institution is the foundation upon which true magical excellence can be built. Students rarely find themselves in his office without reason - and fewer still leave without consequence.
Despite his stern reputation, he is undeniably effective, and under his guidance, Neverwood has begun to regain a sense of order... even if that order comes with a watchful eye.
