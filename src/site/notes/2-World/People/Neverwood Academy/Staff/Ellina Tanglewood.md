# Biography
Ellina Tanglewood is the cheerful and capable manager of the Firejolt Café, serving as both its chief barista and guiding presence. A skilled mage with an easygoing demeanour, she works closely with a small team of student employees—typically one senior student from each college—while remaining open to mentoring younger students who show genuine enthusiasm for the craft.

Under Ellina’s leadership, the Firejolt Café has become a lively gathering place on campus, known for its expertly brewed drinks and welcoming atmosphere. Balancing creativity with responsibility, she fosters a workspace where students can learn, collaborate, and feel at home, making the café a favourite stop for both early mornings and late-night study sessions.
