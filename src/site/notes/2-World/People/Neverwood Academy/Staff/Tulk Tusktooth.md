# Biography
Tulk “The Bulk” Tusktooth is the steadfast manager of Bow’s End Tavern, known for his imposing presence and even more imposing sense of fairness. A former gladiator, Tulk stands nearly seven feet tall, all muscle and quiet authority, yet greets patrons with a warm smile and the glint of a signature golden earring.

Kind but uncompromising, Tulk has no tolerance for misbehavior and ensures the tavern remains a welcoming place for students and staff alike. Under his watchful eye, Bow’s End is lively but orderly—a balance Tulk enforces with calm words long before he ever needs to rely on strength.
