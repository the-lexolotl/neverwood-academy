# Biography
Groff Lundquist is the cheerful and fashion-forward manager of the student shop within the Biblioplex. Known for his impeccable eye for colour coordination, Groff has an uncanny talent for pairing house colours with just the right accessory, ensuring students can display their college pride with style.

Always ready with a friendly greeting, he helps students find everything from practical notebooks and enchanted quills to the perfect scarf or pin. With an instinct for both fashion and function, Groff has made the shop a welcoming stop for anyone looking to prepare for class—or simply look their best while doing so.
