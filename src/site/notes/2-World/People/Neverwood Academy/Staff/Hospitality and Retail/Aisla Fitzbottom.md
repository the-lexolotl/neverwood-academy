# Biography
Aisla Fitzbottom is the cheerful yet no-nonsense gnome responsible for keeping the Biblioplex Café running like a well-oiled machine. With an ever-present smile and an eye for efficiency, she manages a rotating team of student staff, ensuring that coffee is hot, pastries are fresh, and scholars are properly fuelled for long hours of study.

Though friendly and approachable, Aisla has little patience for dawdling or disorder, and students quickly learn that her good humour is paired with clear expectations. Under her steady guidance, the café has become a reliable haven within the Biblioplex—a place where tired minds can rest, refocus, and carry on with their work.
