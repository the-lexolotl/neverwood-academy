# Biography
Valentin is an ancient vampire and Witherbloom professor of decay, whose study of life and death is as unnerving as it is brilliant. Drawing visions from the blood he consumes, Valentin sees the hidden histories of creatures and places alike, using these insights to uncover truths invisible to most scholars. He does not shy away from exploiting the bayou’s creatures for his research, earning the cautious respect — and sometimes the quiet ire — of his colleagues.

His classes focus on dissection, decay, and the arcane applications of life’s darker aspects. Students are taught that knowledge can be drawn even from what others consider grim or unpleasant, and that power itself is neutral; its value is defined by the wielder. While his methods are controversial, Valentin is precise, commanding, and utterly fascinating to those willing to engage with his unusual perspective.
