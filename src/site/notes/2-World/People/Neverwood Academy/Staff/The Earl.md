# Biography
Known simply as the Earl, this refined clockwork butler continues to tend Dapplewing Manor long after the passing of Captain Raymous Dapplewing. Impeccably dressed in a silver waistcoat, black tailcoat, and neatly tied cravat, the Earl paces the manor’s halls with measured steps, ever vigilant in its duties.

Though its master is gone, the Earl maintains the household with unwavering dedication, polishing silver, preparing rooms, and preserving the manor exactly as it was in Captain Dapplewing’s lifetime. Visitors often remark on the butler’s quiet, tireless presence, as though the manor itself refuses to forget its former owner.
