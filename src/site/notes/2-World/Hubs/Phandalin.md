# General
Phandalin is a small frontier town located south of [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] along the Triboar Trail. Though modest in size, the settlement has become increasingly prosperous thanks to renewed mining efforts and growing trade connections.
The town attracts miners, merchants, adventurers, and settlers hoping to build new lives on the frontier.

## Geography
Phandalin lies near the foothills of the Sword Mountains, surrounded by forests, hills, and old ruins dating back to ancient kingdoms.
Nearby locations include:
* **Wave Echo Cave** – legendary mine rich with magical history.
* **Conyberry** – abandoned village east of town.
* **[[2-World/Places/Neverwinter Woods\|Neverwinter Woods]]** – dangerous forest to the north.
* **Icespire Peak** – mountain home to monsters and dragons.

## History
Phandalin was originally part of the ancient settlement of **Phandelver**, which prospered through mining and trade alliances between humans, dwarves, and gnomes.
The original settlement was destroyed centuries ago by orcs and monsters. In recent years, settlers rebuilt the town, and the rediscovery of Wave Echo Cave brought renewed prosperity.
By the early 1500s DR, Phandalin has become a growing frontier community balancing opportunity with constant danger.

## Government
Phandalin has no formal noble ruler. The town is overseen by local officials and influential townsfolk, particularly the **Townmaster**.
Law enforcement is informal compared to larger cities, relying heavily on local militias and adventurers.

## Defences
The town’s protection consists mainly of:
* Local militias
* Adventurers
* Temporary guards hired by merchants and miners

Palisade walls and defensive measures continue to improve as the town expands.

## Society
Phandalin’s people are practical, hardworking, and community-minded. Most residents are miners, farmers, traders, or craftspeople trying to carve out stable lives on the frontier.
Visitors are common, especially adventurers seeking fame or treasure.

## Religion
Small shrines and temples honour:
* **Tymora**
* **Chauntea**
* **Tempus**
* **Lathander**

Religious life is simple compared to the great cities of the Sword Coast.

## Trade & Craftsmanship
Phandalin’s economy depends on:
* Mining
* Farming
* Logging
* Caravan trade

Its location between major trade routes gives the town growing economic importance.

## Districts
Phandalin is small enough that it has no formal districts, though key locations include:
* **Stonehill Inn**
* **Townmaster’s Hall**
* **Phandalin Miners’ Exchange**
* **Shrine of Luck**

## Factions & Organizations
* **The Harpers**
* **The Zhentarim**
* **The [[2-World/Groups/Lords' Alliance\|Lords' Alliance]]**
* **Order of the Gauntlet**

## Symbol
Phandalin has no widely recognised heraldic symbol, though mining imagery and pickaxes are commonly associated with the settlement.
