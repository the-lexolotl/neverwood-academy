## Description
**Citadel Adbar** is one of the oldest and most formidable dwarven fortresses in **[[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]]**, located in the northern reaches of [[2-World/Regions/The North\|The North]] near the **Ice Mountains**. Known for its unmatched defensive strength and martial discipline, it stands as a bulwark against the many threats of the northern wilderness.

Unlike [[2-World/Hubs/Mithral Hall\|Mithral Hall]]’s wealth-driven culture, Citadel Adbar is defined by its militaristic society, with nearly every citizen trained in warfare and defence.

## Geography
Citadel Adbar lies partially above and below ground, with massive stone fortifications rising from the foothills of the Ice Mountains and extensive tunnels stretching deep beneath.

### Notable Regions
* **The Iron Gates** – Heavily fortified entrances designed to withstand prolonged sieges.
* **The Lower Halls** – Residential and industrial sectors deep within the mountain.
* **The Warforges** – Massive foundries producing arms and armour in great quantities.
* **The Deep Roads** – Tunnel networks connecting to other dwarven holds and the Underdark.

### Geographical Features
* **Ice Mountains** – Harsh, snow-covered peaks providing natural defence.
* **Frozen Tundra** – Surrounding landscape, difficult for large armies to traverse.
* **Underdark Access Points** – Constantly monitored and heavily guarded.

## Major Cities and Settlements
* **Citadel Adbar** — The central fortress-city and seat of dwarven power in the region.
* **Outer Watch Posts** — Military outposts used to detect incoming threats.

## Culture and Politics
Citadel Adbar operates as a highly structured military society, where duty and discipline take precedence over personal ambition.

### Major Powers
* **The King of Citadel Adbar** – Absolute ruler, typically a seasoned war leader.
* **The War Council** – Senior generals and advisors overseeing military strategy.
* **Forge Guilds** – Responsible for the production of arms and armour.
* **Priests of Moradin and Clangeddin** – Provide spiritual guidance and war blessings.

Cultural values include:
* Military readiness at all times
* Loyalty to the citadel above all else
* Strength through unity and discipline
* Craftsmanship focused on function and durability

## History
### Founding and Early Days
Citadel Adbar was founded in ancient times by shield dwarves seeking a defensible position against orc and giant threats. Its location near the Ice Mountains made it an ideal stronghold for long-term defence.

### Age of Constant War
Throughout its history, Citadel Adbar has endured:
* Repeated orc invasions from the North
* Attacks by frost giants and other northern creatures
* Underdark incursions from below

Its reputation as an unbreakable fortress was forged through centuries of successful defence.

### Alliance and Influence
Citadel Adbar has long maintained alliances with other northern powers, including:
* **[[2-World/Hubs/Mithral Hall\|Mithral Hall]]**
* **[[Silverymoon\|Silverymoon]]**
* The cities of the **[[Silver Marches\|Silver Marches]]**

These alliances ensure mutual defence and trade support across the region.

### Recent Events
In the late 15th century DR:
* Citadel Adbar remains one of the strongest dwarven bastions in the North
* It continues to guard against northern threats and maintain regional stability
* Its forges supply arms to allied settlements and military forces
