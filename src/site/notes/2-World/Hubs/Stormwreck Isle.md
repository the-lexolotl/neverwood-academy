# General
Stormwreck Isle is a remote island in the [[2-World/Regions/Sea of Swords\|Sea of Swords]], known for violent storms, ancient dragon legends, and dangerous ruins. Sailors often avoid the island, believing it cursed by draconic magic and centuries of conflict.
Despite its reputation, the island also serves as a place of spiritual reflection and study for those seeking balance between metallic and chromatic dragon influences.

## Geography
The island is dominated by rocky cliffs, volcanic terrain, and storm-battered coastlines.
Key locations include:
* **Dragon’s Rest** – monastery dedicated to Bahamut.
* **Seagrow Caves** – coastal caverns inhabited by dangerous creatures.
* **Compass Rose** – shipwreck ruin along the shoreline.
* **Clifftop Observatory** – ancient draconic ruin.

## History
Stormwreck Isle has long been associated with dragons and magical conflict. Legends speak of ancient battles between metallic and chromatic dragons whose magic scarred the island permanently.
Over time, shipwrecks and ruins accumulated along the shores, giving the island its name.
By the early 16th century DR, the isle remains sparsely populated, visited mainly by monks, explorers, and adventurers.

## Government
Stormwreck Isle has no formal government. Small communities and monastic groups govern themselves independently.

## Defences
The island relies primarily on natural hazards for protection:
* Treacherous reefs
* Violent storms
* Dangerous wildlife
* Isolated terrain

The monks of Dragon’s Rest also defend the island when necessary.

## Society
Very few permanent residents live on Stormwreck Isle. Most inhabitants are monks, sailors, fishers, or travellers seeking knowledge about dragons and the island’s mysteries.

## Religion
The island’s spiritual traditions are strongly tied to dragons and balance.
The monks of Dragon’s Rest primarily honour:
* **Bahamut**
* **Tiamat**
* Draconic spiritual traditions

## Trade & Craftsmanship
Stormwreck Isle has little formal trade. Supplies arrive occasionally from mainland settlements via small ships.
Fishing and salvaging shipwrecks provide limited resources.

## Districts
Stormwreck Isle has no true districts, though several notable locations serve as population centres:
* **Dragon’s Rest**
* **Compass Rose**
* **Seagrow Caves**

## Factions & Organizations
* **Dragon’s Rest Monastery**
* Small independent adventuring groups and explorers
