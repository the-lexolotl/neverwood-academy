# General
Waterdeep, known across [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]] as the *City of Splendors*, is the greatest metropolis of the [[2-World/Regions/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]] and one of the wealthiest cities in the Realms. Built beneath the shadow of Mount Waterdeep and stretching along the shores of the [[2-World/Regions/Sea of Swords\|Sea of Swords]], the city serves as a centre of trade, culture, diplomacy, and adventure.

Waterdeep’s streets are crowded with merchants, nobles, sailors, adventurers, scholars, and travellers from every corner of [[2-World/Planets/Toril\|Toril]]. Though famed for wealth and opportunity, the city also hides countless secrets beneath its polished surface.

# Geography
Waterdeep lies on the western coast of [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]], roughly midway between [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] and [[2-World/Hubs/Baldur’s Gate\|Baldur’s Gate]]. Its enormous harbour is protected by natural cliffs and guarded by massive defensive walls.
Key landmarks include:
* **Mount Waterdeep** – towering above the city and concealing the infamous dungeon of Undermountain.
* **Undermountain** – an immense subterranean labyrinth ruled by the mad mage Halaster Blackcloak.
* **Blackstaff Tower** – home of the Blackstaff and the city’s magical defenders.
* **Castle Waterdeep** – military headquarters overlooking the harbour.

# History
Waterdeep began as a small trading settlement and gradually grew into the dominant city of the [[2-World/Regions/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]]. Over centuries it became renowned for commerce, naval power, and political influence.
The city survived numerous crises, including wars, dragon attacks, magical disasters, and political conspiracies. By the early 16th century DR, Waterdeep remains stable under the leadership of **Open Lord [[Laeral Silverhand\|Laeral Silverhand]]**, one of the famed Seven Sisters.
Waterdeep’s influence extends far beyond its walls, shaping trade and politics across [[2-World/Regions/The North\|The North]] and the wider [[2-World/Regions/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]].

# Government
Waterdeep is governed by the **Lords of Waterdeep**, a secretive council whose identities are largely hidden from the public.
The city’s visible ruler is the **Open Lord**, currently **[[Laeral Silverhand\|Laeral Silverhand]]**, who oversees diplomacy and administration. Beneath the Lords operate numerous magistrates, guild officials, and civic institutions responsible for maintaining order.
Waterdeep’s laws are strict but generally fair, and the city maintains one of the most sophisticated legal systems in [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]].

# Defences
Waterdeep possesses some of the strongest defences on the [[2-World/Regions/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]]:
* **The City Guard** – soldiers responsible for defending the walls and gates.
* **The City Watch** – peacekeepers who patrol the streets.
* **The Griffon Cavalry** – elite aerial riders mounted on griffons.
* **Force Grey** – adventurers and agents secretly serving the city.
* **The Watchful Order of Magists and Protectors** – licensed arcane spellcasters who support civic defence.

The city’s harbour is heavily fortified, and magical wards protect important locations throughout Waterdeep.

# Society
Waterdeep is cosmopolitan even by the standards of the Realms. Humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, dragonborn, tieflings, and countless others live within the city walls.
Citizens value ambition, commerce, and reputation. Wealth and status carry influence, but skilled adventurers and talented artisans can rise quickly through society.
The city is also famous for festivals, theatre, taverns, and public celebrations.

# Religion
Nearly every major faith in [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]] maintains temples within Waterdeep.
Prominent faiths include:
* **Tymora** – goddess of luck and adventurers.
* **Oghma** – god of knowledge.
* **Mystra** – goddess of magic.
* **Torm** – god of duty and righteousness.
* **Sune** – goddess of beauty and passion.

Large temple complexes dominate several districts, reflecting the city’s religious diversity.

# Trade & Craftsmanship
Waterdeep is the greatest trade hub on the [[2-World/Regions/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]]. Goods from across [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]] pass through its markets and docks.
The city’s guild system regulates most professions, ensuring quality and maintaining economic stability. Waterdeep is especially renowned for:
* Shipbuilding
* Fine metalwork
* Magical services
* Luxury crafts
* Banking and mercantile trade

# Districts
Waterdeep is divided into major wards:
* **Castle Ward** – centre of government and commerce.
* **Dock Ward** – busy harbour district known for sailors and smugglers.
* **North Ward** – wealthy residential neighbourhoods.
* **Sea Ward** – noble villas and embassies.
* **Trades Ward** – bustling markets and workshops.
* **Southern Ward** – caravan traffic and inns.
* **Field Ward** – overcrowded settlement outside the main walls.

# Factions & Organizations
Many influential groups operate within Waterdeep:
* **The Harpers** – covert agents working for balance and justice.
* **The Zhentarim** – mercenary and criminal network.
* **The [[2-World/Groups/Lords' Alliance\|Lords' Alliance]]** – coalition of major cities and rulers.
* **Force Grey** – secret protectors of the city.
* **The Xanathar Guild** – criminal organisation operating beneath the city.

# Symbol
Waterdeep’s symbol is a crowned tower rising above the sea, representing the city’s strength, prosperity, and authority over trade along the [[2-World/Regions/Sword Coast\|Sword Coast]].
