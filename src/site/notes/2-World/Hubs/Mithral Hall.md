## Description
**Mithral Hall** is one of the most famous and wealthy dwarven strongholds in **[[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]]**, located beneath the **Spine of the World** in [[2-World/Regions/The North\|The North]]. Renowned for its immense wealth, master craftsmanship, and deep cultural significance, it stands as a symbol of dwarven resilience and reclamation.

Originally founded by the **Shield Dwarves** of clan Battlehammer, Mithral Hall was lost for centuries before being reclaimed in the 14th century DR. Since its restoration, it has once again become a centre of dwarven power, trade, and military strength in the North.

## Geography
Mithral Hall lies deep beneath the mountains east of the **Surbrin Valley**, connected to the surface through heavily fortified gates and hidden passages.

### Notable Regions
* **The Upper Halls** – The primary residential and trade districts, including markets and clan quarters.
* **The Deep Mines** – Vast mining networks extending miles beneath the mountains, rich in mithral and rare ores.
* **The Battle Caverns** – Defensive tunnels and kill-zones designed to repel invaders.
* **The Throne Hall** – Seat of rulership, adorned with ancient dwarven artistry and clan banners.

### Geographical Features
* **Spine of the World** – Towering mountain range sheltering the hall.
* **The Underdark** – Ever-present threat beneath the deepest tunnels.
* **Subterranean Rivers** – Provide fresh water and power for industry.

## Major Cities and Settlements
* **Mithral Hall** — The primary stronghold and capital of the Battlehammer dwarves.
* **Surface Outposts** — Small fortified settlements and trade enclaves connecting the hall to the wider North.

## Culture and Politics
Mithral Hall is governed according to traditional dwarven clan structure, placing heavy emphasis on honour, ancestry, and craftsmanship.

### Major Powers
* **Clan Battlehammer** – The ruling clan of Mithral Hall.
* **The Council of Elders** – Advises the ruling king on matters of law, trade, and war.
* **Priesthood of Moradin** – Influential in both spiritual and political matters.
* **Merchant Clans** – Control trade routes and resource distribution.

Dwarven society within Mithral Hall values:
* Craftsmanship as a sacred duty
* Loyalty to clan and kin
* Honour in both war and trade
* The preservation of ancestral traditions

## History
### Founding and Early Days
Mithral Hall was founded in ancient times by the **Battlehammer clan**, who discovered vast veins of mithral within the mountains. The hall grew into a legendary stronghold, famed for its wealth and craftsmanship.

### Fall of Mithral Hall
In **1356 DR**, the stronghold was overrun by a **shadow dragon**, later revealed to be **Shimmergloom**, along with duergar and other Underdark forces. The dwarves were forced to abandon their ancestral home, which remained lost for decades.

### Reclamation
In **1370 DR**, **Bruenor Battlehammer**, alongside companions including Drizzt Do’Urden, led a successful campaign to reclaim Mithral Hall. After fierce battles against duergar and drow forces, the hall was restored to dwarven control.

### Recent Events
Following its reclamation:
* Mithral Hall has re-established itself as a dominant dwarven power in the North
* Trade with [[Silverymoon\|Silverymoon]], [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]], and other cities has expanded
* Continued vigilance is maintained against Underdark threats
