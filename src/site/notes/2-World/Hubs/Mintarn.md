## Description
Mintarn is a disciplined and highly strategic island-state known across [[2-World/Continents/Faerûn\|Faerûn]] as the **premier source of professional soldiers**.
Unlike [[2-World/Hubs/Luskan\|Luskan]]’s chaos, Mintarn operates with calculated precision. War here is not passion — it is **business**.

## Geography
The island is rocky and heavily fortified, with natural cliffs providing strong دفاع against naval assault.
Its harbours are designed for rapid deployment, and its inland areas are dominated by training grounds and barracks.

## History
Mintarn began as a military outpost but evolved into a mercenary hub as its strategic position became clear.
Over time, it developed a reputation for **reliability and discipline**, attracting contracts from cities across the Sword Coast — including [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]].

## Government
Mintarn is ruled by a coalition of **military leaders and contract houses**, each representing powerful mercenary companies.
Decisions are made based on profit, strategy, and long-term stability.

## Society
Life on Mintarn revolves around military service.
Even civilians are connected to the war economy, supporting logistics, supply chains, and training.
Honor exists — but it is defined by **fulfilling contracts**, not ideals.

## Religion
**Tempus**, god of war, is the dominant deity.
Worship is direct and practical, focusing on strength, discipline, and victory.

## Trade & Economy
Mintarn exports:
* Trained soldiers
* Officers and tacticians
* Military consultation

Its contracts influence conflicts across the entire region.

## Threats
* Conflicting contracts creating indirect wars
* Espionage between rival companies
* External powers attempting to control Mintarn
