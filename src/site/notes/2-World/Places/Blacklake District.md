# General

**Location:** [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter.md|Neverwinter]]
**Category:** Unknown

## Overview
Blacklake District is the seat of wealth and influence within Neverwinter. Built around the deep, dark waters of Blacklake itself, the district is home to noble families, influential merchants, and those who prefer their power exercised behind closed doors rather than from public squares.

Unlike [[2-World/Places/Protector's Enclave\|Protector's Enclave]], which performs authority openly, Blacklake cultivates exclusivity. High walls, wrought-iron gates, and manicured gardens conceal sprawling estates and private towers. Here, politics is conducted over fine wine, contracts are sealed in drawing rooms, and reputations are crafted with precision.

Blacklake is not loud. It does not need to be.

## District Character
**Architecture:**  
Grand manors, private towers, enclosed courtyards, and lake-facing terraces. Dark stone and polished marble are common, often accented with imported timber and ornamental ironwork.

**Atmosphere:**  
Refined, guarded, and quietly competitive. Servants move efficiently. Carriages arrive without fanfare. Conversations lower when strangers approach.
**Common Sounds:**  
- Hooves on cobblestone  
- Fountain water in enclosed gardens  
- Distant string quartets  
- Low, controlled conversation  
**Common Smells:**  
- Expensive perfume  
- Polished wood  
- Garden blooms  
- Fine tobacco  

## The Blacklake Itself
The lake is deep and unusually dark, its waters reflecting little light even at midday. Private docks extend from certain estates, and small pleasure craft are sometimes seen gliding across its surface.

Rumours persist that parts of the lakebed conceal relics from before the eruption of Mount Hotenow — or older still.

## Notable Locations
- Noble estates of long-standing Neverwinter families  
- Private arcane towers  
- Exclusive dining halls and wine salons  
- Discreet meeting houses for merchant consortiums  
- Lakeside pavilions used for galas and masked gatherings  

## People You Might Encounter
- Nobles and their heirs  
- Political advisors  
- Retired adventurers turned patrons  
- Private tutors and arcane scholars  
- Skilled servants who know far more than they reveal  

## Factions Present
- Noble Houses of Neverwinter  
- Merchant Consortiums  
- Private Arcane Circles  
- Discreet agents of the Lord Protector  
- Independent information brokers  

## Rumours
- “The lake has no bottom.”  
- “Some of the estates predate the current city entirely.”
