# General

![map-1.5-silverquill-campus.jpg|500](/img/user/z_Assets/Maps/Campus%20Maps/map-1.5-silverquill-campus.jpg)

**Location:** [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwood Academy\|Neverwood Academy]]

**Category:** College Campus 

### Description
The Silverquill campus is located due north of the central Neverwood campus and looks much like an extension of it. Silverquill is the most urban of the five colleges, with a campus that features buildings of stately elegance.
