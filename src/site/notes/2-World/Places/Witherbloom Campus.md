# Description
The Witherbloom Campus is inseparable from [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Sedgemoor\|Sedgemoor]], existing not as a separate entity but as a network of structures woven into the swamp itself. Buildings rise from the water on stilts or grow directly from living wood, connected by winding boardwalks and rope bridges that shift with the terrain.
Life and decay coexist here in a delicate balance. Rotting vegetation feeds new growth, and the boundary between vitality and decomposition is treated as a source of power rather than something to be avoided. [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Widdershins Hall\|Widdershins Hall]] anchors the campus, while more remote structures like [[2-World/Points of Interest/Neverwood Academy/Wiltroot Hall\|Wiltroot Hall]] extend its reach into the deeper wetlands.
Students learn through direct interaction with the environment, gathering ingredients, observing ecological cycles, and experimenting with life-altering magic. The swamp is both teacher and test, offering resources to those who respect it and danger to those who do not.
The atmosphere is heavy, humid, and deeply alive. Sounds carry strangely through the mist, and the line between natural and magical phenomena is often indistinguishable. For Witherbloom students, understanding the swamp means understanding the fundamental cycles of life itself.
