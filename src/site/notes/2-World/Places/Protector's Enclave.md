# General

**Location:** [[2-World/Hubs/Neverwinter.md|Neverwinter]]
**Category:** Unknown

## Overview
Protector’s Enclave serves as the political, cultural, and symbolic heart of Neverwinter. Rebuilt following the devastation of Mount Hotenow’s eruption, the district stands as a testament to resilience, order, and carefully curated civic pride.

Broad avenues converge on a grand central square dominated by statuary and public memorials. Government offices, temples, guild representatives, and high-end establishments cluster here, making the Enclave both a centre of authority and a stage upon which the city presents its most polished face.

For visitors arriving for the first time, Protector’s Enclave is Neverwinter as it wishes to be seen.

## District Character
**Architecture:**  
White stone facades, colonnades, wide plazas, and heroic statuary. Buildings are symmetrical and intentionally imposing, blending classical grandeur with post-eruption reconstruction.
**Atmosphere:**  
Orderly, proud, and slightly performative. Guards are visible but not oppressive. Street performers, preachers, and merchants operate under watchful civic eyes.
**Common Sounds:**  
- The ring of hammer on stone from ongoing reconstruction  
- Public announcements  
- Market chatter  
- Temple bells  
**Common Smells:**  
- Fresh bread from nearby bakeries  
- Incense from temples  
- Sea breeze carried up from the docks
