*Neverwood Academy deals in dangerous knowledge, unsettling truths, and situations that may challenge its students.*

This game assumes a mature table, mutual trust, and open communication.

---
## 🧠 Shared Responsibility
There are **no hard lines or predefined veils** in this campaign.
Content may include:
- Horror
- Moral ambiguity
- Loss, fear, and consequences
- Themes that are uncomfortable or unsettling

That said, **player wellbeing always comes first**.

It is each player’s responsibility to:
- Monitor their own comfort
- Step away from the table if needed
- Voice concerns either to the group or privately to the DM

No justification is required.

---
## 🗣️ Speaking Up
If something feels off, overwhelming, or no longer fun:
- You may pause the scene
- You may step away temporarily
- You may message the DM privately during or after the session

Concerns will be taken seriously and addressed respectfully.

---
## 🔄 Adjustments & Aftercare
- Intense scenes happen.
- Emotional bleed is normal.
- Check-ins may occur after particularly heavy moments.

If something lingers, you are encouraged to raise it — even days later.

---
## 🕯️ In-World Framing
Neverwood Academy acknowledges that:
- Curiosity can be dangerous
- Knowledge can wound
- Not all lessons are gentle

However, **player distress is not part of the curriculum**.

---
## 📌 Final Note
You are trusted to know your own limits.  
You are supported if you reach them.

*Crying is a free action. So is stepping away.*
