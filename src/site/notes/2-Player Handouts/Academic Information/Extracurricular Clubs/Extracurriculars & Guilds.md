> [!info] Free Time Slots
> **Extracurricular Clubs** cost **1 Free Time Slot** and grant a **Student Die (1d4)** in one of 2 associated skills.
> **Student Guilds** cost **2 Free Time Slots** (both slots), grant a **Student Die (1d4)** in one of 3 skills, access to guild jobs, and opportunities to earn **Group Cup Points**.
> 
> *For more information see [[3-Homebrew Mechanics/Academics/Free Time System\|Free Time System]]*
